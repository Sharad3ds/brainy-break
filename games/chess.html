📘 How the pieces move
  • ♟ Pawn – moves forward 1 (2 on its first move) and captures diagonally.
  • ♞ Knight – jumps in an “L” shape and can hop over pieces.
  • ♝ Bishop – slides any distance diagonally.
  • ♜ Rook – slides any distance in straight lines.
  • ♛ Queen – slides like a rook and a bishop together.
  • ♚ King – moves 1 square any way. Keep it safe!
  • 🏁 Checkmate – trap the enemy king so it cannot escape and you win.
