😂 What does a hyena's giggle say?

A hyena's giggle carries messages about who it is, how old it is, and how bossy it is.

The big idea

A hyena's giggle tells who it is, how old it is and how bossy it is. A study recorded 17 hyenas to find out.

How it works

🪪

An ID call

Each hyena has its own giggle notes.

📉

Age clue

The pitch of the notes drops as the hyena gets older.

👑

Rank clue

Dominant hyenas vary less from note to note than lower-ranking ones.

Quick stats

More about spotted hyenas

  • The hyenas were in a captive colony at UC Berkeley.
  • The colony keeps the matrilineal system of wild hyenas, where females are dominant.
  • Giggles were recorded while hyenas competed for food.

Words to know

Dominant
The boss of the group.
Matrilineal
A family system led through the mothers.

🧪 Try it yourself

Make a steady ha ha ha ha, then a wobbly ha he hi ha. Which sounds more like a boss? That is the hyena trick.
