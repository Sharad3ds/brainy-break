🦎 How fast can a chameleon shoot its tongue?

A tiny chameleon's tongue can go from 0 to 60 mph in about a hundredth of a second.

The big idea

A chameleon's tongue is about twice its body length and can go 0 to 60 mph in about 1/100 of a second.

How it works

🪗

Elastic spring

Folded elastic tissue releases by elastic recoil.

🚀

Huge power

The highest acceleration and power of all amniotes, the study said.

🎯

Hits its target

Once locked on, chameleons rarely miss.

More about chameleons

  • The smallest species tested was an endangered chameleon from Tanzania.
  • The study was by Christopher Anderson of Brown University.
  • Amniotes include reptiles, birds and mammals.

Words to know

Elastic recoil
Springing back after being stretched.
Acceleration
How quickly something speeds up.

🧪 Try it yourself

Stretch a rubber band and let it fly across the room safely. A chameleon tongue works like that, just much faster!
