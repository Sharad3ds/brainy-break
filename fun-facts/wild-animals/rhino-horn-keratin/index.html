🦏 What is a rhino horn made of?

A rhino horn is made of keratin, the same protein as your hair and fingernails.

The big idea

A rhino horn is made mostly of keratin, the protein in your hair and fingernails. It grows about 2 cm a year.

How it works

💅

Keratin

The same protein found in human hair and fingernails.

🧱

Solid

It is not hollow fibers. It is solid, like a horse hoof or turtle beak.

📈

Slow growth

About 2 cm each year, built in layers from skin cells.

More about rhinoceross

  • The greater one-horned rhino and the Javan rhino have one horn.
  • Asian rhino horns are smaller than African ones.
  • Three of the five rhino species have two horns.

Words to know

Keratin
A tough protein found in hair, nails and horns.

Hunting animals against the law.

🧪 Try it yourself

Feel your fingernail. It is made of the same stuff as a rhino horn! Notice how it grows a little every week.
