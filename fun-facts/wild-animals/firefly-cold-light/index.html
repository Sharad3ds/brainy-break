✨ How do fireflies make light without heat?

Nearly all the energy in a firefly's glow comes out as light, the most efficient light in the world.

The big idea

A firefly's glow turns nearly 100% of its energy into light, the most efficient light in the world.

How it works

🧪

Bioluminescence

Oxygen, calcium, ATP and luciferin react with luciferase.

💡

Efficient light

Nearly all the reaction energy is emitted as light.

💛

Flash codes

Each species has its own flash pattern to attract mates.

Quick stats

2,000+species
~100%energy turned to light
4key ingredients: oxygen, calcium, ATP, luciferin
1enzyme: luciferase

More about fireflys

  • Fireflies are beetles.
  • Some larvae live underground or underwater and still glow.
  • Larvae glow warn predators that they taste bad.

Words to know

Bioluminescence
Light made by a living thing.
Enzyme
A protein that speeds up a chemical reaction.

🧪 Try it yourself

Turn off the lights and look at a glow-in-the-dark toy. Fireflies make their own glow with chemistry!
