🐜 Why do pangolins have scales?

Pangolins are covered in keratin scales, swallow stones to digest food, and eat up to 20,000 ants a day.

The big idea

A pangolin's keratin scales are about 20% of its weight. It has no teeth and can eat 20,000 ants a day.

How it works

🛡️

Scale armor

Keratin scales, like fingernails, make up about 20% of body weight.

🪨

Stone teeth

No teeth, so they swallow stones to grind food.

👅

Super tongue

Some tongues are over 40 cm long, longer than head and body combined.

Quick stats

20%body weight in scales
40 cm+tongue length
8species: 4 Asian, 4 African
20,000ants a day (one pangolin)

More about pangolins

  • Pangolins are hunted for meat, traditional medicine and fashion accessories.
  • In 2017, Cameroon burned 8 tonnes of confiscated scales, from about 15,000 animals.
  • Pangolins are called the most heavily trafficked animal in the world.

Words to know

Keratin
A tough protein found in nails, hair and scales.
Trafficking
Illegal buying and selling.

🧪 Try it yourself

Look at your fingernail. It is keratin, like a pangolin scale! Imagine your body covered in them.
