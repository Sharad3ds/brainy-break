🦁 How far can a lion roar be heard?

A lion's roar can be heard 5 miles away, and the lionesses do most of the hunting.

The big idea

A lion's roar reaches 114 decibels and can be heard about 5 miles away. Lions live in prides, and the lionesses are the main hunters.

How it works

📢

A huge roar

About 114 decibels, heard around 8 km (5 miles) away.

👪

A family group

A pride has related females, their cubs and a few adult males.

🏹

Teamwork hunting

Lionesses ambush prey and fan out in a half circle.

Quick stats

More about lions

  • Males protect the pride from other lions and hyenas.
  • Males in a pride are usually brothers or close relatives.
  • A roar tells other lions where the pride is.

Words to know

Pride
A family group of lions.
Decibel
A unit for measuring how loud a sound is.

🧪 Try it yourself

Take a big breath and roar as quietly as you can, then as loudly as you can (outside!). Now imagine one that carries 5 miles.
