The big idea

The monarch super generation flies 3,000+ miles to Mexico and lives up to 8 months, about 8 times longer than its parents.

How it works

🦋

Super generation

The last generation of the year migrates south.

📍

Long trip

More than 3,000 miles, about 50 miles a day.

⏳

Long life

Up to eight months, about eight times longer than parents.
