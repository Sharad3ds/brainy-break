🐜 Why are ants so strong for their size?

Small animals have more strength for their size, and an ant's mandible can strike with up to 400 times its body weight.

The big idea

Strength per body weight falls as animals get bigger, so ants are mighty. A mandible strike can reach 400 times body weight.

How it works

📏

Scaling

Strength per unit of mass decreases as body size increases.

💪

Muscle build

Muscle fiber density and attachment angles help leverage.

⚡

Fast and slow fibers

Mandible muscles mix quick and steady fibers.

More about ants

  • Each mandible weighs about 0.07 milligrams.
  • One video reported over 1.5 million m/s squared acceleration in a mandible snap.
  • An ant is about 29 percent the length of a human fingertip.
