🦉 How can an owl turn its head almost all the way around?

Owls can turn their heads up to 270 degrees, and special blood vessels keep the brain safe.

The big idea

Owls can turn their heads up to 270° either way. Wide bone holes, air pockets and spare blood-vessel links keep blood flowing to the brain.

How it works

🦴

Wide bone holes

Holes in the neck bones are about ten times wider than the artery.

🩸

Blood pools

Arteries at the base of the head expand and store blood while turning.

🔗

Backup links

Small vessel links keep blood flowing to the brain in extreme twists.

More about owls

  • The study used CT scans and angiography on owls that died of natural causes.
  • The vertebral artery enters the neck at the 12th bone, not the 14th, which gives extra slack.
  • The team planned to study hawks next to see if other birds share these features.

Words to know

Artery
A tube that carries blood from the heart.
Vertebra
One of the bones in the neck and back.

🧪 Try it yourself

Turn your head slowly to the left as far as you can, then to the right. An owl turns about twice as far as you!
