🪹 Why does an orangutan build a new bed every night?

Orangutans build a fresh nest in the treetops every night, and their name means man of the forest.

The big idea

An orangutan builds a new nest every night. Big males have an arm span of over 6 feet, and orangutan means man of the forest.

How it works

🪹

Treetop beds

A new nest of branches and twigs each night keeps them out of reach of predators.

💪

Long arms

Arm span of over six feet in big males.

👩‍👧

Long childhood

Babies nurse for up to eight years.

Quick stats

1new nest each night
6 ft+arm span, big males
40–50years in the wild
~8years between babies

More about orangutans

  • Orangutans use tools to find food in the wild.
  • In captivity they can live about 60 years.
  • One captive orangutan picked a lock with a piece of wire.

Words to know

Canopy
The top layer of branches in a forest.
Arm span
The distance from fingertip to fingertip with arms wide.

🧪 Try it yourself

Stretch out your arms and measure your arm span. A big male orangutan is over six feet! Who has the longer arms, you or an adult?
