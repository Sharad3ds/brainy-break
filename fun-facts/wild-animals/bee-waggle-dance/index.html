🐝 How do bees tell each other where the flowers are?

Honey bees dance a figure-eight waggle dance that shows direction and distance.

The big idea

A bee's waggle dance shows direction by its angle to the sun and distance by its speed. Von Frisch won a Nobel Prize in 1973.

How it works

🧭

Direction

The waggle angle matches the sun-to-food angle.

📏

Distance

Closer food means a faster dance.

🏅

Nobel Prize

Karl von Frisch shared it in 1973.

More about honey bees

  • Von Frisch's first theory said the round dance meant nectar and the waggle dance meant pollen, which proved wrong.
  • He shared the prize with Niko Tinbergen and Konrad Lorenz.
  • He also studied fish, especially in winter.

Words to know

🧪 Try it yourself

Walk in a figure-eight around two chairs and point to a snack. Now you can give directions like a bee!
