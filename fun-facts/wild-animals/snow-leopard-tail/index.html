🏔 Why does a snow leopard have such a long tail?

A snow leopard's heavy tail and snowshoe paws help it move in the mountains.

The big idea

A snow leopard's tail is up to 36 inches long and helps it balance. Its big paws act like snowshoes, and it can jump as far as 50 feet.

How it works

⚖

Balance tail

The long, heavy tail helps the cat steady itself during a chase.

👣

Snowshoe paws

Big paws spread the weight so the cat does not sink.

🧥

Warm coat

Soft, dense fur keeps it warm and its spots hide it.

More about snow leopards

  • Snow leopards live in rugged, cold mountains like the peaks of Mongolia.
  • The soft, dense fur keeps the cat warm in bitter cold.
  • Spotted beige fur blends in with rocks.

Words to know

Balance
Staying steady without falling over.
Camouflage
Colors or patterns that help an animal hide.

🧪 Try it yourself

Stand on one leg with your arms out. Now try with your arms by your sides. A long tail helps a snow leopard balance in the same way.
