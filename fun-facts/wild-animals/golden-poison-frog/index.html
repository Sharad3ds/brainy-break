🐸 Why is the golden poison frog so dangerous?

A golden poison frog is under 2.2 inches long, yet its skin poison could kill about 10 people.

The big idea

One golden poison frog's skin could kill about 10 people, yet its poison comes from its diet. Its bright color is a warning.

How it works

☠

Super poison

10 to 20 times more deadly than other poison frogs.

🪲

From food

Scientists think it comes from a kind of beetle.

🚨

Warning colors

Bright skin warns predators, called aposematism.

Quick stats

More about poison frogs

  • Captive frogs fed toxin-free crickets and flies are harmless.
  • They live in tropical forests of Central and South America.
  • The frogs live in lowland forests of the Amazon.

Words to know

Aposematism
Warning colors that tell predators to stay away.
Toxin
A poison made by a living thing.

🧪 Try it yourself

Look for a bright warning color on a sign or a toy. Bright colors can mean stay away, in nature and in cities!
