The big idea

The record bald eagle nest was 20 feet deep and weighed over two tons. Eagle pairs rose from 471 in 1963 to about 71,400.

How it works

🪹

Growing nests

Eagles keep adding to the same nest.

👁️

Great eyes

Eagles see sharper than people and can see ultraviolet light.

🌱

A comeback

After DDT was banned, captive breeding and reintroduction helped.

More about bald eagles

  • DDT thinned eggshells so they broke.
  • Congress passed a protective act in 1940.
  • Eagles sometimes lock talons and spin while falling toward the ground.

Words to know

Ultraviolet
Light that people cannot see.
Nesting pair
A male and female that raise chicks together.

🧪 Try it yourself

Measure 5 feet on the floor with a tape. That is about how wide an average eagle nest is. Could you fit inside?
