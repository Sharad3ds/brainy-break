🦚 What do peahens really look at?

A peacock's fan is covered in eyespots, but scientists disagree on whether peahens count them.

The big idea

Peahens may not count eyespots exactly. Birds with the fewest eyespots rarely mated, which hints at a threshold.

How it works

👁️

Eyespots

Eye-shaped spots tip the train feathers.

🔬

Mixed results

Some studies found more spots helped, others did not.

📉

A threshold?

The fewest-spot males rarely mated at all.

Quick stats

More about peacocks

  • The English studies were done by Petrie.
  • Dakin and Montgomerie also studied two Canadian and one US population.
  • The authors say it is unclear whether individual peahens have a threshold.

Words to know

Eyespot
A round pattern that looks like an eye.
Threshold
A minimum level that must be reached.
