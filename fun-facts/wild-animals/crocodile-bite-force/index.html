The big idea

A 17-foot saltwater crocodile bit with 3,700 pounds of force. The study tested 83 animals over 11 years.

How it works

🔬

Big study

83 adult alligators and crocodiles over 11 years.

🦷

Tooth pressure

About 350,000 psi.

🦖

Ancient giants

Extinct 35 to 40 foot crocodilians may have bitten with up to 23,100 pounds.

Quick stats

3,700 lbrecord bite force
17 ftthe croc's length
2,980 lbearlier alligator record
11 yrsstudy length

More about saltwater crocodiles

  • The lead researcher was Gregory Erickson of Florida State University.
  • The study was published in PLoS ONE in 2012.
  • It covered every crocodilian species.

Words to know

Crocodilian
An animal in the crocodile family, including alligators.
psi
Pounds per square inch, a way to measure pressure.

🧪 Try it yourself

Squeeze a tennis ball as hard as you can. Your grip is nothing next to a 3,700 pound croc bite!
