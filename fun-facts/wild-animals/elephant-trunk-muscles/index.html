🐘 How many muscles does an elephant trunk have?

The big idea

An elephant's trunk has no bones but almost 40,000 muscles, compared with about 600 in a whole human body.

How it works

💪

Muscle power

Almost 40,000 muscles and no bones.

🛠

A Swiss army nose

Used for breathing, drinking, feeding, grabbing, smelling and trumpeting.

👃

Super smell

More smell receptors than any other mammal, including dogs.

Quick stats

~40,000muscles in a trunk
~600muscles in a human body
0bones in a trunk
1–2finger-like tips

More about elephants

  • Scientists think the trunk began as a snorkel in a semi-aquatic ancestor.
  • African elephants have two finger-like tips and Asian elephants have one.
  • The trunk is a nose joined to the upper lip.

Words to know

Trunk
An elephant's long nose.
Receptor
A tiny part that senses something like a smell.

🧪 Try it yourself

Try to pick up a coin from the table with just your nose and lip. Impossible? An elephant can do it with its trunk!
