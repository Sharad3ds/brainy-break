🌳 Why do leopards drag their food up trees?

Leopards haul big meals into trees to keep them safe from thieves.

The big idea

Leopards hoist kills into trees to protect them. They lift prey of 40% to 140% of their own body weight, and still lose about 20% of kills to thieves.

How it works

🌳

Safe from thieves

A hoisted kill lasts longer than one left on the ground.

💪

Heavy lifting

Kills of 40% to 140% of the leopard's body mass were hoisted.

🐕

Hyenas

Hyenas caused about half of the stolen kills in the study.

Quick stats

40–140%of their own weight
50%+kills hoisted
~20%kills stolen
~50%thefts by hyenas

More about leopards

  • The study was in South Africa's Sabi Sands Game Reserve.
  • Male leopards hoist more often than females.
  • Hoisted kills are visible, so other leopards can find them too.

Words to know

Hoist
To lift something up.
Kleptoparasitism
When one animal steals another animal's food.

🧪 Try it yourself

Try lifting a bag that is half your weight. (Be careful!) A leopard sometimes lifts more than its own weight up a tree.
