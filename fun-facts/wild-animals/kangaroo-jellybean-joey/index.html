A newborn kangaroo is about the size of a jellybean and crawls into the pouch alone.

The big idea

A newborn kangaroo is about the size of a jellybean and weighs under a gram. It crawls into the pouch and stays about 5 months.

How it works

🫘

Tiny newborn

About the size of a jellybean, under 1 gram.

🧗

Self-climbing

The joey crawls up into the pouch with its front limbs.

🌡

Warm pouch

The pouch is about 105 °F (40.5 °C), like the mother's body.

Quick stats

Under 1 gweight at birth
~5 moin the pouch

4teats in the pouch

More about kangaroos

  • The joey weans between 10 and 12 months.
  • The pouch skin is soft, like the inside of a human wrist.
  • The mother cleans the pouch with her tongue.

Words to know

Joey
A baby kangaroo.
Wean
To stop drinking mother's milk.

🧪 Try it yourself

Find a jellybean and put it in your hand. That is the size of a newborn kangaroo! How would it climb to a pouch?
