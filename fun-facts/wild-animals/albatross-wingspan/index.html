The big idea

Albatross wings reach 11 feet. They glide by dynamic soaring and a shoulder lock, with very little flapping.

How it works

📏

Giant wings

Up to 11 feet across, the biggest of any living bird.

💨

Wind shear

S-shaped turns pull energy from the wind near the waves.

🔒

Shoulder lock

Wings stay outstretched without effort.

More about albatrosss

  • The study tracked birds breeding on Bird Island, off South Georgia.
  • They lack the muscle for sustained flapping.
  • In low winds they seem to gain extra energy from updrafts over waves.

Words to know

Wingspan
The distance from wingtip to wingtip.
Wind shear
A change in wind speed with height.

🧪 Try it yourself

Stretch your arms wide and measure the span. An albatross is up to 11 feet, much more than a grown-up!
