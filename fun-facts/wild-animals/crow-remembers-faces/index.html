The big idea

Crows remember faces, make hook tools from twigs, and can match the puzzle skills of a five- to seven-year-old.

How it works

🎭

Face memory

Banded crows later scolded researchers wearing the same mask.

🪝

Tool makers

New Caledonian crows craft hook tools from twigs.

🧩

Puzzle solvers

They dropped stones to raise water, like in Aesop's fable.

More about crows

  • Crows hide food and move it if a squirrel sees them.
  • A Cornell researcher learned his crows ignored his new car until one learned his face.
  • Some scientists think social life helped big brains evolve.

Words to know

🧪 Try it yourself

Look at a friend's face for 5 seconds, then close your eyes and describe it. Crows can remember faces for a long time!
