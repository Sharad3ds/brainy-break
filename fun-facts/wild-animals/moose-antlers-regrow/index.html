🫎 Do moose really regrow their antlers every year?

A moose in its prime can add a pound of antler every day while they grow back.

The big idea

Moose shed their antlers every year and regrow them. A moose in its prime can add a pound of antler a day, and six-foot antlers weigh about 40 pounds each.

How it works

🔄

Yearly cycle

Antlers are shed every year and regrown a few months later.

🧸

Velvet

Fuzzy skin carries blood to the growing antlers, then dries and is rubbed off.

🦴

Real bone

Antlers start as cartilage and harden into bone.

More about mooses

  • Moose are the largest deer species and have the largest antlers.
  • Horns are permanent and covered in keratin, antlers are shed.
  • Caribou are the only species where both males and females grow antlers.

Words to know

Velvet
The furry skin that covers growing antlers.
Antler
Bone that grows on a deer's head and is shed each year.

🧪 Try it yourself

Measure six feet on the floor with a tape measure. That is how wide a big moose's antlers can be!
