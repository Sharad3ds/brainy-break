🐯 Are a tiger's stripes on its skin too?

Tiger stripes are like fingerprints, and they go all the way down to the skin.

The big idea

No two tigers have the same stripes, like fingerprints. The skin is striped as well as the fur, and the stripes help the tiger hide.

How it works

☝

Like fingerprints

No two tigers share the same pattern.

🧬

Skin deep

Tigers have striped skin, not just striped fur.

🌙

Camouflage

Stripes hide the body shape. In moonlit grass they look like shadows.

More about tigers

  • Scientists can tell tigers apart by their stripe patterns.
  • The stripes make the tiger hard to see in long grass.
  • A tiger shaved of its fur would still show its stripes.

Words to know

Camouflage
Colors or patterns that help an animal hide.
Pattern
A design that repeats or looks a certain way.

🧪 Try it yourself

Press your fingertip on an ink pad and make a print. Make another with a friend. Compare them. Tiger stripes are different for each tiger too!
