🌴 Why does a toucan have such a big bill?

A toucan's bill is about a third of its body length and works like a radiator.

The big idea

A toucan's bill is about one-third of its body, and it works as a radiator, shedding up to four times the heat it makes at rest.

How it works

📏

Largest for its size

The toco toucan has the biggest bill for its body of any bird.

🌡

No sweat

Toucans cool off through the bill.

🩸

Blood network

Blood vessels adjust how much heat leaves.

More about toucans

  • The researcher was Denis Andrade of São Paulo State University.
  • The bill has little feather cover.
  • The bird studied was the toco toucan.

Words to know
