🦩 Why are flamingos pink?

Flamingos are pink because of the food they eat, and chicks are born gray.

The big idea

Flamingos get their pink color from food: algae and brine shrimp full of carotenoids. Chicks hatch gray.

How it works

🦐

Pink food

Carotenoids in algae and brine shrimp color the feathers.

🍼

Crop milk

Both parents make a nutrient-rich fluid for chicks.

🫗

Upside-down feeding

Comb-like plates in the bill trap food.

More about flamingos

  • Chicks hatch with straight bills; they become hook-shaped as they grow.
  • Flamingos are filter feeders.
  • Tucking one leg under the belly can reduce heat loss.

Words to know

Carotenoid
A red, yellow or orange pigment in plants.
Filter feeder
An animal that strains food from water.
