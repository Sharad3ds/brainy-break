🦍 Why do gorillas beat their chests?

A gorilla's chest beat may tell others how big it is.

The big idea

Gorillas beat their chests to warn rivals, and the biggest gorillas make the lowest sounds. They share 98.3% of their DNA with humans.

How it works

🥁

A signal

The beats may warn off competitors and show body size.

👑

The silverback

The adult male leads the troop, and is about double a female in size.

🧬

Close relatives

Gorillas share 98.3% of their DNA with humans.

More about gorillas

  • Both gorilla species build a new nest to sleep in each night.
  • Gorillas rarely need to drink because plants have lots of water.
  • Mountain gorillas eat almost entirely young bamboo shoots in bamboo season.

Words to know

Silverback
The adult male who leads a gorilla troop.
Troop
A family group of gorillas.

🧪 Try it yourself

Pat your chest softly and listen. Now try a lower sound by humming deep. Big gorillas make the deepest thumps!
