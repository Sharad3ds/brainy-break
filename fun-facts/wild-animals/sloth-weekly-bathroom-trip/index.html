🦥 Why does a sloth only go to the bathroom once a week?

A sloth digests so slowly that one leaf can take weeks, and its fur grows its own garden.

The big idea

A sloth's energy use is only about 40–45% of normal for its size. One leaf can take 157 to 1,200 hours to pass, and a sloth goes to the bathroom about once a week.

How it works

🔋

Low-power mode

Metabolic rate about 40 to 45 percent of the average for a mammal that size.

🍃

Slow digestion

It can take 157 to 1,200 hours to pass one leaf.

🌿

Fur garden

Algae grows in cracks in the fur and supports insects.

More about sloths

  • Hundreds of insects can live on one sloth.
  • There are two families: two-toed and three-toed sloths.
  • Sloths bask in the sun to warm up and rest in shade to cool down.

Words to know

Metabolism
How fast a body uses energy.
Algae
Simple plant-like living things that grow in damp places.
