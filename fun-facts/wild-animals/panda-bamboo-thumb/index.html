🎋 How does a panda hold bamboo?

Pandas use a special wrist bone like a thumb to hold bamboo.

The big idea

Pandas hold bamboo with enlarged wrist bones that work like thumbs. They eat about 26 to 84 pounds of bamboo a day.

How it works

🖐

Pseudo-thumb

Enlarged wrist bones work like opposable thumbs.

🎋

Lots of bamboo

About 26 to 84 pounds a day, depending on the part eaten.

🍶

Tiny cubs

A newborn is about the size of a stick of butter.

More about giant pandas

  • Pandas live in temperate forests in southwest China.
  • The pseudo-thumb is an enlarged wrist bone, not a real finger.
  • A panda is a bear, and it is not a primate.

Words to know

Pseudo-thumb
A wrist bone that works like a thumb.
Bamboo
A tall, fast-growing grass with hollow stems.

🧪 Try it yourself

Try to pick up a pencil without using your thumb. It is hard! A panda has a special bone to help it grip.
