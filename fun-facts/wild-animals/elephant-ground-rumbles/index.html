🔊 Can elephants hear through their feet?

Elephant rumbles travel through the ground, and elephants may feel them in their feet.

The big idea

Elephant rumbles are about 20 hertz and also travel through the ground. Scientists think elephants feel these vibrations through their feet.

How it works

🔈

Low rumbles

About 20 hertz, a range well suited to long distance communication.

🌍

Ground waves

Models estimate ground signals reach 10 to 20 miles, while air rumbles carry about 6.

🦶

Foot sensing

Scientists suspect bone conduction from the toenails to the ear and receptors in the foot.

More about elephants

  • The study was published in the Journal of the Acoustical Society of America.
  • In one test, a young female bit the ground when she felt a warning call.
  • The Texas experiments used two captive female Asian elephants.

Words to know

Seismic wave
A wave that moves through the ground.
Hertz
A unit that counts how many waves happen each second.

🧪 Try it yourself

Put your hand on a speaker playing a low sound. You can feel it! Elephants may feel sound through their feet the same way.
