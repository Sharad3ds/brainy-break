🐒 How do chimpanzees fish for termites?

In 1960, Jane Goodall saw a chimp use a stick as a tool, and that changed science.

The big idea

In October 1960, Jane Goodall saw a chimp use a stick to fish for termites. It showed that humans are not the only tool users.

How it works

🪵

Stick tools

Chimps push a stick into a termite mound and pull out snacks.

🧠

Big discovery

It changed what scientists thought was special about humans.

👩‍👧

Teaching

Mothers in the Republic of Congo may teach infants by giving them sticks.

Quick stats

More about chimpanzees

  • The chimp was called David Greybeard.
  • Goodall arrived at Gombe on July 14, 1960.
  • Scientists also use chimp tool use to guess how early humans solved problems.

Words to know

Tool
An object used to do a job.
Primatologist
A scientist who studies apes and monkeys.

🧪 Try it yourself

Try to get a small toy out of a narrow jar using a long straw or stick. You are fishing like a chimp!
