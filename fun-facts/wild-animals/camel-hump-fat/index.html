🐪 Is there water in a camel's hump?

A camel's hump holds fat, not water.

The big idea

A camel's hump holds fat, not water. It can feed a camel for up to 4 or 5 months, and a camel can drink up to 30 gallons in one go.

How it works

🧈

Fat storage

The hump is a fat reserve, and it flops over when empty.

🍽

Months of food

A full hump can feed a camel for four or five months.

💧

Big drinks

Up to 30 gallons (114 liters) in one sitting.

More about camels

  • A camel's ability to go long periods without water likely led to the myth.
  • The expert quoted is Rick Schwartz of the San Diego Zoo.
  • An empty hump flops over to the side.

Words to know
