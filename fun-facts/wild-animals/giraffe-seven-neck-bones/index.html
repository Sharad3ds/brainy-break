The big idea

A giraffe has just 7 neck bones, the same as a human, but each is about 10 inches long. A giraffe stands over 17 feet tall.

How it works

7️⃣

Seven bones

The same number as a human neck, but each is about 25 cm long.

🔝

A multi-tool

The neck helps with feeding, fighting called necking, and looking out for predators.

🩸

Blood control

The rete mirabile keeps blood from flooding the brain when the giraffe bends down.

More about giraffes

  • Scientists still debate which job of the neck mattered most.
  • In necking, giraffes swing their necks like clubs.
  • Their height helps them spot predators across open country.

Words to know

Vertebra
One bone in the backbone or neck.
Necking
When giraffes swing their necks to fight over who is boss.

🧪 Try it yourself

Feel the back of your neck. You have seven bones there. Now imagine each one is as long as your hand and wrist!
