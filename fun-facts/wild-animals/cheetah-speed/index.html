🐆 How fast can a cheetah run?

The cheetah can go from 0 to 45 mph in about 2.5 seconds.

The big idea

A cheetah's top speed averages 60 to 70 mph, and it goes from 0 to 45 mph in 2.5 seconds. It can hold top speed for only about 300 yards.

How it works

🚀

Super start

0 to 45 mph (72 km/h) in 2.5 seconds.

🦵

Giant strides

At top speed, one stride covers 23 feet (7 m), four times every second.

😎

Tear marks

Dark lines from eyes to mouth may reduce glare and help hunting.

More about cheetahs

  • Cheetahs have blunt, semi-retractable claws that help grip the ground.
  • A cheetah's tail works like a rudder for quick turns.
  • Cheetahs creep to within about 100 yards before they sprint.

Words to know

Rudder
A part that steers a boat or plane.
Stride
One long step when running.
