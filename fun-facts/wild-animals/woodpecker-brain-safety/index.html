🪵 Why don't woodpeckers get headaches?

A woodpecker pecks about 20 times a second, and its head stops at around 1,200 g.

The big idea

A woodpecker pecks about 20 times a second and its head slows by about 1,200 g. Stiff bones, less fluid and a springy tongue bone help.

How it works

🦴

Tough skull

Stiffer bone with more minerals.

🥚

Less fluid

Little fluid limits brain movement, like a hard-boiled yolk.

🪀

Springy tongue bone

It wraps around the skull and dampens vibration.

More about woodpeckers

  • Strong tail feathers and claws help balance.
  • Researchers are still studying whether woodpeckers show signs of brain injury.
  • Neck muscles and specialized beaks help too.

Words to know

g-force
A measure of how hard something speeds up or slows down.
Concussion
An injury to the brain from a hard knock.

🧪 Try it yourself

Tap your finger on a table about 20 times in one second. Too fast? That is a woodpecker's normal rate!
