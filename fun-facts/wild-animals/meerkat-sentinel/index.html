👀 Why do meerkats stand guard?

Meerkats post sentinels who sing a watchman's song and look out for danger.

The big idea

Meerkat sentinels stand guard on high posts, up to 6 meters in trees, and announce it with a special watchman's song.

How it works

👀

Sentinels

One, sometimes several, meerkats watch while the others forage.

🎵

Watchman's song

A special call announces that a sentinel is on duty.

🦅

Threats

Birds of prey, wild cats, jackals and snakes.

Quick stats

60 cmaverage lookout height
6 mhighest tree posts
2–50meerkats per group
1dominant pair per group

More about meerkats

  • Sentinels guard from safe sites and only when their bellies are full.
  • Supersentinels guard more often and for longer than the others.
  • Posts are higher in the rainy season when grass is tall.

Words to know

Sentinel
A guard who watches for danger.
Mob
A group of meerkats.
