🛡️ Which mammal wears a shell?

Armadillos are the only mammals with a shell of bony plates, and one species rolls into a ball.

The big idea

Armadillos are the only mammals with a shell, made of bony plates. Nine-banded mothers have identical quadruplets.

How it works

🛡️

Bony armor

The shell is made of bony plates.

⚽

Ball trick

Only the three-banded armadillo can roll up completely.

👪

Identical quads

Nine-banded babies share identical DNA and are the same gender.

More about armadillos

  • Armadillos are expert diggers with big claws on their front feet.
  • The six-banded armadillo has an extra layer of keratin on its shell.
  • Seven-banded armadillos also have identical offspring, 4 to 8 at a time.

Words to know

Armor
A hard covering that protects.
Identical
Exactly alike.

🧪 Try it yourself

Curl up in a tight ball on the floor. Imagine a shell on your back! Only one armadillo species can do this fully.
