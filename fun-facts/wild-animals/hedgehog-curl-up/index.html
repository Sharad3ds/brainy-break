🦔 How does a hedgehog protect itself?

A hedgehog curls into a tight spiky ball to protect its soft parts.

The big idea

A hedgehog curls into a tight ball and tucks in its head, tail and legs, leaving only its spines showing.

How it works

🦔

Spiny coat

Spines cover most of the body except the face, legs and belly.

⚽

Ball defense

Curling up protects the soft, spine-free parts.

🐛

Bug eaters

Insects are the top food, and they also eat mice, snails, lizards and more.

Quick stats

5–12 inbody length (13–30 cm)
1–2 intail length
14–39 ozweight
Insectstop food

More about hedgehogs

  • Hedgehogs are classed as carnivores.
  • The face, legs and belly have no spines.
  • Tucking everything in leaves only spines facing out.

Words to know

Spine
A sharp, stiff point on an animal's body.
Carnivore
An animal that eats mainly meat.
