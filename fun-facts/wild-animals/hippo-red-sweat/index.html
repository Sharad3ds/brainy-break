🦛 Why do hippos ooze red liquid?

Hippos ooze a red-orange liquid that acts like sunscreen and may block germs.

The big idea

Hippo skin oozes a liquid that turns red-orange and then brown. Its pigments absorb UV light like sunscreen, and the red one slowed bacteria in lab tests.

How it works

🧴

Color change

It starts colorless, turns red-orange in minutes, then goes brown.

☀

UV shield

The pigments absorb ultraviolet light.

🦠

Germ fighter

The red pigment slowed the growth of two kinds of bacteria in lab tests.

More about hippos

  • The pigments are called hipposudoric acid (red) and norhipposudoric acid (orange).
  • The findings were reported in the journal Nature.
  • Ueno Zoo keepers in Tokyo collected samples by swabbing hippo skin with gauze.

Words to know

Pigment
A substance that gives color.
Ultraviolet (UV)
Invisible light from the sun that can burn skin.
