🐨 Do koalas have fingerprints like people?

Koalas have fingerprints almost like ours, even though we are not close relatives.

The big idea

Koalas have fingerprints that look almost like human ones, even though our last shared ancestor lived over 100 million years ago.

How it works

☝

Loops and whorls

Koala paws have the same kind of patterns as human fingerprints.

🌿

Useful grip

They may help with delicate feeding and gripping narrow branches.

🔁

Grew separately

Convergent evolution: both evolved them on their own.

Quick stats

2species that have them: koalas and humans

More about koalas

  • The scientist is Maciej Henneberg of the University of Adelaide.
  • Koalas pluck and chew eucalyptus leaves.
  • Koalas are marsupials, not bears.

Words to know

Convergent evolution
When different animals evolve a similar feature separately.
Whorl
A swirl-shaped pattern.

🧪 Try it yourself

Press your finger on an ink pad or a pencil scribble and then on paper. Look for loops and whorls in your fingerprint, just like a koala!
