🐍 Why do snakes flick their tongues?

A snake smells in stereo: each tongue tip delivers scent to its own organ.

The big idea

A snake's forked tongue carries scent to two vomeronasal organs, so it can smell in stereo and tell which side is stronger.

How it works

👅

Smell, not taste

The tongue carries odor molecules to the nose-like organs.

⚖

Stereo smell

The brain compares the two tongue tips.

🌀

Air swirls

Flicks make vortices that pull odors in.

More about snakes

  • The organs are also called Jacobson's organs.
  • Early proto-snakes lived underground, where smell mattered more than sight.
  • Kurt Schwenk is at the University of Connecticut.

Words to know

Vomeronasal organ
A smell organ in the roof of the mouth.
Vortex
A swirl of air or water.

🧪 Try it yourself

Sniff the air with your eyes closed and point toward the strongest smell. A snake does this with its forked tongue!
