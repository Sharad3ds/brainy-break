🦇 How do bats see in the dark with sound?

Bats shout ultrasonic calls and listen to the echoes to find their food.

The big idea

Bats make ultrasonic calls and listen to the echoes to find food. Small bats can catch over 1,000 insects an hour.

How it works

📣

Call and echo

The echo reveals size, distance, texture, speed and direction.

🔊

Ultrasound

Most calls are 20–200 kilohertz, above the 20 kHz people hear up to.

🍽

Big eaters

Bats can eat over half their body weight in insects in one night.

Quick stats

20–200 kHzmost bat calls
50–120 dBcall loudness
1,400+bat species
~1,000use echolocation

More about bats

  • Some bats make calls with the larynx, tongue clicks or through the nostrils.
  • The greater wax moth can sense frequencies up to 300 kHz.
  • Lower frequencies travel farther, and higher ones give more detail.

Words to know

Echolocation
Finding things using echoes of your own sounds.
Ultrasonic
Too high-pitched for people to hear.

🧪 Try it yourself

Clap in an empty room and listen to the echo. A bat does this with super-high sounds and reads the echo to find a snack!
