🪶 Why is an ostrich's eye bigger than its brain?

The ostrich has the largest eyes of any land animal, and each is bigger than its brain.

The big idea

Ostriches have the largest eyes of any land animal, can run up to 43 mph, and lay eggs as big as 24 chicken eggs.

How it works

👁️

Giant eyes

Each eye is larger than the brain.

🏃

Fast runner

Up to 43 miles per hour.

🥚

Huge eggs

One egg equals 24 chicken eggs.

More about ostrichs

  • Ostriches cannot fly.
  • They are the biggest living birds.
  • Their huge eyes help them see far across open land.
