🦆 How does a platypus find food with its eyes closed?

A platypus hunts with its eyes, ears and nose shut, using electric sensors in its bill.

The big idea

A platypus hunts with eyes, ears and nose closed, sensing prey with electroreceptors in its bill. It is a mammal that lays eggs.

How it works

⚡

Electric sense

The bill has tens of thousands of receptors, including electroreceptors.

🥚

Egg layer

Females lay one to three eggs about the size of small grapes.

☠️

Spur

Males have a hollow keratin spur linked to a venom gland.

More about platypuss

  • Webbed forefeet power swimming, and the webbing folds back for digging.
  • The IUCN lists the platypus as Near Threatened.
  • Males are 16 to 25 inches long, females 15 to 22.

Words to know

Electroreception
Sensing weak electric signals from other animals.
Spur
A sharp pointed part on an animal's leg.

🧪 Try it yourself

Close your eyes and feel for a toy hidden in a box of dry rice. A platypus uses its sensitive bill the same way underwater!
