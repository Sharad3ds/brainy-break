🐺 Why do wolves howl?

Wolves howl to stay in touch across big areas, and the sound can carry about 11 km.

The big idea

A wolf's howl can be heard from about 11 km away on open tundra. Wolves howl to stay in touch, mark territory, find mates and bond.

How it works

📡

Built to travel

A howl is a low-pitched, long call that carries over large distances.

🗺

Big homes

Home ranges are usually 200 to 600 square kilometers.

🤝

Staying together

Howling keeps the pack in contact and strengthens bonds.

More about wolfs

  • Wolves also bark.
  • Howling together strengthens the bonds between pack members.
  • Lone wolves can use howls to find a mate.

Words to know

Pack
A family group of wolves.
Territory
The area an animal or group defends as its own.
