The big idea

A hummingbird's heart can beat up to 1,200 times a minute, and its wings beat about 53 times a second.

How it works

❤

Racing heart

Up to 1,200 beats per minute.

🪽

Fast wings

About 53 beats a second in a ruby-throated hummingbird.

😴

Torpor

A hibernation-like state each night saves energy.

More about hummingbirds

  • The bee hummingbird of Cuba is the world's smallest bird.
  • Before long migrations, they nearly double their weight in fat.
  • Their flight is more like an insect's than other birds'.

Words to know

Torpor
A deep, sleep-like state that saves energy.
Nectar
The sweet liquid inside flowers.

Flap your arms as fast as you can for ten seconds. A hummingbird does this 53 times every second!
