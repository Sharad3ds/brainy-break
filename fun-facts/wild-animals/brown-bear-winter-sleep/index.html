💤 How long does a brown bear sleep in winter?

Brown bears sleep in a den for about five to six months and cool down a few degrees.

The big idea

Brown bears spend about 151 to 175 days in a den. Their heart rate drops and their body temperature falls from about 37 °C to about 34 °C.

How it works

🍂

Den in autumn

Bears in the study entered dens mostly in October and November.

📉

Slower body

Heart rate drops, and body temperature settles around 33.8 °C.

🌸

Out in spring

They left between 21 March and 6 May, with a middle date of 6 April.

More about brown bears

  • The study followed brown bears over two winters.
  • Heart rate started dropping about 24 days before den entry.
  • Body temperature near den exit was about 36.7 °C.

Words to know

Hibernation
A long winter sleep that saves energy.
Den
An animal's sheltered home.
