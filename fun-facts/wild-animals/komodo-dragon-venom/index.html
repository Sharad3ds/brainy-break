The big idea

Komodo dragons have six venom glands on each side of the lower jaw, holding about 1.2 mL of venom.

How it works

🦎

Giant lizard

About 10 feet long and over 300 pounds.

🧪

Venom glands

MRI scans showed six on each side of the lower jaw.

🩸

Shock

The venom can send prey into shock.

More about Komodo dragons

  • They eat almost any meat, including carrion.
  • The old bacteria idea was dismissed by Bryan Fry.
  • In a lab, injected venom made rats become still.

Words to know

Venom
Poison that is injected, usually by a bite.
Carrion
The meat of an animal that is already dead.
