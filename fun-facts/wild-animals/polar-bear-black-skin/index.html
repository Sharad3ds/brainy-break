🐻‍❄️ What color is a polar bear really?

A polar bear's fur is clear and its skin is black.

The big idea

A polar bear's skin is black and its hairs are clear and hollow. They scatter light, so the bear looks white. A 4.5 inch fat layer keeps it warm.

How it works

⚫

Black skin

Black skin soaks up heat from sunlight.

💡

Clear hairs

Hollow, unpigmented hairs scatter light and look white.

🧈

Warm fat

A fat layer about 4.5 inches thick insulates the bear in water.

More about polar bears

  • Snow and ice look white for the same reason: they scatter light.
  • Bears look yellowish after eating seals because of the oils.
  • The white coat helps them hide from seals.

Words to know

Insulate
To keep heat from escaping.
Molt
To shed fur and grow new fur.

🧪 Try it yourself

Look at a glass of crushed ice. It is clear, but it looks white as a pile. Polar bear hair works in a similar way.
