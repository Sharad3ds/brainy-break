🦓 Why does a zebra have stripes?

Scientists dressed horses in zebra coats and found that stripes confuse biting flies.

The big idea

Stripes may keep biting flies away. In a UK study, fewer flies landed on horses in striped coats than on plain ones.

How it works

🪰

Fly fighters

A 2014 study found stripes repel biting flies.

🐴

Zebra coats

Horses wearing striped coats got fewer landings than plain coats.

🎥

Slow-mo video

Flies did not slow down before hitting a zebra, unlike with horses.

More about zebras

  • Flies still landed on the bare heads of horses in zebra coats, so the stripes were the cause.
  • Some biting flies carry deadly diseases.
  • The lead researcher was Tim Caro.
