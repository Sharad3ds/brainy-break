🤧 Why does a marine iguana sneeze salt?

Marine iguanas are the only lizards that feed in the ocean, and they sneeze out extra salt.

The big idea

Marine iguanas are the only lizards that feed in the ocean. Salt glands by the nostrils remove salt from their blood, and they sneeze it out.

How it works

🤧

Salt sneeze

Glands connected to the nostrils remove salt from the blood.

🌿

Algae meals

They scrape algae from rocks with a flat nose and sharp teeth.

☀

Sun warm-ups

They warm up in the sun before feeding.

More about marine iguanas

  • Males can grow to more than four feet long.
  • Experts estimate the species evolved about 5.7 million years ago.
  • There are seven recorded subspecies.

Words to know

Salt gland
A body part that removes extra salt.
Algae
Simple plant-like living things in water.

🧪 Try it yourself

Make a pretend salty sneeze: put a pinch of salt on the table and blow it away. Marine iguanas get rid of it a similar way!
