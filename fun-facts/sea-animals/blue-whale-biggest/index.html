The big idea

The blue whale is the largest animal known to have ever lived. It can grow up to about 110 feet long, and the biggest ones may eat up to 6 tons of krill in a day.

How it works

📏

Super long

Antarctic blue whales can reach about 110 feet. Blue whales in the North Atlantic and North Pacific reach about 90 feet.

🦐

Tiny food

Blue whales eat krill, which are small shrimp-like animals, in huge amounts.

💙

Protected

Blue whales are endangered and protected by law. Their numbers are slowly increasing.

More about blue whales

  • A blue whale is a mammal. It breathes air and feeds its calf milk.
  • A mother carries her calf for about 10 to 12 months.
  • Calves drink milk until they are about 6 to 7 months old.

Words to know

Krill
Tiny shrimp-like animals that many whales eat.
Endangered
A kind of animal that is rare and needs extra protection.

🧪 Try it yourself

Step outside and take 37 big steps in a line. That is about 110 feet, as long as the biggest blue whale!
