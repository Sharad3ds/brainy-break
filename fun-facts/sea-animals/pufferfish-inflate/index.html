🎈 How does a pufferfish puff up?

Pufferfish gulp water to swell up into a spiky ball.

The big idea

A pufferfish gulps water into an elastic stomach and swells to three or four times its normal size, with its spines sticking out, to look hard to swallow.

How it works

🎈

Buccal pumping

They draw water, or air if out of water, into a very elastic stomach.

🛡

Hard to swallow

The round shape and sticking-out spines put off predators.

😮‍💨

It is tiring

Inflated puffers use up to five times more oxygen, and recovery can take about five hours.

More about pufferfishs

  • Collagen fibres in the stomach skin run in parallel rows, so it can stretch and spring back.
  • Almost all pufferfish carry the toxin TTX, made by bacteria on their food. Puffers are immune because of a genetic mutation.
  • Puffed-up fish swim less well, so they usually deflate once danger passes.

Words to know

Inflate
To fill something with air or water so it gets bigger.
Toxin
A poison made by living things.

🧪 Try it yourself

Blow up a balloon and watch how it stretches and gets round. A pufferfish stomach stretches in a similar way, but with water!
