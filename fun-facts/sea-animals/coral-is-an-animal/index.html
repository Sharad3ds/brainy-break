🪸 Is coral a plant, a rock or an animal?

Coral looks like rock, but each tiny polyp is an animal with algae helpers.

The big idea

Coral polyps are animals closely related to sea anemones. Tiny algae partners give them most of their energy, and one square meter can hold over 10,000 polyps.

How it works

🪸

Animals

Polyps are animals related to sea anemones.

🌿

Partnership

Zooxanthellae live under the polyp skin and make energy for it.

🏙

Colonies

Polyps live on a skeleton of rock they build together.

Quick stats

More about corals

  • A healthy reef can have tens of thousands of colonies per square kilometer.
  • The relationship between polyps and algae is called mutualism.
  • Algae live inside the polyp, under its skin.

Words to know

Zooxanthellae
Microscopic algae that live inside coral.
Mutualism
A partnership where both partners gain.

🧪 Try it yourself

Make a coral colony out of small blobs of clay on a lump of clay rock. Each blob is one polyp, and together they make a reef!
