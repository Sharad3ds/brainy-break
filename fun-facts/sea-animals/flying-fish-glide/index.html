The big idea

Flying fish glide above the water on long fins. The farthest recorded flight was about 400 meters, and gliding likely helps them escape predators.

How it works

🪁

Gliding, not flapping

They launch from the water with strong tail beats and spread their fins to glide.

📏

Long flights

Up to about 8 meters high, and up to 400 meters for the farthest recorded flight.

🏃

Escape trick

Gliding may have evolved to escape fast predators like dolphinfish.

More about flying fishs

  • They are cigar-shaped with long pectoral fins. Some species also have enlarged pelvic fins.
  • The tail is forked with a longer lower lobe.
  • Flying fish range from about 15 to 50 centimeters long.

Words to know

Glide
To move through the air without flapping.
Predator
An animal that hunts other animals.

🧪 Try it yourself

Fold a paper plane and see how far it glides. Now try one with bigger wings. Does it go farther? Flying fish have long fins for gliding.
