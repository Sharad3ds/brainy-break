The big idea

The manta ray is the largest ray in the world. A giant oceanic manta can have a wingspan of up to 29 feet and lives up to 50 years.

How it works

🪽

Giant wings

Giant oceanic mantas reach up to 29 feet. Reef mantas average about 11 feet.

🦐

Gentle feeding

Mantas filter zooplankton and krill from the water with gill plates.

🧠

Big brains

Mantas have the largest brain-to-body-size ratio of any cold-blooded fish.

More about manta rays

  • There are two species: the reef manta and the giant oceanic manta.
  • Females have about one pup every couple of years, and pregnancy lasts around 12 to 13 months.
  • Studies suggest mantas can build mental maps of their surroundings.

Words to know

Zooplankton
Tiny animals that drift in the water.
Wingspan
The distance from one wingtip to the other.

🧪 Try it yourself

Stretch your arms out wide and measure your wingspan with a tape. A giant manta is about 29 feet. How many of you would fit across?
