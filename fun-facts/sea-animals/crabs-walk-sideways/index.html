Most crabs scuttle sideways, and it likely evolved just once.

The big idea

Sideways walking in crabs seems to have evolved just once, about 200 million years ago. Nearly 7,500 species belong to the sideways group.

How it works

1️⃣

Once

All sideways-walking crabs trace back to one ancestor.

🦵

Simpler legs

Fewer nerve cells were needed, and two joints did most of the work.

🏃

Escape?

Scientists suggest it may have helped escape ambushing predators.

Quick stats

200Myears ago it began

More about crabs

  • The study was published in eLife.
  • Three-quarters of species died in the Triassic–Jurassic extinction before sideways crabs spread.
  • At least three other crustaceans evolved crab-like bodies, but none learned to walk sideways.

Words to know

Ancestor
An animal long ago that later animals came from.
Eubrachyura
The big group of crabs that walk sideways.
