The big idea

The whale shark is the largest fish in the ocean. Each one has a unique spot pattern, and scientists use star-identifying software to tell them apart.

How it works

📏

Giant fish

Whale sharks reach about 18 meters, and maybe more.

🔵

Unique spots

Pale spots on grey, blue or brown skin make a pattern that is different for every shark.

🥄

Plankton eaters

They filter plankton through gill rakers, sometimes gathering by the hundreds.

More about whale sharks

  • The page lists a maximum size of 17 to 21 meters.
  • Software designed to spot star clusters has been adapted to identify individual whale sharks.
  • Whale sharks are sharks, not whales!

Words to know

Plankton
Tiny living things that drift in the sea.
Gill rakers
Comb-like parts that filter food from the water.

🧪 Try it yourself

Dab dots with a marker on a sheet of paper to make your own spot pattern. Now ask a friend to make a different one. No two are the same!
