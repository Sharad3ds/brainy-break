🧽 Are sponges among the oldest animals?

Sponges filter huge amounts of water, and may be among the first animals.

The big idea

Sponges are animals that filter water, up to 120,000 gallons per pound per day. A fossil may be 890 million years old, but that claim is debated.

How it works

🧽

Filter feeders

Water flows through a 3-D network of channels, and the sponge takes out bacteria.

📍

Rooted

Most live fixed in one spot, in rivers or on the sea floor.

🏺

Ancient

A study in Nature claimed an 890 million year old fossil. Some experts think it is younger.

More about sponges

  • The fossil was found in rocks in the Northwest Territories of Canada.
  • The claim came from a single study published in Nature.
  • Sponges produce sperm, like other animals.

Words to know

Filter feeder
An animal that eats by straining food from water.
Fossil
A preserved trace of ancient life.

🧪 Try it yourself

Squeeze a clean kitchen sponge in a bowl of water. See how water flows through the holes? A living sponge does the same to eat.
