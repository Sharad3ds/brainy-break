The big idea

An orca, or killer whale, is a member of the dolphin family. Orcas live in pods led by mothers, and each family has its own calls and whistles.

How it works

🐬

A big dolphin

Orcas belong to the family Delphinidae, with bottlenose dolphins and other ocean dolphins.

👪

Family pods

Several mother-led families travel together to make one pod.

🗣

Dialects

Families have their own calls, and mothers pass them to their calves.

More about orcas

  • Orcas are the largest members of the dolphin family.
  • Different orca families eat different things. In the northeastern Pacific, residents eat fish and squid and transients eat marine mammals.
  • Orcas hunt and live in groups called pods.

Words to know

Pod
A group of whales or dolphins that live and travel together.
Dialect
A special way of talking that one group uses.

🧪 Try it yourself

Make up a special family call, like a whistle or a clap pattern, and use it to find each other in a park. That is how orca families stay together!
