The big idea

Emperor penguin dads keep the egg warm on their feet for about 65 to 75 days through the Antarctic winter, while the colony huddles together for warmth.

How it works

🥚

Egg on the feet

Males keep the egg on their feet in a brood pouch until it hatches.

🫂

Huddling

Colonies of 5,000 or more adults and chicks huddle together and rotate who stands on the cold outside edge.

🌊

Great divers

The deepest recorded dive is 564 meters, and the longest lasted nearly 32 minutes.

More about emperor penguins

  • Winds can reach 200 km/h in the Antarctic.
  • An emperor penguin weighs about 40 kg.
  • The longest recorded dive lasted nearly 32 minutes.

Words to know

Brood pouch
A flap of skin that keeps an egg warm.
Colony
A large group of animals living together.
