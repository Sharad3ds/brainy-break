🦭 What are walrus tusks for?

Walrus tusks help them climb out of the water and show who is boss.

The big idea

Walruses use their tusks to haul out onto ice and to show who is in charge. Their whiskers find clams, and thick blubber keeps them warm.

How it works

🧊

Haul-out helpers

Tusks help a walrus pull itself out onto ice and rocky shores.

🧔

Super whiskers

A walrus has about 400 to 700 whiskers that feel for food in the sediment.

🧈

Thick blubber

Blubber can be up to 15 cm (6 inches) thick, which keeps the walrus warm and stores energy.

More about walruss

  • Newborn walrus calves weigh about 45 to 75 kg.
  • Females weigh about 400 to 1,250 kg.
  • Walruses mainly eat clams and other shellfish, and sometimes fish.

Words to know

Tusk
A very long tooth that sticks out of the mouth.
Haul out
When a sea animal climbs out of the water to rest.
