The big idea

Belugas are called the canaries of the sea because they make so many sounds: chirps, clicks, whistles and squeals, using air in nasal sacs instead of vocal cords.

How it works

🎤

Lots of sounds

Clicks, squeaks, chirps, groans, whistles and squeals.

👃

No vocal cords

Belugas make sound with nasal sacs near the blowhole.

😮

A bendy melon

The rounded forehead can change shape, so belugas can make different faces and sounds.

More about beluga whales

  • Belugas use clicks to find food. This is called echolocation.
  • Researchers once recorded a beluga whose sounds were remarkably close to human speech.
  • Belugas are toothed whales that live in cold Arctic waters.

Words to know

Echolocation
Using sounds and listening to the echoes to find things.
Melon
The round, flexible forehead of a beluga.

🧪 Try it yourself

Make five different sounds with your mouth: a click, a whistle, a squeak, a hum and a squeal. You just made a beluga chorus!
