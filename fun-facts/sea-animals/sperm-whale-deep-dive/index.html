Sperm whales dive deeper than any other marine mammal.

The big idea

Sperm whales are the deepest-diving marine mammals, reaching about 3,000 meters. They also have the largest brain of any animal.

How it works

🌊

Deep, long dives

A usual dive lasts 20 to 25 minutes and goes 400 to 600 meters down. The deepest reach about 2,987 meters.

🦑

Squid hunters

They mainly eat large and medium squid, and occasionally giant squid.

🧠

Biggest brain

A sperm whale brain can weigh up to six times as much as a human brain.

More about sperm whales

  • Adult males are much bigger than females, the biggest size difference of any whale.
  • A male usually has 22 to 25 teeth on each side of the lower jaw.
  • A male's head is about one third of its body length.

Words to know

Marine mammal
A mammal that lives in the sea, like whales, dolphins and seals.
Squid
A soft sea animal with ten arms.

🧪 Try it yourself

Count how long you can hold your breath, then stop and breathe. A sperm whale usually holds its breath for 20 to 25 minutes!
