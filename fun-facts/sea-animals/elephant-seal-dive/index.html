The big idea

Northern elephant seals can dive more than a mile deep and hold their breath for nearly two hours. Even young seals swim thousands of miles on their first trip.

How it works

🌊

Very deep

Adults can dive more than a mile down.

⏱

Long breath-holds

Adults can hold their breath for nearly two hours.

🗺

Long journeys

On a first trip, young seals swim about 5,000 miles or more.

More about elephant seals

  • Some adults have covered more than 13,000 miles in a migration season.
  • Northern seals often feed at depths similar to southern seals. Their deepest, longest dives are mostly for resting or traveling.
  • After 30 days at sea, young northern seals were diving over 850 feet.

Words to know

Juvenile
A young animal that is not yet fully grown.
Migration
A long trip animals make at certain times of year.

🧪 Try it yourself

Take a deep breath and hold it while a grown-up counts the seconds. An elephant seal can do it for almost two hours!
