🧠 How does a jellyfish live without a brain?

Jellyfish have no brain, no heart and no bones, yet they sense the whole ocean.

The big idea

Jellyfish have no brain, heart or bones. A nerve net senses the water around them, and fossils show they are more than 500 million years old.

How it works

🕸

Nerve net

A web of nerves spread through the body senses temperature, salt, oxygen, vibrations and currents.

💡

Light sensors

Clusters of nerve endings called rhopalia sense light and help with balance.

💧

Mostly water

The jelly layer between the skin layers is mostly water.

Quick stats

0brains

More about jellyfishs

  • Some jellyfish have simple eyes, even without a brain.
  • The jelly layer is called mesoglea.
  • Jellyfish are not fish. They are related to corals and anemones.

Words to know

Nerve net
A web of nerve cells spread through the body.
Mesoglea
The jelly-like layer in a jellyfish.
