The big idea

A sea urchin chews with five teeth in a ring, called Aristotle's lantern, and scrapes algae off rocks.

How it works

5️⃣

Five teeth

Five calcium carbonate teeth form the lantern.

🏛

Named by Aristotle

He compared the mouth to a horn lantern with the panes left out.

🌿

Algae scraper

Urchins scrape algae off rock surfaces.

More about sea urchins

  • A purple sea urchin is the species described in the Living Coast source.
  • Urchins live on the sea floor.
  • The lantern is on the underside of the body.

Words to know

Algae
Simple plant-like living things in water.
Calcium carbonate
A hard mineral that makes shells and chalk.

🧪 Try it yourself

Draw five triangles in a circle, like five teeth meeting in the middle. That is the shape of the lantern!
