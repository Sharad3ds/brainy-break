⚔️ What is a swordfish bill for?

Swordfish use their bill to slash prey and have warm eyes in cold water.

The big idea

Swordfish slash large prey with their bill and have special organs that warm their eyes and brain by up to 15 °C above the water, which helps them see in cold, dim depths.

How it works

⚔

A slashing bill

The bill is used to slash larger prey before eating it.

🔥

Heater organ

Special heat-producing organs keep the eyes and brain warmer than the water.

🌊

Deep water

They are comfortable around 800 meters down but sometimes bask or breach at the surface.

Quick stats

More about swordfishs

  • Swordfish spawn in the upper ocean in warm water, above about 23 °C.
  • The twilight zone is 200 to 1,000 meters deep.
  • Smaller prey is likely swallowed whole.

Words to know

Bill
The long, pointed upper jaw of a swordfish.
Twilight zone
The dim part of the ocean between 200 and 1,000 meters.

🧪 Try it yourself

Stand in a dark room and look at a small light. Your eyes can adjust. Swordfish eyes get help from a warm heater to see in the cold, dim sea.
