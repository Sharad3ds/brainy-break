👁 Why does a giant squid have eyes as big as basketballs?

The giant squid has some of the biggest eyes in the animal world, and scientists think they spot whales.

The big idea

A giant squid's eye is about 10.5 inches across. Scientists think such big eyes help spot a sperm whale coming in the dark deep sea.

How it works

👁

Huge eyes

The eye is about 10.5 inches across, the pupil about 3.5.

🌑

Dim light

Deep water has little light, so big eyes gather more.

🐋

Whale watch

A moving whale can make glowing plankton light up.

Quick stats

10.5 ineye width (26.7 cm)
3.5 inpupil width (8.9 cm)
10 incolossal squid eye (25.4 cm)
1main predator: sperm whale

More about giant squids

  • Whale sonar is not something squid can hear, so seeing the glow matters.
  • Eyes bigger than an orange do not help spot small prey nearby.
  • A colossal squid eye measured about 10 inches.

Words to know

Pupil
The dark opening in the middle of an eye.
Predator
An animal that hunts other animals.
