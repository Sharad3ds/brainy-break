🏠 How do hermit crabs swap homes?

Hermit crabs line up by size and trade shells in a chain.

The big idea

Hermit crabs line up by size and trade shells in a vacancy chain, so each crab in the line gets a better-fitting home.

How it works

🐚

Borrowed homes

Hermit crabs need empty shells to protect their soft bodies.

⛓

Vacancy chain

One crab moves in and each one behind takes the shell left.

🌊

A threat

Ocean acidification can thin shells and affect crab choices.

More about hermit crabs

  • A crab that finds a shell too big waits nearby for a larger crab to upgrade.
  • The behavior was shown on the BBC show Life Story.
  • Acidification may make crabs less likely to give up poorly fitting shells.

Words to know

Vacancy chain
A line of animals each moving into the home left by the one before.
Acidification
The ocean becoming more acidic.

🧪 Try it yourself

Line up three shoes from big to small. Pretend a new big shoe appears. Who takes which shoe? That is a vacancy chain!
