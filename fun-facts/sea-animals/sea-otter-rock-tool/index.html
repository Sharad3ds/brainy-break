The big idea

Sea otters use rocks as tools to crack open shellfish and keep them in a pouch under their arm. Their fur is the densest of any animal, about a million hairs per square inch.

How it works

🪨

Tool users

Otters use rocks to open shellfish, which makes them one of the few marine mammals that use tools.

🧥

Super fur

With no blubber, otters keep warm with dense fur. Grooming spreads oils and traps air bubbles.

🍽

Big appetite

They can eat about 25% of their body weight a day, including sea urchins, crabs, mussels and clams.

More about sea otters

  • Otters spend many hours a day cleaning their fur.
  • The rock pouch is under their armpit.
  • Sea otters feed on the sea floor, so they dive to find food.

Words to know

Tool
An object an animal uses to do a job.
Blubber
A thick layer of fat under the skin that keeps animals warm.

🧪 Try it yourself

Wrap a hand in a thick glove and dip it in water. Does it get cold fast? A sea otter's super-thick fur traps air to stay warm.
