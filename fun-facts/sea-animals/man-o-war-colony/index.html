A man o war looks like one jellyfish, but it is a team of animals working together.

The big idea

A Portuguese man o war is a colony of specialized animals called zooids. Its tentacles are usually about 30 feet long, and detached ones can still sting.

How it works

🤝

Teamwork

Zooids are specialized animals that work together as one.

🎈

Floater

The float sits at the surface and holds gas.

⚠

Painful stings

The sting is a reflex, so even detached tentacles can sting.

Quick stats

More about Portuguese man o wars

  • A vinegar rinse deactivates stinging cells.
  • It floats at the surface and cannot swim on its own.
  • Beached ones can still sting.

Words to know

Siphonophore
A colony of animals that acts like one.
Zooid
One of the specialized members of a colony.
