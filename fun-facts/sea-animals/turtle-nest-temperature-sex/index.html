🌡 How does sand temperature decide if a turtle is a boy or girl?

Warm nests make more girls and cool nests make more boys.

The big idea

For sea turtles, nest temperature decides the sex. About 29 °C gives a 50:50 mix, warmer gives more girls, and cooler gives more boys.

How it works

🌡

Warm = girls

Nests above 31 °C in the middle third are 100% female.

❄

Cool = boys

Nests below about 27.7 °C are 100% male.

🏝

Climate change

Rising temperatures push sex ratios toward females.

More about sea turtles

  • The pivotal temperature can vary slightly by species.
  • Scientists try shading and spraying nests to cool them.
  • Raine Island has made nearly all female hatchlings since the 1990s.

Words to know

Incubation
The time an egg needs to develop before hatching.
Pivotal temperature
The temperature that gives an equal mix of boys and girls.
