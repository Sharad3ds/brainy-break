🚌 Which fish hitches a ride on sharks?

Remoras stick to sharks and manta rays with a sucker made from a fin.

The big idea

A remora's sucker is a rearranged dorsal fin. It lets the fish hitch rides on sharks, manta rays and boats, and eat scraps without harming its host.

How it works

🔧

A fin turned sucker

Three typical fin parts change during development to make the sucker disc.

🤝

Not a parasite

Remoras hitch rides and eat food scraps from their hosts without harming them.

🔬

Studied from larvae

Researchers tracked the disc forming and compared it to a white perch dorsal fin.

Quick stats

1 mup to about 1 m (3 ft) long

More about remoras

  • Remoras hitch rides on manta rays, sharks and boat hulls in tropical waters.
  • Young remoras have hooked teeth on the lower jaw that may help them hang on before the disc forms. Scientists think this might be so, but it is not confirmed.
  • The study was published in the Journal of Morphology.

Words to know

Dorsal fin
The fin on the back of a fish.
Host
The animal that another animal rides on or lives with.

🧪 Try it yourself

Press a suction cup to a window and then try to pull it off. A remora's sucker disc grips in a similar way!
