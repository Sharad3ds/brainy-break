🎵 Why do humpback whales sing?

Male humpback whales sing long songs, and other whales learn them.

The big idea

Male humpback whales sing long, complex songs that can last for hours. Whales in a group share and change their songs over time, a bit like a song going around a playground.

How it works

🎶

Moans, cries and squeaks

A song is built from many sounds put together in patterns.

🤝

Shared songs

Males in a group learn the same song and add their own changes, which others then pick up.

🔄

Songs change

In a 13-year study of whales off eastern Australia, songs gradually grew longer and more elaborate, then whales switched to a simpler song.

More about humpback whales

  • Scientists do not know exactly why males sing. Ideas include attracting females or impressing other males.
  • Songs can travel between groups of whales across oceans, so a tune from one place can show up in another.
  • Humpbacks are mammals. Calves drink their mother's milk.

Words to know

Population
A group of one kind of animal living in the same area.
Cultural revolution
When whales quickly switch to a new kind of song.

🧪 Try it yourself

Hum a short tune and teach it to a friend. Ask them to add one new note and pass it on. That is how a whale song can change as it spreads!
