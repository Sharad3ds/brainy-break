🥊 How hard can a mantis shrimp punch?

A smasher mantis shrimp strikes with the acceleration of a bullet.

The big idea

A smasher mantis shrimp's club moves at about 50 mph and punches with a force over 2,500 times its own weight. A collapsing bubble adds a second hit.

How it works

🥊

Fast club

The club edge moves at about 50 mph.

🫧

Bubble blow

The claw makes a bubble that collapses and hits again.

🐌

Shell cracker

Smashers crack shells, and even quarter-inch glass.

Quick stats

50 mphclub speed

2,500×body weight

More about mantis shrimps

  • The claw moves so fast it makes a low-pressure bubble in the water.
  • Scientists compare the acceleration to that of a 22-caliber bullet.
  • The punch is about 50 times faster than a human blink.

Words to know

Cavitation
A bubble that forms and collapses in fast-moving water.
Microsecond
One millionth of a second.

🧪 Try it yourself

Snap your fingers as fast as you can. A mantis shrimp punch takes under 80 microseconds, far faster than any snap!
