💥 How does a tiny shrimp make a sound as loud as a gunshot?

A pistol shrimp snaps its claw and makes a super hot, super loud bubble.

The big idea

A pistol shrimp's snap makes a bubble that pops at up to 218 decibels and gets hotter than 4,000 °F inside, stunning small prey.

How it works

💨

Fast jet

The claw shoots a water jet at about 60 mph.

🫧

Hot bubble

The bubble collapses with a loud pop, a flash, and over 4,000 °F inside.

🐟

Stun gun

The shock wave can stun small fish.

More about pistol shrimps

  • The bubble also makes a flash of light.
  • The sound pressure can be up to 11.74 psi at 4 centimeters.
  • The bubble pop is the loud part, not the claw hitting.

Words to know

Decibel
A unit for measuring how loud a sound is.
Shock wave
A strong wave of pressure.
