The big idea

Male seahorses carry the babies in a pouch on their bodies for about 20 days, then give birth. Some species release more than 1,000 babies at once.

How it works

🥚

Eggs in a pouch

The female places her eggs into the male's pouch, and the male fertilizes them.

🗓

About 20 days

The babies form eyes, tiny snouts and tails while in the pouch.

🌊

Many babies

Some species release over 1,000 young at once, but only one or two usually survive to adulthood.

More about seahorses

  • Pipefish and seadragons, close relatives of seahorses, also have dads who carry the young.
  • The pouch is like a kangaroo's, not an internal womb.
  • After the birth, the father gives no more care.

Words to know

Pouch
A pocket of skin on the body that holds babies or eggs.
Courtship
The dance or display animals do before mating.
