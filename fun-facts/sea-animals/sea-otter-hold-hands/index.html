🦦 Why do sea otters sleep together?

Sea otters float in groups and wrap up in kelp so they do not drift apart.

The big idea

Sea otters sleep floating together in groups called rafts. They stay close by touching, wrapping in kelp, and sometimes holding paws, so they do not drift apart.

How it works

🌿

Kelp wraps

Otters wrap themselves in kelp so they stay in place.

🔥

Staying warm

Sea otters have no blubber. Their fur and staying together help them keep warm.

🛡

Safer together

Rafts help otters avoid predators and save energy.

More about sea otters

  • Rafts are usually split by sex, with males in one group and females in another.
  • Sea otters were hunted nearly to extinction in the 1800s.
  • A 2024 study found sea otter numbers were the strongest clue to kelp forest changes from 1910 to 2016.

Words to know

Raft
A group of sea otters floating together.
Kelp
A tall, fast-growing seaweed that grows in underwater forests.

🧪 Try it yourself

Place two paper boats in a bowl of water. Do they drift apart? Now tie them together with a bit of string, like otters wrapped in kelp.
