🥒 Why does a sea cucumber push out its insides?

Sea cucumbers can expel their organs and regrow them in a few weeks.

The big idea

Sea cucumbers can push out their organs through the head or rear and regrow them in a few weeks. Scientists are still working out why.

How it works

😮

Autotomy

The animal shoots out its intestines and some other organs.

❓

Reasons

It may flush parasites, survive low oxygen or put off predators.

🔁

Regrowth

The gut regrows first, and the animal is good as new in a few weeks.

More about sea cucumbers

  • Some species expel separate organs, called Cuvierian tubules, when attacked.
  • Sea cucumbers are echinoderms, like sea stars and urchins.
  • They live on the sea floor.

Words to know

Autotomy
When an animal drops or pushes out a body part on purpose.
Echinoderm
A spiny-skinned sea animal, like a sea star.
