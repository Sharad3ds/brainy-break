The big idea

A sea star has two stomachs and can digest prey outside its body. It has up to 15,000 tube feet per arm and can regrow lost arms.

How it works

🥣

Two stomachs

The cardiac stomach releases enzymes on prey and the pyloric stomach engulfs it.

🦶

Glue feet

Tube feet make a glue to hold on and a solvent to let go.

🔁

Regrowth

Some species can regrow a whole body from one arm.

More about sea stars

  • Sea stars have eyespots, not eyes, at the end of each arm.
  • Some species have 10, 20 or even 50 arms.
  • The sun star can weigh more than 10 pounds.

Words to know

Tube feet
Tiny sucker-like feet used for walking and gripping.
Regenerate
To grow back a lost body part.
