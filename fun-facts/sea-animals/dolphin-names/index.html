📛 Do dolphins have names?

Dolphins have special whistles that work a bit like names.

The big idea

Bottlenose dolphins each have a signature whistle. When separated, dolphins may copy a friend's whistle, which is a bit like calling their name.

How it works

🎶

Own whistle

A 2006 study found that dolphins use unique whistles to identify themselves.

📻

Copying to reunite

In a study off Sarasota Bay, Florida, separated dolphins copied the whistles of their companions.

👩‍👧

Close bonds

Mothers with calves and allied males were most likely to do it.

More about bottlenose dolphins

  • Dolphins are mammals. They breathe air through a blowhole.
  • Dolphins find things with clicks and echoes. This is called echolocation.
  • A 2011 study suggested that sperm whales may give each other names too.

Words to know

Signature whistle
A special whistle that belongs to one dolphin.
Echolocation
Using sounds and listening for echoes to find things.

🧪 Try it yourself

Invent a special whistle for yourself. Ask a friend to copy it. Could you find each other across a playground using only your whistle?
