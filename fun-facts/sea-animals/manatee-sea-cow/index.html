🌿 Why are manatees called sea cows?

Manatees graze on underwater plants all day.

The big idea

Manatees are called sea cows because they graze on plants. An adult eats about 4 to 9 percent of its body weight each day and spends 5 to 8 hours eating.

How it works

🌿

Plant-eaters

They eat mainly aquatic plants, such as cordgrass, turtle grass, eelgrass, water hyacinth and hydrilla.

🍽

Big meals

About 32 pounds of plants a day, for 5 to 8 hours of eating.

🫧

Holding breath

They can stay under up to 12 minutes, but usually about 4.5 minutes.

More about manatees

  • Calves are 3 to 4 feet long at birth and weigh 40 to 60 pounds.
  • The West Indian manatee is one of four living species in the group called Sirenia.
  • Manatees sometimes eat small sea animals and fish too.

Words to know

Graze
To eat plants slowly over a long time.
Aquatic plant
A plant that grows in water.

🧪 Try it yourself

Weigh some grapes or apples on a kitchen scale and imagine eating that much, plus much more. A manatee eats about 32 pounds of plants a day!
