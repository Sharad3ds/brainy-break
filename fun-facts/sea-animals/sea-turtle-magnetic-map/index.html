🧭 How do sea turtles find their way home?

The big idea

Loggerhead turtles travel thousands of miles over a decade or more and use Earth's magnetic field to return to the area where they hatched.

How it works

🧲

Magnetic map

Turtles use variations in Earth's magnetic field to navigate.

🏠

Back home

After years at sea they return to the nesting area where they hatched.

❓

Mystery

Scientists are still working out how they sense the field.
