🪼 Which giant turtle eats jellyfish?

The leatherback has a leathery shell, backward spines in its throat, and dives over 1,000 meters.

The big idea

The leatherback turtle has a leathery shell, eats jellyfish with help from backward spines in its throat, and can dive about 1,280 meters.

How it works

🛡

Flexible shell

Unlike other sea turtles, its shell is leathery with seven ridges.

🪼

Jellyfish eater

Backward spines in the mouth and throat hold slippery prey.

🌊

Deep diver

Dives to about 1,280 meters, for up to 85 minutes.

More about leatherback sea turtles

  • Leatherbacks are the biggest of the sea turtles.
  • Salps and siphonophores are also on the menu.
  • The shell has seven ridges.

Words to know

Salp
A clear, jelly-like sea animal that floats in the water.
Leathery
Tough and flexible like leather.

🧪 Try it yourself

Compare a soft leather shoe and a hard plastic cup. The leatherback shell is like the shoe, flexible, while other turtles have a hard plastic-like shell.
