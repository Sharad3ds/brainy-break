⏪ Is there a jellyfish that can turn back into a baby?

The big idea

Turritopsis dohrnii can turn back into a polyp when hurt or starving. In theory the cycle could repeat, but it is not truly immortal.

How it works

⏪

Cell change

The bell and tentacles break down and the cells change type to form a polyp.

⚠

Not unbeatable

Predators and disease still get most of them.

🌍

Worldwide

First found in the Mediterranean. Now in temperate to tropical seas.

More about jellyfishs

  • Triggers include starvation, sudden temperature change, low salt and damage.
  • It may have spread around the world in the ballast water of ships.
  • Scientists study the rewind to learn how cells change.

Words to know

Polyp
The young, fixed stage of a jellyfish.
Medusa
The bell-shaped adult jellyfish.

🧪 Try it yourself

Draw a jellyfish life cycle as a circle: polyp, baby jelly, adult jelly, and then an arrow back to polyp. That is the rewind!
