🐚 How does a nautilus float with its shell?

A nautilus uses a chambered shell and a tiny tube to control floating.

The big idea

A nautilus shell has more than 30 chambers. A tube called the siphuncle moves water in and out to control buoyancy.

How it works

🐚

Chambers

The animal lives in the outer chamber. The others hold gas.

🧵

A buoyancy tube

The siphuncle moves water in and out of chambers.

🌊

Depth limit

Shells implode around 750–900 meters, which limits where they live.

Quick stats

30+chambers
47pairs of tentacles (up to)
500M+years: nautiloids
750 mshell depth limit begins

More about nautiluss

  • Nautilus tentacles have no suckers or hooks.
  • Nautilus eyes are large but simple, with no lens or cornea.
  • Young nautiluses have color all around the shell. Adults only on the top.

Words to know

Siphuncle
The tube that connects the shell chambers.
Buoyancy
How much something floats or sinks.

🧪 Try it yourself

Fill a bottle a little with water and see how it floats. A nautilus changes the water in its chambers to float at the right level.
