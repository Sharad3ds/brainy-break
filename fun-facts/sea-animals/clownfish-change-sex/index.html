🔄 Can clownfish change from boy to girl?

All clownfish are born male, and the boss of the group can become female.

The big idea

All clownfish are born male. In a group, the most dominant male can change into a female, and the change can take weeks or months.

How it works

♂

Born male

All clownfish start life as males.

👑

The boss changes

The most dominant male becomes female. If the female dies, the top male replaces her.

⏳

A slow change

It affects size, color, markings, body parts and behavior, and takes weeks or months.

More about clownfishs

  • Clownfish spend their lives in a small area, so there are few chances to meet a mate.
  • Scientists think environmental and chemical factors are involved in the change.
  • This kind of change is a clever way to make sure a pair can have babies.

Words to know

Dominant
The boss of the group.
Matriarch
The female leader of a group.
