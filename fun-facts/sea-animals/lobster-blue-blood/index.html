🦞 Why do lobsters have blue blood and stomach teeth?

Lobster blood turns blue in air, and their teeth are in their stomach.

The big idea

A lobster's blood turns blue in air, it chews with three teeth in its stomach, and it tastes with its feet.

How it works

🔵

Copper blood

Hemocyanin is a copper-based pigment that turns blue with oxygen.

🦷

Stomach teeth

The gastric mill grinds food inside the stomach.

🦶

Taste feet

Chemosensory hairs on the legs work like taste buds.

More about lobsters

  • Cooked lobster blood turns white.
  • Each eye can have up to 10,000 square-ended tubes that reflect light.
  • Regrowing a lost limb can take several years.

Words to know

Hemocyanin
A blue copper-based pigment in some animal blood.
Molt
To shed an old shell and grow a new one.
