🦄 What is a narwhal's tusk?

The unicorn of the sea has a tusk that is really a tooth.

The big idea

A narwhal's tusk is a long, spiral tooth that can grow up to about 9 feet (3 meters). Scientists think it may show rank, and it may also help the narwhal sense the water.

How it works

🦷

A tooth, not a horn

In most males the left canine tooth grows out through the gums in a counterclockwise spiral.

♂

Mostly males

Most tusks belong to adult males. Rarely, a female grows one or two.

📡

Very sensitive

A 2014 study found nerve endings in the tusk that may detect changes in temperature and salinity.

More about narwhals

  • Narwhals are toothed whales, but their mouths have no other working teeth.
  • Some males have two tusks, usually of different sizes.
  • Females are smaller: about 13 feet long and 2,000 pounds.

Words to know

Canine tooth
A pointed tooth near the front of the mouth.
Counterclockwise
Turning the opposite way to the hands of a clock.

🧪 Try it yourself

Twist a paper towel into a long spiral and try holding it like a tusk. Which way does your spiral turn? A narwhal tusk turns counterclockwise.
