👁 How can a jellyfish see without a brain?

A box jellyfish has 24 eyes and finds its way home without a brain.

The big idea

A box jellyfish has 24 eyes of four kinds. Its upward-looking lens eyes help it swim back to mangrove trees, with no central brain.

How it works

👁

24 eyes

Four types. Two have lenses and can form images.

🌳

Home in the trees

It stays near mangrove roots, where it feeds on copepods.

🧠

No central brain

Its navigation works without a central brain, the study found.

More about box jellyfishs

  • The jellyfish in the study is Tripedalia cystophora.
  • When the trees were hidden, the jellyfish lost their direction.
  • The study was by Anders Garm at the University of Copenhagen.

Words to know

Mangrove
A tree that grows in salty water along the coast.
Lens
A clear part of an eye that focuses light.

🧪 Try it yourself

Close one eye, then both. Which is easier for finding a toy? The jellyfish has 24, so it has plenty of backup.
