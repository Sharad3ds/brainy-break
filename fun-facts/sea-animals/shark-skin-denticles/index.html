🦷 Why is shark skin made of tiny teeth?

Shark skin is covered in tiny tooth-like scales.

The big idea

Shark skin is covered in dermal denticles, tiny tooth-like scales that work like flexible chain mail and help the shark swim smoothly and quietly.

How it works

🦷

Tiny skin teeth

Each scale has a base, a narrow stalk and a ridged crown.

⛓

Chain mail

The overlapping crowns protect from predators and parasites without limiting movement.

💧

Less drag

In white sharks, the ridges reduce drag by up to 8%. Smooth skin also makes little noise.

More about sharks

  • The scales are made of dentine, with hard mineral and soft collagen, about as hard as granite according to the source.
  • Fast sharks like the shortfin mako have smaller, lighter denticles.
  • Because the skin is so smooth, swimming sharks make little turbulence noise.

Words to know

Dermal denticle
A tiny tooth-like scale in shark skin.
Drag
The force that slows something moving through water or air.

🧪 Try it yourself

Stroke a feather from base to tip, then the other way. Feel the difference? Shark skin feels smooth one way and rough the other too.
