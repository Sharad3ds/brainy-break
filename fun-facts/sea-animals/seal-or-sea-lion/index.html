🦁 What's the difference between a seal and a sea lion?

The big idea

Sea lions have ear flaps, long front flippers, can walk on land and bark loudly. True seals have no ear flaps, wriggle on their bellies and make soft grunts.

How it works

👂

Ears

Sea lions have small external ear flaps. Seals have only tiny holes on the sides of the head.

🦶

Flippers

Seals have small, thinly webbed flippers with claws. Sea lions have longer front flippers mostly covered in skin.

📢

Voices

Sea lions bark loudly. Seals are quieter and make soft grunts.

More about sea lions

  • On land, a seal's back flippers point backward and cannot rotate.
  • A sea lion turns its back flippers forward under its body to walk.
  • Both are marine mammals that live in and near the sea.

Words to know

Flipper
A flat limb that helps an animal swim.
Marine mammal
A mammal that lives in the sea.
