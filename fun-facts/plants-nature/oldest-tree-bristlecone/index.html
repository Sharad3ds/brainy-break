The big idea

The oldest known tree is a bristlecone pine, estimated at about 5,000 years old.

How it works

🌲

Tough tree

Great Basin bristlecone pines live high in dry mountains.

🤫

Protected

The oldest one's location is kept secret so nobody harms it.

📛

Methuselah

The oldest named tree is about 4,856 years old.

More about nature

  • The age is an estimate based on counting tree rings.
  • Bristlecone pines grow slowly in harsh places.
  • These trees are older than the Egyptian pyramids.

Words to know

Bristlecone
A very long-lived kind of pine tree.
Estimate
A careful guess based on evidence.
