🧊 How big can a hailstone be?

A hailstone that fell in Vivian, South Dakota in 2010 measured 8 inches across.

The big idea

The record Vivian hailstone was 8 inches across and weighed nearly 2 pounds.

How it works

📅

Record day

It fell on July 23, 2010.

📏

Wide

It measured 8 inches across and 18.625 inches around.

⚖️

Heavy

It weighed 1.9375 pounds when measured.

More about nature

  • The numbers are from after the stone had melted a little.
  • South Dakota is in the central United States.
  • Hail falls from tall thunderstorm clouds.

Words to know

Hail
Balls of ice that fall from thunderstorms.
Circumference
The distance around a round object.

🧪 Try it yourself

Measure 8 inches on a ruler. Imagine a ball of ice that wide falling from the sky. Ouch!
