The big idea

About 71 percent of Earth's surface is water, and about 96.5 percent of that water is in the oceans.

How it works

🌊

Oceans

Oceans, seas and bays hold about 96.5 percent of all water.

🧊

Ice

Over 68 percent of fresh water is in ice and glaciers.

🌱

Ground

About 30 percent of fresh water is in the ground.

Quick stats

71%of the surface is water
96.5%of water is in oceans
68%+of fresh water is ice
30%of fresh water is underground

More about nature

  • Most of Earth's water is salty.
  • Fresh water that we can easily use is a small part.
  • Saving water helps everyone.

Words to know

Fresh water
Water without much salt in it.
Glacier
A huge, slow river of ice.

🧪 Try it yourself

Turn off the tap while brushing your teeth. Count how many cups of water you save!
