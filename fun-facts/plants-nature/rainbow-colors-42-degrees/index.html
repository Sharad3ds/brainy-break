🌈 How are rainbows made?

Sunlight bends and bounces inside raindrops and comes out at about 42 degrees.

The big idea

Rainbows form when sunlight bends and reflects inside raindrops, leaving at about 42 degrees.

How it works

🌧️

Raindrops

Each round drop bends the light and splits it into colors.

🔴

Color order

Red is on the outer edge and blue on the inner edge.

🌅

Sun behind you

The sun must be behind you and the rain in front.

Quick stats

More about nature

  • A double rainbow forms when light reflects twice inside the drops.
  • The second bow is at about 50 degrees and has reversed colors.
  • We see only an arc because the ground blocks the rest of the circle.

Words to know

Refraction
The bending of light as it passes through something.
Arc
A curved part of a circle.

🧪 Try it yourself

On a sunny day, use a garden hose spray with your back to the sun. Can you make your own rainbow?
