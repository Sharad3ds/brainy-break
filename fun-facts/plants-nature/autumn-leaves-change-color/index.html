🍂 Why do leaves change color in fall?

Green chlorophyll fades, and yellow, orange and red colors show up.

The big idea

Leaves turn yellow, orange and red as green chlorophyll fades in the fall.

How it works

🟡

Hidden yellow

Carotenoids stay in the leaf after the green disappears.

🔴

New red

Anthocyanins are made just before leaves fall.

🟠

Mixing

Red plus yellow makes orange.

Quick stats

3main pigments
Greenchlorophyll
Yellowcarotenoids
Redanthocyanins

More about nature

  • About 70 percent of the shrubs and trees at Harvard Forest make red pigments.
  • Different trees turn different colors.
  • Some leaves, like red maples, can vary even within one tree.

Words to know

Pigment
A natural color inside a plant.
Carotenoid
A yellow or orange pigment in leaves.
