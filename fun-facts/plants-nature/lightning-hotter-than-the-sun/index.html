The big idea

Lightning can heat the air to about 50,000 degrees Fahrenheit, around five times the Sun's surface.

How it works

🔥

Super hot

The air along the bolt's path can reach about 50,000 degrees Fahrenheit.

☀️

Hotter than the Sun

That is about five times the Sun's surface temperature, says the NWS.

💥

Boom

Rapid heating and cooling of air makes thunder.

More about nature

  • The heated channel of air is very thin.
  • Indoors is the safest place during a thunderstorm.
  • Thunder is the sound of the air expanding.

Words to know

Lightning
A giant spark of electricity in the sky.
Thunder
The sound made by lightning heating the air.

🧪 Try it yourself

During a storm, stay indoors and watch the sky from a window. Count the seconds between the flash and the boom!
