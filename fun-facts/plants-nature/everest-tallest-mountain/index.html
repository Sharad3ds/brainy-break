The big idea

Mount Everest stands about 29,031.7 feet (8,848.86 meters) above sea level.

How it works

📏

New height

Nepal and China announced the new height in December 2020.

📜

Old number

A 1954 survey gave 29,028 feet.

🌊

Sea level

Heights are measured above sea level.

More about nature

  • The height grew a few feet in the new count.
  • Measuring a mountain takes careful survey work.
  • Everest is in the Himalayas, on the border of Nepal and China.

Words to know

Sea level
The height of the ocean, used as zero for measuring.
Survey
Careful measuring of land.
