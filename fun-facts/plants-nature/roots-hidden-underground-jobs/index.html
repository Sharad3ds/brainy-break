🥕 What do roots do?

Roots anchor a plant, soak up water and minerals, and sometimes store food.

The big idea

Roots anchor the plant, drink water and minerals, and some store food.

How it works

⚓

Anchor

Roots hold the plant in the soil.

💧

Drink

They absorb water and minerals and carry them up.

🥕

Storage

Carrots and turnips swell with stored food.

More about nature

  • Most of the root growth is hidden below ground.
  • Root hairs sit just behind the root tip.
  • Roots send water up to the stem.

Words to know

Root
The part of a plant that grows in the soil.
Mineral
A natural nutrient taken from soil.

🧪 Try it yourself

Carefully pull up a weed with a grown-up. Look for the tiny hairs on the roots!
