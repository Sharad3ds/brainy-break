🌿 How do mangrove trees live in salt water?

Mangroves are salt-tolerant trees with stilt-like roots that protect coasts and shelter young fish.

The big idea

Mangroves grow in salt water, and their roots protect coasts and shelter young fish.

How it works

🦵

Prop roots

Stilt-like roots add stability and bring oxygen to roots underground.

🌊

Coast guard

They reduce wave action and help protect shorelines.

🐟

Nursery

Many reef fish use mangroves as nursery habitat.

Quick stats

Saltthey tolerate

Seedlingsstart on the parent tree

More about nature

  • Roots trap sediment, which can build up new land.
  • Barnacles, sponges and algae live on exposed prop roots.
  • They grow in low-oxygen soil where water moves slowly.

Words to know

Mangrove
A tree that lives in salty coastal water.
Sediment
Tiny bits of soil and sand carried by water.
