💨 What makes the wind blow?

Wind is air moving from high pressure to low pressure, driven by uneven heating from the Sun.

The big idea

Wind is air moving from high pressure to low pressure, powered by the Sun.

How it works

☀️

Uneven heat

Different places warm by different amounts.

⬆️

Rising air

Warm air rises and leaves low pressure.

🌬️

Pressure gap

The bigger the pressure difference, the faster the wind.

Quick stats

2zones: high and low pressure
1power source: the Sun
Biggergap means stronger wind
Oceansand mountains change heating

More about nature

  • Oceans and mountains change how much each area warms.
  • Cooler air is denser than warm air.
  • Wind is the air that we feel moving.

Words to know

Pressure
How hard the air pushes down.
Dense
Packed tightly.

🧪 Try it yourself

Make a paper pinwheel and hold it outside. Which way does it spin when the wind blows?
