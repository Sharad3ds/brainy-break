🌲 What are pine cones for?

Pine cones are where conifers make their seeds, no flowers needed.

The big idea

Conifers make seeds in cones instead of flowers.

How it works

♂️

Pollen cones

Smaller male cones release pollen.

♀️

Seed cones

Seeds grow under the scales of female cones.

🐿️

Seeds travel

Wind or animals spread the seeds.

Quick stats

550+kinds of conifer
2kinds of cones: male and female
0flowers needed
Needlesmany have needle-like leaves

More about nature

  • Conifers include pines, spruces, firs, cedars, redwoods and yews.
  • Most conifers are evergreen, but larches shed their needles in winter.
  • Needles often have a waxy coating that limits water loss.

Words to know

Conifer
A tree that makes seeds in cones.
Evergreen
A plant that keeps its leaves all year.

🧪 Try it yourself

If you find a pine cone, look between the scales. Seeds may be tucked under them!
