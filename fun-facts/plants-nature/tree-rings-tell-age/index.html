🪵 What do tree rings tell us?

Each ring in a tree trunk is usually one year of growth, and wide or narrow rings tell a story.

The big idea

Each tree ring shows one year of growth. Counting rings tells a tree's age.

How it works

🔢

Count them

One ring equals one year of growth.

↔️

Wide or narrow

Wide rings suggest good conditions. Narrow rings can mean drought or disease.

🌡️

Climate clues

Rings help scientists work out how the climate changed long ago.

More about nature

  • Rings reach back far beyond the roughly 150 years of weather measurements.
  • The study of tree rings is called dendrochronology.
  • Scientists can sample a tree with a thin tool without cutting it down.

Words to know

Ring
A circle of wood a tree adds each year.
Drought
A long time with very little rain.

🧪 Try it yourself

Next time you see a tree stump, ask a grown-up if you can count the rings. Each ring is one year!
