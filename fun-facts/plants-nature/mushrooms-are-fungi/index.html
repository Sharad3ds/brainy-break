🍄 Is a mushroom a plant?

Mushrooms are fungi, not plants, and the mushroom is only a small part of the fungus.

The big idea

Mushrooms are fungi, not plants. They spread tiny spores instead of seeds.

How it works

🚫

No chlorophyll

Fungi cannot make food from light like plants do.

🕸️

Mycelium

The main body is a network of filaments in soil or wood.

💨

Spores

Spores are extremely fine and take the place of seeds.

More about nature

  • The mushroom is the fruiting structure of the fungus.
  • A mushroom often collapses after it releases its spores.
  • Never eat wild mushrooms unless an expert says it is safe.

Words to know

Fungus
A living thing like a mushroom or mold, not a plant or animal.
Spore
A tiny cell that can grow into a new fungus.

🧪 Try it yourself

Look for mushrooms after rain, but do not touch or eat them. Just look and draw what you see!
