🙈 Which plant folds up when you touch it?

The sensitive plant, Mimosa pudica, folds its leaves when touched, which may protect it from hungry animals.

The big idea

The sensitive plant folds its leaves when touched, a defense against hungry animals.

How it works

✋

Touch

Touch, shaking, watering or heat can trigger it.

🪡

Hairs

Tiny hairs called trichomes detect the touch.

🛡️

Defense

It is one of the plant's main defenses against herbivores.

Quick stats

Mimosa pudicascience name
Fastfolds in a moment
Roughertouch folds it faster
Varieshow long it stays closed

More about nature

  • Rougher handling makes the plant close more quickly.
  • How long it stays closed depends on how strong the touch was.
  • It is also called the touch-me-not.

Words to know

Herbivore
An animal that eats plants.
Trichome
A tiny hair on a plant.

🧪 Try it yourself

Gently touch a sensitive plant at a garden center with permission. Count how long it stays closed!
