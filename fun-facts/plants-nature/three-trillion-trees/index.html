The big idea

A 2015 study estimated about 3 trillion trees on Earth, roughly 422 per person.

How it works

🛰️

Counted smartly

Scientists used satellite pictures and forest measurements.

👤

Per person

That is around 422 trees for each person.

📅

An estimate

It comes from a 2015 study, and counts can change.

Quick stats

More about nature

  • The number is an estimate, not an exact count.
  • Satellites helped scientists see forests from space.
  • Trees grow in every kind of forest, from cold to tropical.

Words to know

Trillion
A thousand billion, or 1,000,000,000,000.
Estimate
A careful guess from the facts we have.
