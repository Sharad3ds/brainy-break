🍁 How do seeds travel?

Seeds hitch rides on wind, water and animals, or even pop out of pods.

The big idea

Seeds travel on wind, water and animals, and some pods burst to fling seeds out.

How it works

🌬️

Wind

Maple seeds spin and dandelion seeds float on parachutes.

🐿️

Animals

Hooks cling to fur, and birds spread seeds they swallow.

💥

Bursting

Touch-me-not pods burst open when touched lightly.

More about nature

  • Squirrels bury acorns, and uneaten ones may sprout.
  • Seeds near water can float downstream.
  • Some pine cones open only in high heat, such as a forest fire.

Words to know

Dispersal
The spreading of seeds to new places.
Pod
A case that holds seeds.

🧪 Try it yourself

Toss a maple seed or a dandelion puff into the air. Watch how it spins or floats!
