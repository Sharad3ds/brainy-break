🌊 What is a tsunami?

A tsunami is a very long wave caused by a sudden underwater shift, and it can move as fast as a jet plane.

The big idea

A tsunami is a very long wave that can travel as fast as a jet plane over deep water.

How it works

🌋

Sudden shift

Undersea earthquakes, landslides and eruptions displace water.

✈️

Fast

In deep water they can travel as fast as jet planes.

🏖️

Inland

They can reach far inland.

More about nature

  • Tsunamis are not the same as tides.
  • Their cause is unrelated to the tide level.
  • Warnings help people move to higher ground.

Words to know

Tsunami
A giant, very long wave from a sudden underwater event.
Warning
An alert telling people to stay safe.
