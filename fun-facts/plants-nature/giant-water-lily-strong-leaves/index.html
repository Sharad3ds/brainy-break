🪷 Which leaf can hold up a child?

Giant Amazon waterlily leaves reach nearly 3 meters wide and can support a small child.

The big idea

Giant waterlily leaves reach nearly 3 meters across and can support a small child.

How it works

📏

Big

Leaves reach nearly 3 meters across.

🕸️

Strong frame

A criss-cross network of thick veins supports the leaf.

🪶

Efficient

Researchers found it is strong for the amount of plant material used.

More about nature

  • The frame is flexible and can bend without damage.
  • The leaves have high sides, unlike flat disc-like leaves.
  • Engineers study natural designs like this one.

Words to know

Vein
A tube that carries water in a leaf and supports it.
Buoyant
Able to float.
