🌱 How does a seed sprout?

A seed soaks up water and swells, then a tiny root grows down and a shoot grows up.

The big idea

A seed soaks up water, then a tiny root grows down first and a shoot grows up.

How it works

💧

Water first

The seed absorbs water and swells.

🍞

Stored food

Food stored in the seed gives the embryo energy.

🌱

Root, then shoot

The root emerges first and the shoot rises the opposite way.

Quick stats

1stthe root comes out
2ndthe shoot grows up
Waterstarts everything
Cuessome seeds need cold, heat or light

More about nature

  • Some seeds wait for cold, heat, light or darkness before sprouting.
  • Tough seeds may need to be cracked open first.
  • Orchid seeds need certain fungi to help them grow.

Words to know

Germinate
To start to grow from a seed.
Embryo
The tiny baby plant inside a seed.

🧪 Try it yourself

Put a bean seed on a damp paper towel in a clear bag. Watch for the root to peek out in a few days!
