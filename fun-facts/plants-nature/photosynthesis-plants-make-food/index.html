🍃 How do plants make food?

Plants use light, water and carbon dioxide to make sugar, and they give off oxygen.

The big idea

Plants make their own food with light, water and carbon dioxide, a process called photosynthesis.

How it works

🟢

Green helper

Chlorophyll is a green pigment inside chloroplasts that absorbs light.

🍬

Sugar made

The sugar is used by plants to grow and is eaten by other living things.

💨

Oxygen out

Oxygen is released into the air during the process.

Quick stats

3things go in: light, water, carbon dioxide

More about nature

  • Photosynthesis happens inside tiny parts of plant cells called chloroplasts.
  • Chlorophyll absorbs mostly blue and red light.
  • Plants, algae and some bacteria can all do photosynthesis.

Words to know

Photosynthesis
How plants use light to make sugar from water and carbon dioxide.
Chlorophyll
The green pigment that catches light.

🧪 Try it yourself

Look at a leaf on a sunny day. It is a tiny food factory working quietly, with no noise at all!
