🪤 How does a Venus flytrap know when to snap?

A Venus flytrap closes only when two trigger hairs are touched within about 20 seconds.

The big idea

A Venus flytrap snaps shut only after two touches within about 20 seconds.

How it works

🔢

It counts

Two of the trigger hairs must be touched, about 20 seconds apart at most.

⚡

Fast

The trap closes in under a second.

🐜

Not worth it

Tiny insects can escape the loose first closure.

Quick stats

2touches to start closing
20 sectime window
6trigger hairs in all
Under 1 secto snap shut

More about nature

  • Extra touches by prey seal the trap fully.
  • Counting prevents wasted energy on raindrops.
  • Once sealed, enzymes digest the prey.

Words to know

Trigger hair
A tiny hair that senses a touch.
Enzyme
A natural chemical that breaks food down.

🧪 Try it yourself

Visit a plant shop or garden with a grown-up and look at a flytrap. Do not poke it, because snapping uses a lot of its energy!
