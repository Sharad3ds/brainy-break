🌻 Do sunflowers follow the sun?

Young sunflowers turn with the sun, but grown-up sunflowers face east.

The big idea

Young sunflowers follow the sun. Mature sunflowers stop and face east.

How it works

⏰

Inner clock

A roughly 24 hour clock drives the turning.

📏

Uneven growth

Different sides of the stem grow at different times of day.

🐝

Warm blooms

In one study, east-facing flowers got five times as many pollinators.

More about nature

  • Researchers found that bees like warm flowers.
  • East-facing flowers warm up faster in the morning.
  • The turning is called heliotropism.

Words to know

Pollinator
An animal, like a bee, that carries pollen between flowers.
Heliotropism
Turning toward the sun.

🧪 Try it yourself

If you see sunflowers, check which way they face in the morning and afternoon. Do they move?
