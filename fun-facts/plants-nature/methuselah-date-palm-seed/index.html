🌴 Can a 2,000 year old seed still grow?

A date palm seed about 2,000 years old sprouted and was nicknamed Methuselah.

The big idea

A date palm seed about 2,000 years old sprouted into a plant named Methuselah.

How it works

🏺

Found in ruins

The seeds were found at King Herod's fortress at Masada.

🔬

Dated

Radiocarbon dating confirmed the seed's age.

🏜️

Dry helps

Extreme heat and dryness may have helped keep it from rotting.

Quick stats

More about nature

  • The seeds waited about 40 years in storage before being planted.
  • Genetic tests suggest the palm may belong to an extinct lineage.
  • It is the oldest seed known to have grown into a plant.

Words to know

Radiocarbon dating
A way to work out how old something is.
Archaeologist
A scientist who studies the human past.
