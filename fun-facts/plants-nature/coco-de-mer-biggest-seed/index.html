🌴 What is the biggest seed in the world?

The coco de mer palm makes the biggest seed, up to about 50 centimeters long.

The big idea

The coco de mer palm makes the biggest seed, up to 50 centimeters long.

How it works

📏

Long

A seed can be up to 50 centimeters, or 19.6 inches, long.

⚖️

Heavy

It can weigh up to 25 kilograms, or 55 pounds.

🌴

Palm

It comes from the coco de mer palm, Lodoicea maldivica.

More about nature

  • The scientific name is Lodoicea maldivica.
  • Seeds range from tiny dust-like ones to giants like this.
  • Records like this list the largest known examples.

Words to know
