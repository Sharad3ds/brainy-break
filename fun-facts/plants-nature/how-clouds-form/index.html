The big idea

Clouds form when rising air cools and water vapor condenses onto tiny particles.

How it works

⬆️

Rise and cool

Rising air expands and cools until it reaches its dew point.

🧂

Tiny seeds

Droplets form around particles like smoke, ash, sea spray or soil.

🔄

Always changing

Droplets keep forming and evaporating, so clouds change shape.

More about nature

  • Clouds grow when condensation beats evaporation.
  • They shrink when evaporation wins.
  • Each droplet has a tiny particle at its core.

Words to know

Water vapor
Water as an invisible gas.
Condense
To change from a gas into tiny liquid drops.

🧪 Try it yourself

Breathe onto a cold mirror and watch it fog. Your breath vapor condensed, just like a cloud!
