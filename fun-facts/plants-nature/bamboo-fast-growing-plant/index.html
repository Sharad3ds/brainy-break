🎋 What is the fastest growing plant?

Some bamboo can grow about 91 centimeters, nearly a yard, in a single day.

The big idea

Some bamboo can grow about 91 centimeters in one day, under the best conditions.

How it works

🎋

Speedy

Bamboo is called the fastest growing plant in the world.

📏

Tall

One of the tallest kinds can reach about 30 meters.

🌤️

Right conditions

The top speed happens only in the best conditions.

Quick stats

91 cmper day, at best
3 ftper day, about
30 mtall, about
1stfastest, often said

More about nature

  • Bamboo is actually a kind of grass.
  • Different kinds of bamboo grow at different speeds.
  • Most bamboo does not grow at its top speed all the time.

Words to know

Bamboo
A tall, woody grass with hollow stems.
Conditions
The weather, water and soil a plant has.
