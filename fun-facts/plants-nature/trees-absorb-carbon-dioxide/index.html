🌳 How much carbon dioxide can a tree absorb?

According to the USDA, a mature tree can absorb more than 48 pounds of carbon dioxide in a year.

The big idea

A mature tree can absorb more than 48 pounds of carbon dioxide a year, says the USDA.

How it works

💨

Takes in CO2

Trees take carbon dioxide from the air to make food.

🍃

Leaves matter

How much depends on the tree type and how many leaves it has.

🌱

Helpers

Healthy trees help clean the air we breathe.

Quick stats

More about nature

  • The figure is reported from the USDA, so treat it as an average guide.
  • Different species give off different amounts of oxygen.
  • Douglas fir, spruce, beech and maple are named as big oxygen producers.

Words to know

Carbon dioxide
A gas that plants use to make food.
Mature
Fully grown.
