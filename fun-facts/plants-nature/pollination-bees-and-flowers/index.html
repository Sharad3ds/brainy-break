🐝 Why do flowers need bees?

Pollination moves pollen between flowers so seeds and fruit can form.

The big idea

Almost 80 percent of the world's roughly 1,400 crop plants need animals to pollinate them.

How it works

🌼

Pollen moves

Animals or wind carry pollen from one flower to another.

🍎

Fruit forms

Pollination lets seeds and fruit form.

🌾

Our food

More than 150 food crops in the US rely on pollinators.

More about nature

  • Wind and water can move pollen too.
  • Pollinators help produce larger, more flavorful fruit.
  • Plants such as sunflowers give us oils thanks to pollinators.

Words to know

Pollen
Tiny grains from flowers that help plants make seeds.
Pollinator
An animal that moves pollen between flowers.

🧪 Try it yourself

Watch a flower for two minutes. Does a bee or butterfly visit? Be gentle and keep your distance!
