💥 Why do some volcanoes explode?

Thin, runny magma lets gas escape, but thick, sticky magma traps gas and can erupt violently.

The big idea

Runny magma flows out gently. Thick, sticky magma traps gas and can explode.

How it works

🍯

Thickness

How easily gas escapes depends on how thick the magma is.

🌊

Gentle

Runny magma lets gas escape, so it flows out as lava.

💥

Explosive

Trapped gas builds pressure until it escapes violently.

More about nature

  • Slow Hawaiian lava flows rarely kill people.
  • Tephra means rock fragments blasted into the air.
  • Gas is the key to how an eruption behaves.

Words to know

Tephra
Bits of rock blasted out of a volcano.
Pressure
The push of trapped gas.

🧪 Try it yourself

Shake a fizzy drink bottle with a grown-up, then open it outside. The trapped gas shows how pressure builds!
