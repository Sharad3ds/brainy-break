🌋 What is the difference between magma and lava?

Magma is molten rock underground, and lava is molten rock that has reached the surface.

The big idea

Magma is molten rock underground. Lava is molten rock on the surface.

How it works

🌡️

Melted rock

Rock deep underground melts into magma.

⬆️

Lighter

Magma is lighter than the rock around it, so it rises.

🌋

Reaches out

It escapes through vents and fissures.

More about nature

  • Magma collects in chambers before an eruption.
  • The words describe where the molten rock is.
  • The same rock can have either name, depending on where it is.

Words to know

Magma
Molten rock underground.
Vent
An opening where a volcano erupts.
