🌪️ What is a tornado?

A tornado is a violently rotating column of air reaching from a thunderstorm to the ground.

The big idea

A tornado is a violently rotating column of air from a thunderstorm touching the ground.

How it works

⛈️

Supercells

Most tornadoes form from supercell thunderstorms.

⏱️

Short

Most last under 10 minutes.

📊

EF scale

The Enhanced Fujita scale rates tornadoes from EF0 to EF5 by damage.

Quick stats

More about nature

  • Scientists still study exactly how a funnel forms.
  • Width does not always show how strong a tornado is.
  • The peak US season is April through June.

Words to know

Supercell
A strong, rotating thunderstorm.
Funnel
The cone-shaped spinning cloud.

🧪 Try it yourself

Swirl water in a cup and watch the little vortex. If a tornado warning is ever issued, follow a grown-up to a safe place!
