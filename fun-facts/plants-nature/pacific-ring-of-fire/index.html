🔥 What is the Ring of Fire?

The Ring of Fire circles the Pacific Ocean and is the most active zone for earthquakes and volcanoes.

The big idea

The Ring of Fire is the most volcanically and seismically active zone in the world.

How it works

⭕

A circle

It rings the Pacific Ocean.

🧩

Plate edges

The Pacific Plate meets many surrounding plates there.

🌋

Active

It is the most active zone for earthquakes and volcanoes.

Quick stats

1big ring around the Pacific
Manyplates meet there
Mostactive zone on Earth
2big effects: volcanoes and quakes

More about nature

  • Plates are huge slabs of Earth's outer shell.
  • Seismic means related to earthquakes.
  • Many countries around the Pacific sit on the ring.

Words to know

Plate
A huge slab of Earth's crust.
Seismic
About earthquakes and shaking ground.
