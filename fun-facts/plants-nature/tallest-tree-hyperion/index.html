🌲 What is the tallest tree in the world?

Hyperion, a coast redwood in California, was measured at about 380 feet tall in 2019.

The big idea

The tallest living tree is Hyperion, a coast redwood about 380 feet tall.

How it works

🌲

Coast redwood

Hyperion is a Sequoia sempervirens, a coast redwood.

📏

Measured

It stood 116.07 meters tall when measured in 2019.

📍

California

It lives in Redwood National Park in California, USA.

More about nature

  • Coast redwoods are one of the tallest kinds of tree.
  • A tree keeps growing slowly, so its height can change over time.
  • Record trees are measured with tools such as lasers and climbing.

Words to know

Redwood
A giant evergreen tree that grows in California.
Record
The biggest or best measurement ever found.

🧪 Try it yourself

Step outside and pick a tall tree. Ask a grown-up to help you guess its height. Hyperion would be many times taller!
