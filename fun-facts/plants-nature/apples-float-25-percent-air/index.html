🍎 Why do apples float?

An apple is about 25 percent air, so it floats in water.

The big idea

An apple floats because about 25 percent of it is air.

How it works

🫧

Air inside

Apples are about 25 percent air.

🪶

Lighter

The air makes an apple less dense than water.

🍏

Many kinds

There are over 7,000 varieties of apples worldwide.
