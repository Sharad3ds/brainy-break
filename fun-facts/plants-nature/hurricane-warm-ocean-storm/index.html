🌀 How do hurricanes form?

Hurricanes form over warm tropical oceans of at least 80 degrees Fahrenheit.

The big idea

Hurricanes form over ocean water of at least 80 degrees Fahrenheit, with winds of 74 mph or more.

How it works

🌡️

Warm water

They typically form where the ocean is at least 80 degrees Fahrenheit.

☁️

Fuel

Evaporating warm water supplies moist air that fuels the storm.

💨

Spinning winds

It is a hurricane once winds reach 74 miles per hour.

More about nature

  • Hurricanes form in tropical regions near the equator.
  • Winds blowing across the ocean pull in more evaporated water.
  • They weaken when they move over cooler water or land.

Words to know

Hurricane
A huge spinning storm with strong winds.
Tropical
Near the warm middle of Earth.

🧪 Try it yourself

Stir a bowl of water with a spoon and watch it spin. A hurricane spins too, but with air and clouds!
