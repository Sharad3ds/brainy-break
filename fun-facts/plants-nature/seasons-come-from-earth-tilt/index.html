🍁 Why do we have seasons?

Seasons happen because Earth's axis is tilted about 23.45 degrees, not because of distance from the Sun.

The big idea

Seasons come from Earth's tilt of about 23.45 degrees, not from its distance from the Sun.

How it works

📐

Tilted axis

Earth's axis is tilted about 23.45 degrees.

☀️

Direct light

A hemisphere leaning toward the Sun gets more direct sunlight and summer.

🌍

Opposite

The two hemispheres have opposite seasons.

More about nature

  • The Sun stays lower in the sky in winter and higher in summer.
  • Earth is farthest from the Sun in July.
  • The tilt may come from a big impact early in Earth's history.

Words to know

Axis
The imaginary line Earth spins around.
Hemisphere
Half of the Earth, like north or south.
