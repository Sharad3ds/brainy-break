🔽 How deep is the deepest part of the ocean?

Challenger Deep in the Mariana Trench is about 10,935 meters, or 35,876 feet, deep.

The big idea

Challenger Deep is about 10,935 meters (35,876 feet) deep, deeper than Everest is tall.

How it works

📍

Mariana Trench

It lies in the Mariana Trench in the Pacific Ocean.

📏

Very deep

About 10,935 meters, or 35,876 feet.

🏔️

Deeper than tall

Everest's peak would still be under water if placed there.
