🍂 Can a whole forest be one tree?

Pando is a grove of quaking aspens in Utah that are clones of one original tree.

The big idea

Pando looks like a forest, but its stems are clones of one tree.

How it works

🧬

Same DNA

DNA analysis in 2008 confirmed it is one genetic individual.

🌿

Sprouts

New stems sprout from shared roots.

📏

Big

It covers more than 100 acres in Fishlake National Forest.

Quick stats

47,000stems, estimated 1976
100+ acrescovered
100-150years a stem lives
80,000-1Myears, age estimates conflict

More about nature

  • Experts disagree on its true age.
  • The original mother stem is probably long gone.
  • The stems stay connected and share food.

Words to know

Clone
An exact genetic copy of a living thing.
Aspen
A tree with leaves that flutter in the wind.

🧪 Try it yourself

Next time you see a patch of tiny trees sprouting around one big tree, ask a grown-up if they might be sprouts from the same roots!
