🌳 What is the biggest tree in the world?

General Sherman, a giant sequoia, is the largest tree by volume, about 275 feet tall.

The big idea

General Sherman is the largest tree in the world by volume, at about 52,500 cubic feet.

How it works

🌲

Giant sequoia

General Sherman is a giant sequoia in Sequoia National Park.

📏

Tall

It stands 274.9 feet, or 83.8 meters, above its base.

📦

Bulky

Its volume is estimated at 52,500 cubic feet.

More about nature

  • The tallest tree and the biggest tree are different trees.
  • Volume measures how much space the whole tree takes up.
  • Giant sequoias are some of the biggest living things.

Words to know

Volume
How much space something takes up.
Sequoia
A giant evergreen tree from California.
