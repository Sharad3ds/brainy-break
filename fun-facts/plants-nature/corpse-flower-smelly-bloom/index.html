💐 Why is the corpse flower so smelly?

The titan arum, or corpse flower, blooms rarely and smells terrible to attract beetles and flies.

The big idea

The corpse flower blooms only 24 to 36 hours, and smells awful to attract beetles and flies.

How it works

📏

Huge

In gardens it usually reaches 6 to 8 feet before the bloom opens.

🪰

Stinky helper

The odor attracts carrion beetles and flesh flies.

⏰

Rare

A plant from seed needs about ten years to bloom the first time.

More about nature

  • It may rebloom every three to seven years afterward.
  • The name corpse flower comes from its smell.
  • Its science name is Amorphophallus titanum.

Words to know

The giant plant nicknamed the corpse flower.

Ask a botanical garden near you whether a corpse flower is expected to bloom. Crowds line up to see it!
