🌊 What makes ocean waves?

Most ocean waves are made by wind blowing over the water.

The big idea

Most ocean waves are made by wind. Tides come from the Sun and Moon.

How it works

💨

Wind

Friction between wind and water makes surface waves.

🌙

Tides

The pull of the Sun and Moon makes tides.

🌋

Tsunamis

Underwater earthquakes, landslides and eruptions can make them.

Quick stats

More about nature

  • Tsunamis are not caused by tides.
  • They can happen at any tide.
  • Waves form a crest at the top as the wind keeps disturbing the surface.

Words to know

Crest
The top of a wave.
Tide
The rise and fall of the sea each day.

🧪 Try it yourself

Blow gently across a bowl of water with a straw. Watch the ripples grow, like waves made by wind!
