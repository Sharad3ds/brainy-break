🌼 Is a daisy really one flower?

A daisy head is a cluster of many tiny flowers called florets.

The big idea

A daisy head is a cluster of many tiny flowers, not just one.

How it works

🟡

Tiny florets

The head is made of many small individual florets.

⚪

Ray petals

A ring of ray florets around the edge each has one large petal.

👪

Big family

The daisy family has over 32,000 accepted species.

Quick stats

Manytiny flowers in one head
32,000+species in the family
1big petal on each ray floret
Ringof ray florets around the edge

More about nature

  • Sunflowers are in the daisy family too.
  • The daisy family is also called Asteraceae.
  • Looking closely at the middle shows lots of tiny parts.

Words to know

Floret
A tiny flower that is part of a bigger flower head.
Species
One kind of living thing.

🧪 Try it yourself

Look closely at a daisy or dandelion with a grown-up. Can you see the tiny parts in the middle?
