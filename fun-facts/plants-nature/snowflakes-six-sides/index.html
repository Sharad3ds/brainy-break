❄️ Why do snowflakes have six sides?

Ice has a six-sided crystal structure, and each snowflake grows its own pattern in the cloud.

The big idea

Snowflakes have six sides because of the way ice crystals are built.

How it works

🧊

Ice structure

Ice has a six-sided crystal structure.

🌿

Branches

Vapor sticks to bumps, which then grow into branches.

🎨

Unique

Tiny changes in temperature and moisture shape each flake differently.

Quick stats

6sides or arms
1tiny particle at the start
Manychanges on the way down
Rarelytwo exactly alike

More about nature

  • A snowflake can switch between flat faces and branches as it grows.
  • The shape depends on the exact path it takes.
  • It is astronomically unlikely that two flakes match perfectly.

Words to know

Crystal
A solid with its parts lined up in a pattern.
Symmetry
When the same shape repeats evenly.

🧪 Try it yourself

Catch a snowflake on dark mittens on a snowy day. Count the arms!
