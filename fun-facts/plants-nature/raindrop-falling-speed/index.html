🌧️ How fast do raindrops fall?

The biggest raindrops fall at about 10 meters per second, around 22 miles per hour.

The big idea

The biggest raindrops fall at about 10 meters per second, roughly 22 miles per hour.

How it works

📏

Size matters

In still air a raindrop falls faster the bigger it is.

⏱️

Long trip

A large drop from 4,000 meters needs at least about seven minutes.

💨

Updrafts

Strong rising wind can stop some drops from falling.

More about nature

  • A raindrop reaches a steady top speed called terminal velocity.
  • Wind changes how fast and where drops land.
  • Small drops fall much more slowly than big ones.

Words to know

Terminal speed
The steady top speed something reaches as it falls.
Updraft
Air moving upward.

Next rainy day, watch drops hit a puddle from a window. Can you spot big drops and small ones?
