🌵 How does a saguaro cactus store water?

A saguaro swells up with water after rain and can live for 150 years or more.

The big idea

A saguaro stores water in its pleated trunk and can live for 150 years or more.

How it works

💧

Water tank

Most of the plant is water.

🪗

Pleats

They expand after rain and shrink during dry times.

🐢

Slow

A young saguaro grows only a few inches in its first ten years.

More about nature

  • Flowers open at night and close the next afternoon.
  • In drier areas, arms can take up to 100 years to appear.
  • Some saguaros may live beyond 200 years.

Words to know

Pleat
A fold, like in an accordion.
Desert
A very dry place with little rain.

🧪 Try it yourself

Soak a dry sponge in water and watch it swell up. A saguaro swells in a similar way, but with its pleated trunk!
