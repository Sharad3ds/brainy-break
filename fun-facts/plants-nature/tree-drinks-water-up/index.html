💧 How does a tall tree drink water?

Water evaporating from leaves pulls water all the way up from the roots.

The big idea

Tall trees have no pump. Evaporation from the leaves pulls water up from the roots.

How it works

🍃

Leaf pull

Water evaporates from stomata and pulls more water up.

🔗

Sticky water

Water molecules cling together, so the column of water stays connected.

🧪

Xylem tubes

Narrow, hollow tubes carry the water up.

Quick stats

More about nature

  • This process is called transpiration.
  • Root pressure only lifts water a few meters.
  • Sunshine helps drive the whole system.

Words to know

Xylem
Tiny tubes in a plant that carry water upward.
Evaporate
To change from liquid water into a gas.

🧪 Try it yourself

Put a stalk of celery in water with a little food coloring. Watch the color climb up the stalk!
