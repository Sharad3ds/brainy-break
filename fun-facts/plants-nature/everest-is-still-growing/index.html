⬆️ Is Mount Everest still growing?

The big idea

Everest rises about 1 millimeter a year, estimated, as two plates keep pushing together.

How it works

🧩

Plates push

The Indian and Eurasian plates are still colliding.

🌬️

Erosion

Wind and water wear the mountain down.

🌍

Quakes

Sudden slips can make a peak grow or shrink a little.

Quick stats

More about nature

  • Scientists estimate northwestern parts of the range rise about 10 millimeters a year.
  • The 2015 Nepal earthquake may have changed the height slightly.
  • Rivers carry eroded sediment away.

Words to know

Plate tectonics
The slow movement of Earth's big crust slabs.
Erosion
Wearing away of rock by wind and water.

🧪 Try it yourself

Push the edges of a rug toward each other on a table. See the middle rise? That is like a plate collision!
