🔢 How far away is the lightning?

Count the seconds between flash and thunder, then divide by 5 to get the distance in miles.

The big idea

Count the seconds from flash to thunder and divide by 5 for the distance in miles.

How it works

⏱️

Count

Start when you see the flash, stop when you hear the thunder.

➗

Divide

Divide the seconds by 5 to estimate miles.

🏠

Stay safe

If you can hear thunder, you are close enough to be struck.

More about nature

  • Light reaches you much faster than sound does.
  • It is only an estimate, not exact.
  • Wait inside well after the last thunder before going out.

Words to know

Estimate
A close guess.
Mile
A distance of about 1.6 kilometers.

🧪 Try it yourself

Next storm, count from the flash to the boom with a grown-up and divide by five. How far was it?
