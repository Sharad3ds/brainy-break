🪰 Why do some plants eat bugs?

Carnivorous plants live in poor soil and catch bugs to get nutrients like nitrogen.

The big idea

There are over 600 kinds of carnivorous plants, catching bugs for nutrients such as nitrogen.

How it works

🌱

Poor soil

They live in nutrient-poor places such as bogs.

🪰

Bugs as vitamins

Prey gives them nitrogen and other nutrients.

🪤

Many traps

Pitfalls, sticky leaves and snap traps all exist.

More about nature

  • Some big pitcher plants can even digest frogs or rodents.
  • Most carnivorous plants catch insects and other small animals.
  • Butterworts have sticky leaves like sundews.

Words to know

Carnivorous
Eating animals.
Nitrogen
A nutrient plants need to grow.
