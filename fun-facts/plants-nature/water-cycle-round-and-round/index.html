♻️ What is the water cycle?

Water evaporates, condenses into clouds, falls as rain or snow, and flows back to the oceans.

The big idea

Water moves in a cycle: evaporation, condensation, precipitation, and back to the sea.

How it works

☀️

Evaporation

Liquid water changes into water vapor.

☁️

Condensation

Cooling air turns vapor back into droplets.

🌧️

Precipitation

Water falls back to Earth as rain or snow.

More about nature

  • Most water that evaporates from the ocean falls back into the ocean.
  • Only about 10 percent of it is carried over land.
  • Runoff and groundwater return water to the oceans.

Words to know

Evaporation
Liquid water turning into vapor.
Precipitation
Rain, snow, sleet or hail falling from clouds.

🧪 Try it yourself

Put a little water in a clear covered cup in the sun. Watch drops form on the lid and fall back down!
