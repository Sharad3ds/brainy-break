🍅 Is a tomato a fruit?

Botanically, a fruit grows from a flower and holds seeds, so a tomato is a fruit.

The big idea

A tomato is a fruit to a plant scientist because it grows from a flower and has seeds.

How it works

🌸

From a flower

A botanical fruit comes from the flower's ripened ovary.

🌱

Seeds

Fruits hold the seeds.

🍳

Cooks differ

Many botanical fruits are called vegetables in cooking.

Quick stats

1flower makes each fruit
Seedsinside every fruit
5+veggie-style fruits listed
2ways to see it: science and cooking

More about nature

  • Maple helicopters and dandelion puffs count as fruits too.
  • Cucumbers and squash are botanical fruits as well.
  • Cooks group foods by taste and use, not by plant parts.

Words to know

Ovary
The part of a flower that grows into a fruit.
Botanical
About plants and plant science.
