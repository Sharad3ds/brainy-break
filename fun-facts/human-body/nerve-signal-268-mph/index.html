⚡ How fast do signals travel along your nerves?

The fastest nerve signals in your body travel at up to 268 miles per hour.

The big idea

Nerve signals can travel up to 268 mph. Your brain has at least 100 trillion synapses.

How it works

🧈

Myelin

A coating that speeds up signals.

🐌

No myelin

Skin sensors without it send signals at about 1 mph.

🌌

Synapses

At least 1,000 times the stars in our galaxy.

More about the human body

  • UCLA researchers found myelin coating peaks around age 39.
  • About 2 billion nerve endings sit in the outermost skin layer.
  • A fingertip can detect grooves as shallow as 50 nanometers.

Words to know

Myelin
A fatty coating around nerve fibers.
Synapse
A tiny gap where one neuron talks to the next.
