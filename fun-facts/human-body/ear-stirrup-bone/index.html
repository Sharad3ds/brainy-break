👂 Where is the smallest bone in your body?

The stapes, shaped like a stirrup, is the smallest bone in your body, and it lives in your ear.

The big idea

The stapes is the smallest bone in your body. The three ear bones together are about the size of an orange seed.

How it works

🔨

The malleus is Latin for hammer.

More about the human body

  • The bones pass vibrations from the eardrum along to the cochlea.
  • The cochlea turns vibrations into neural signals for the brain.
  • The bones sit in the middle ear.

Words to know

Cochlea
A snail-shaped part of the inner ear.
Eardrum
A thin skin that vibrates with sound.

🧪 Try it yourself

Say a loud humming sound with your fingers lightly on your throat. Vibrations make sound, and your tiny ear bones pass them on!
