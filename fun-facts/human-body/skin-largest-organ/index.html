🧤 What is your body's largest organ?

Your skin covers about 22 square feet and does many jobs, from protection to making vitamin D.

The big idea

Skin covers about 22 square feet and has 3 layers. It protects you, controls temperature and makes vitamin D.

How it works

🧱

Three layers

Epidermis, dermis and hypodermis.

🌡️

Temperature

Sweat and blood vessels help control heat.

☀️

Vitamin D

Skin makes it when exposed to sunlight.

More about the human body

  • Skin is thicker on palms and soles and thinner on the eyelids.
  • Nerve receptors detect pain, touch, pressure, itch and temperature.
  • Lymphatic vessels in skin help fight infection.

Words to know

Epidermis
The outer layer of skin.
Dermis
The thick middle layer of skin.

🧪 Try it yourself

Look at your palm and the back of your hand. Which skin looks thicker? Palms have thick skin with extra layers!
