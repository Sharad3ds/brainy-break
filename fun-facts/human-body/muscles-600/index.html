💪 How many muscles do you have?

You have more than 600 muscles, and some work without you even thinking.

The big idea

You have more than 600 muscles of 3 types: skeletal, cardiac and smooth.

How it works

🏃

Skeletal

Voluntary muscles attached to bones by tendons.

🫀

Cardiac

Involuntary heart muscle found nowhere else.

🌀

Smooth

Involuntary muscle that lines some organs.

Quick stats

600+muscles
3types
1place for cardiac muscle: the heart
1000sof tiny fibers in each muscle

More about the human body

  • Muscles are made of thousands of small fibers that stretch and contract.
  • Back muscles work slowly to maintain posture.
  • Smooth muscle helps the lungs expand during breathing.

Words to know

Tendon
A tough cord joining muscle to bone.
Voluntary
Something you control on purpose.

🧪 Try it yourself

Try to wiggle your toes, then try to tell your heart to stop beating. Skeletal muscles obey you; cardiac muscle works on its own!
