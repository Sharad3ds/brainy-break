💧 How much blood do your kidneys filter?

Healthy kidneys filter about 150 quarts of blood a day, but only 1 to 2 quarts become urine.

The big idea

Healthy kidneys filter about 150 quarts of blood a day, but only 1 to 2 quarts become urine.

How it works

🧹

Waste removal

They remove wastes, extra fluid and acid from the body.

⚖️

Balance

They balance water, sodium, calcium, phosphorus and potassium.

🩸

Other jobs

They help control blood pressure and help make red blood cells.

More about the human body

  • Kidneys also help keep bones strong.
  • Ureters carry urine from each kidney to the bladder, which stores it.
  • Each nephron has a filter called a glomerulus and a tube called a tubule.

Words to know

Nephron
A tiny filter unit inside a kidney.
Urine
Liquid waste your body gets rid of.

🧪 Try it yourself

Make a fist. That is about how big one kidney is. Now find the place just below your ribs, near your back. That is where they sit.
