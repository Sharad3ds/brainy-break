🔄 Does your skin replace itself?

New skin cells take about a month to reach the surface, and thousands of old ones fall off every hour.

The big idea

Skin renews about every 28 days. Estimates say 30,000–40,000 dead cells fall off every hour.

How it works

⬆️

Rising cells

New cells travel up from the bottom of the epidermis.

🧹

Shedding

Dead cells at the top are shed daily.

🔢

Estimates

Numbers are rough estimates, and vary by source.

More about the human body

  • One source says household dust contains many dead skin cells.
  • The figures vary between sources, so treat them as estimates.
  • The top layer of the epidermis is made of dead cells.

Words to know

Epidermis
The outer layer of skin.
Shed
To fall off.

🧪 Try it yourself

Rub a dark cloth on your arm and look at the fine dust. A tiny part is skin that is being replaced by new skin!
