🌡️ What is a fever?

A fever is a sign that your immune system is working, usually at 100.4 degrees Fahrenheit or higher.

The big idea

A fever is a symptom, not an illness. For an oral reading it is most commonly counted as 100.4°F (38°C) or higher.

How it works

🚨

A signal

A fever shows that the immune system has been activated.

🕐

Daily rhythm

Body temperature is usually lower in the morning and higher in the evening.

🧒

Kids run warm

Infants and young children normally run slightly warmer.

Quick stats

98.6°Fcommon normal
37°Ccommon normal
100.4°Fcommon fever line
1°Fdifference between thermometer spots, about

More about the human body

  • Rectal and ear readings run about 1 degree Fahrenheit higher than oral readings.
  • Forehead readings run about 1 degree Fahrenheit lower.
  • Vaccines, medicines and many other things can also cause a fever.

Words to know

Symptom
A sign that something is going on in the body.
Fever
A body temperature higher than usual.

🧪 Try it yourself

Ask a grown-up to take your temperature in the morning and again in the evening. Is there a small difference?
