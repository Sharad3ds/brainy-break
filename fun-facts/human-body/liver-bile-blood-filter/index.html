🟢 What does the liver do all day?

Your liver makes about 700 to 1,000 milliliters of bile every day and holds about 13 percent of your blood.

The big idea

More about the human body

  • One pint is roughly 13 percent of your total blood.
  • Your body needs about one gram of liver for each kilogram of body weight.
  • Bile from the liver is sent to help digest food in the small intestine.

Words to know

Bile
A liquid that helps break down fats.
Process
A set of steps that does a job.
