🙃 Do your eyes see the world upside down?

The image on your retina is upside down, and goggle experiments showed the brain can adapt.

The big idea

The picture on your retina is upside down. In goggle tests, people adapted by about the fifth day.

How it works

🔦

Focus

The cornea, pupil and lens focus light onto the retina.

🙃

Flipped image

The lens makes the retinal image upside down.

🧠

Adapting

The brain can adapt to changed visual input.

More about the human body

  • Vision scientists say the brain does not need to flip the image back.
  • Perception depends on how neurons fire patterns about the scene and your body.
  • Newer studies look at which brain areas help us adapt.

Words to know

Retina
The light-sensing layer at the back of the eye.
Lens
The clear part of the eye that focuses light.

🧪 Try it yourself

Hold a spoon at arm's length and look at your reflection in the bowl. You will see yourself upside down!
