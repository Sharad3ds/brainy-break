💅 How fast do your hair and nails grow?

Scalp hair grows about 1 centimeter a month on average, and fingernails grow just over 3 millimeters.

The big idea

Scalp hair grows about 1 centimeter a month and fingernails about 3 millimeters a month, on average.

How it works

🧬

Genes

Genetics is the biggest factor in how fast hair and nails grow.

🧒

Age

Younger people generally grow hair and nails faster.

🥗

Food

Minerals like iron and zinc help keep hair and nails strong.

Quick stats

More about the human body

  • Nail growth is often similar among siblings, especially identical twins.
  • Pregnancy often speeds growth, while some hormones can slow it.
  • Hair cycles through growth, rest and shedding.

Words to know

Genetics
Traits passed down from your family.
Shedding
When hair falls out naturally.
