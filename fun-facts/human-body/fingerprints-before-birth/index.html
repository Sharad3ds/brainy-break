👆 When do your fingerprints form?

Fingerprints begin forming before you are born, and three molecules help shape the ridges.

The big idea

Fingerprints form before birth. Ridges spread from 3 starting points and make an arch, loop or whorl.

How it works

🌱

Early start

Ridges begin as tiny trenches in the skin before birth.

🌀

Patterns

Arch, loop or whorl.

🧪

Molecules

WNT, EDAR and BMP interact to shape ridges.

Quick stats

More about the human body

  • The study was published in the journal Cell in 2023.
  • In mice, more EDAR made wider, more spaced-out ridges.
  • Each person's fingerprints are unique and last a lifetime.

Words to know

Whorl
A swirl pattern.
Ridge
A raised line on the skin.

🧪 Try it yourself

Press your finger on an ink pad or a pencil scribble, then onto paper. Is yours an arch, a loop or a whorl?
