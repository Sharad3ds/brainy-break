👶 Do babies have more bones than adults?

A newborn has 275 to 300 bones, but an adult has only 206, because many bones join together.

The big idea

A newborn has 275–300 bones. An adult has 206, because many join together as we grow.

How it works

🦴

Fusing

Many small bones join into larger ones as the child grows.

🧱

Cartilage

It hardens into bone through ossification.

🍼

Soft spots

Gaps between skull bones close in the first year or two.

More about the human body

  • Bone formation starts around eight weeks after conception.
  • Fusion continues until puberty or shortly after.
  • Five major skull bones let the head change shape during birth.

Words to know

Cartilage
A tough, flexible tissue that can harden into bone.
Fontanelle
A soft spot between a baby's skull bones.

🧪 Try it yourself

Feel your elbow, knee and nose. Some parts are bendy, some hard. Cartilage is the bendy stuff babies have lots of!
