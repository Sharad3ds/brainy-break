✨ What is the hardest substance in your body?

Tooth enamel is the hardest substance in your body, but once it is lost, it does not grow back.

The big idea

Enamel is the hardest substance in your body, 95% minerals. It does not grow back, so protect it!

How it works

💎

Super hard

Harder than bone.

🧪

Minerals

Calcium and phosphorus make up 95% of enamel.

🍬

Sugar and acid

Bacteria turn sugar into acids that wear enamel down.

Quick stats

95%calcium and phosphorus
4%water
1%protein
0regrowth

More about the human body

  • Fluoride treatments can help repair lightly damaged enamel.
  • Saliva washes away acid and bacteria.
  • Grinding teeth can wear enamel down.

Words to know

Enamel
The hard outer layer of a tooth.
Fluoride
A mineral that helps strengthen teeth.

🧪 Try it yourself

Brush your teeth for two full minutes, morning and night. You are protecting the hardest thing in your body!
