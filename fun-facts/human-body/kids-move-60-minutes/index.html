🏃 How much should kids move each day?

Kids aged 6 to 17 should get at least 60 minutes of moderate-to-vigorous activity every day.

The big idea

Kids ages 6 to 17 should get at least 60 minutes of moderate-to-vigorous physical activity every day.

How it works

🏃

Aerobic

Most of the 60 minutes should be aerobic activity.

💪

Strong muscles

Do muscle-strengthening activities on at least 3 days a week.

🦴

Strong bones

Jumping and running help strengthen bones on at least 3 days a week.

Quick stats

More about the human body

  • Moderate activity includes things like brisk walking or riding a bike.
  • Vigorous activity makes you breathe hard, such as running.
  • Jumping rope is a bone-strengthening activity.

Words to know

Aerobic
Activity that makes your heart and lungs work harder.
Vigorous
Hard, energetic effort.

🧪 Try it yourself

Make a 60-minute plan: a walk, a bike ride, some jumping jacks and a game of tag. They all count!
