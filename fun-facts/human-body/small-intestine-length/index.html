🧵 How long is your small intestine?

Your small intestine is about 20 to 25 feet long in adults, and it absorbs more than 95 percent of nutrients.

The big idea

The small intestine is about 20 to 25 feet long and has a surface of about 2,700 square feet, so it can absorb more than 95 percent of nutrients.

How it works

🧵

Long tube

It is about 20 to 25 feet long but only about 1 inch wide.

🪜

Folds and villi

Folds and tiny villi add a huge surface for absorbing food.

🩸

Into the blood

The jejunum and ileum absorb nutrients into the bloodstream.

Quick stats

More about the human body

  • The first part, the duodenum, is about one fifth of the length.
  • The jejunum is about two fifths of the length.
  • Bile and enzymes from the liver, pancreas and gallbladder help in the duodenum.

Words to know

Villi
Tiny finger-like bumps that absorb food.
Absorb
To soak up and take into the body.

🧪 Try it yourself

Stretch out a long rope or a few bath towels tied together. Your small intestine is about as long as a small room is wide!
