🦵 Which is the longest and strongest bone in your body?

The femur, your thigh bone, is about 18 inches long in most adults and can support up to 30 times your body weight.

The big idea

The femur is your longest and strongest bone: about 18 inches long and able to support up to 30 times your body weight.

More about the human body

  • The femur runs from the hip to the knee.
  • It is the body's largest bone.
  • Strong thigh bones help you stand, walk, run and jump.
