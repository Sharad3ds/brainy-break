⏳ How long does food take to digest?

Food usually takes about two days to travel through your digestive system, and the colon is the slowest part.

The big idea

Food usually takes about two days to travel through the digestive system, with the large intestine taking the longest.

How it works

⏱️

Slow colon

The large intestine can take 6 to 8 times longer than the stomach and small intestine.

🍽️

Food matters

The kind and amount of food you eat changes the time.

🧍

Personal speed

Digestion time differs from person to person.

More about the human body

  • The stomach and small intestine together take about 6 hours on average.
  • Food and drink are broken down along the way as well as being moved.
  • Everybody's digestive system works at its own speed.

Words to know

Digest
To break food into tiny pieces your body can use.
Colon
Another name for the large intestine.

🧪 Try it yourself

Eat breakfast, then check a clock. Your food is still traveling about two days later!
