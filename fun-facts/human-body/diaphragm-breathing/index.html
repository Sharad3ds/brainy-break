🌬️ What muscle helps you breathe?

Your diaphragm is a dome-shaped muscle that flattens to pull air into your lungs.

The big idea

Breathe in and your diaphragm flattens, creating suction. Breathe out and it rises back into a dome.

How it works

🌙

Dome muscle

A thin, dome-shaped muscle under the lungs.

⬇️

In

It contracts and flattens, so the chest expands.

⬆️

Out

It relaxes and rises, so air is pushed out.

Quick stats

More about the human body

  • The diaphragm attaches to the sternum, the lower rib cage and the spine.
  • It separates the chest from the abdomen.
  • Hiccups that will not stop can signal a problem with the diaphragm.

Words to know

Diaphragm
The big breathing muscle under the lungs.
Contract
To tighten a muscle.

🧪 Try it yourself

Put a hand on your belly and breathe in slowly. Feel it push out as the diaphragm moves down!
