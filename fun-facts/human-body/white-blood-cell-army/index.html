🛡️ How do white blood cells protect you?

Your body makes nearly 100 billion white blood cells every day, in five kinds.

The big idea

Your body makes nearly 100 billion white blood cells every day, in 5 kinds. They are about 1% of your blood.

How it works

🦠

Germ fighters

Neutrophils kill bacteria and fungi.

🧬

Antibody makers

Lymphocytes fight viruses and make antibodies.

🧹

Cleaners

Monocytes clear away damaged cells.

Quick stats

5kinds
100 billionmade a day
1%of blood
4,000–11,000per microliter, normal

More about the human body

  • Eosinophils help destroy parasites and cancer cells.
  • Basophils trigger allergy symptoms like sneezing.
  • B cells develop in the lymph nodes and spleen.

Words to know

Antibody
A protein that helps fight germs.
Bacteria
Tiny living things, some of which make you sick.

🧪 Try it yourself

Wash your hands with soap for 20 seconds. That helps your white blood cells by keeping germs out in the first place!
