👃 How many smells can your nose tell apart?

A 2014 study estimated that people can tell apart more than a trillion different odor mixtures.

The big idea

A 2014 study estimated people can tell apart more than 1 trillion odor mixtures. It is a calculated minimum from 26 people.

How it works

🧪

The test

Three vials, two the same, pick the odd one.

👃

Smell genes

About 400 human genes code for odor-sensing molecules.

⚠️

A caution

The number is calculated from a small group of 26 people.

Quick stats

1 trillion+mixtures, calculated

More about the human body

  • Mice have about 1,000 such genes and flies about 50.
  • Odors that overlapped by more than 90% could not be told apart.
  • The researchers think humans may tell apart far more than they tested.

Words to know

Odor molecule
A tiny particle that makes a smell.
Gene
A set of instructions in your cells.

🧪 Try it yourself

Close your eyes and ask a friend to hold different foods under your nose, like orange, mint and cinnamon. Can you name them all?
