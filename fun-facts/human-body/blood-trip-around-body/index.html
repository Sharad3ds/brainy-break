🗺️ What path does blood take around your body?

Blood travels from the heart through arteries, capillaries and veins, then back again.

The big idea

Blood leaves the heart through the aorta, swaps oxygen in capillaries, and returns through veins to the heart.

How it works

➡️

Out

Arteries carry oxygen-rich blood to the tissues.

🔄

Swap

Capillaries pass oxygen and nutrients to cells and take waste away.

⬅️

Back

Veins carry oxygen-poor blood to the heart.

Quick stats

More about the human body

  • Blood flows continuously through the vessels.
  • It carries oxygen and nutrients to tissues and removes carbon dioxide and waste.
  • Veins grow larger as they near the heart.

Words to know

Aorta
The largest artery, leaving the heart.
Vena cava
A large vein that returns blood to the heart.

🧪 Try it yourself

Trace the path on a drawing: heart, aorta, arteries, capillaries, veins, vena cava, heart. Use red for out and blue for back!
