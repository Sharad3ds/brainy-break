👅 Does each part of your tongue taste something different?

The tongue map you may have seen is a myth: taste buds for all tastes are spread across your tongue.

The big idea

The tongue map is a myth. Taste buds across the tongue sense 5 tastes: sweet, sour, salty, bitter and savory.

How it works

🗺️

Myth

The old tip, sides and back map is wrong.

5️⃣

Five tastes

Sweet, sour, salty, bitter and savory (umami).

🟠

Beyond the tongue

More about the human body

  • Bitter-sensitive buds are concentrated at the back of the tongue.
  • More taste buds sit along the sides of the tongue than in the center.
  • Taste buds also appear on the soft palate and in the throat.

Words to know

Taste bud
A tiny group of cells that senses taste.
Umami
A savory taste, like in broth or cheese.

🧪 Try it yourself

Put a tiny bit of sugar on different parts of your tongue. Can you taste it everywhere? Try with a pinch of salt too!
