🔴 Why are red blood cells shaped like doughnuts?

Red blood cells are flat, doughnut-like disks that live about 120 days and carry oxygen.

The big idea

Red blood cells live about 120 days, are made in bone marrow, and make up about 40% of your blood.

How it works

🍩

Shape

A flat disk with a dent in the middle.

🫁

Oxygen carrier

Hemoglobin holds the oxygen and gives the red color.

🦴

Made in bones

Bone marrow makes them.

Quick stats

120days of life
40%of blood volume
7days to mature
4.2–6.1 Mper microliter, adults

More about the human body

  • Adult males usually have 4.7 to 6.1 million red cells per microliter of blood.
  • Adult females usually have 4.2 to 5.4 million per microliter.
  • Red cells have no nucleus, so they run out of energy as they travel.

Words to know

Hemoglobin
The protein in red blood cells that carries oxygen.
Bone marrow
Soft tissue inside bones where blood cells are made.

🧪 Try it yourself

Make a doughnut shape with clay or playdough, then press the middle without making a hole. That is a red blood cell!
