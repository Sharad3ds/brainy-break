The big idea

Your heart beats about 100,000 times a day and more than 2.6 billion times in an average lifetime.

How it works

🫀

A busy pump

It pumps nearly 1.5 gallons of blood each minute.

🥄

Each beat

About 4 tablespoons of blood leave the heart with every beat.

🐋

Big hearts

A blue whale's heart weighs more than 1,000 pounds.

More about the human body

  • A grown-up heart is about the size of two clasped hands, and a child's is about the size of one fist.
  • A typical heart valve is about the size of a half dollar.
  • The left lung is slightly smaller than the right to make room for the heart.

Words to know

Pump
A machine that pushes liquid along.
Valve
A flap that lets blood flow one way.

🧪 Try it yourself

Make a fist. A child's heart is about that big! Now squeeze it once a second for one minute. Your heart never gets to rest like that.
