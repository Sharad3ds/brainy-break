💀 How many bones make up your skull?

An adult skull has 22 bones, and only the lower jaw can move.

The big idea

An adult skull has 22 bones: 8 in the brain case and 14 in the face. Only the lower jaw moves.

How it works

🧠

Brain case

Eight cranial bones protect the brain.

😀

Face bones

Fourteen bones form the jaws, nose and eye sockets.

🧩

Sutures

Interlocking joints add strength.

Quick stats

22skull bones
8cranial bones
14facial bones
1movable bone: the mandible

More about the human body

  • Facial bones enclose the eyeballs and support the teeth.
  • Paranasal sinuses reduce skull weight and add resonance to the voice.
  • A newborn's skull has five main bones and soft spots that close with time.

Words to know

Cranium
The bones that protect your brain.
Suture
A joint between two skull bones.

🧪 Try it yourself

Put your fingers on your jaw and open and close your mouth. That is the only moving bone in your skull!
