🩹 What is a scab and how does it help you heal?

A scab is a temporary crust that protects a wound while new skin grows underneath.

The big idea

A scab is a temporary cover. Under it, your body repairs blood vessels and makes collagen. A new scar starts at about 80–90% of normal strength.

How it works

🔴

Inflammation

The area may be red, swollen and sore for a few days.

🧱

Scab

A dry crust protects the wound.

🧵

Collagen

Skin makes it to join the broken tissue.

More about the human body

  • Keeping the wound clean and dry helps healing.
  • The scar takes a few months to reach full strength.
  • Once the scab dries out, it falls off by itself.

Words to know

Collagen
A strong protein that holds skin together.
Inflammation
Redness and swelling as the body responds to injury.
