🍋 Why does stomach acid not melt your stomach?

Stomach acid can be about 10 times as sour as pure lemon juice, yet a mucus coat protects the stomach.

The big idea

Stomach acid can reach a pH of one or lower, roughly 10 times as acidic as pure lemon juice, and a layer of mucus protects the stomach wall.

How it works

🧪

Acid cells

Parietal cells in the stomach wall release hydrochloric acid.

🛡️

Mucus coat

Goblet cells make mucus that traps substances to neutralize acid that seeps in.

🔑

Safe enzyme

Pepsin is made in an inactive form and switched on only inside the stomach.

More about the human body

  • Pancreas juices help neutralize stomach contents when they enter the small intestine.
  • A germ called H. pylori can start sores in the stomach lining, which doctors call ulcers.
  • The stomach acid also helps to start breaking down food.

Words to know

Acid
A sour chemical that can break things down.
Mucus
A slippery, sticky coat that protects surfaces.

🧪 Try it yourself

Taste a little lemon juice, then imagine something 10 times sourer. That is how strong stomach acid can get. Thank your mucus!
