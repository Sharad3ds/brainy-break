🌱 Can your liver grow back?

Your liver can regrow from as little as 25 percent of its original tissue.

The big idea

The liver can regrow from as little as 25 percent of its tissue, and may return to full size in nearly two weeks after a donation.

More about the human body

  • A liver holds about one pint of blood, roughly 13 percent of your total blood.
  • It runs hundreds of body processes.
