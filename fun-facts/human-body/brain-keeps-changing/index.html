🌱 Can your brain really change as you learn?

Neuroplasticity means your brain builds new connections and trims old ones as you learn.

The big idea

Neuroplasticity means neurons build new connections or trim unused ones. Your brain keeps adapting, even as an adult.

How it works

🔗

New links

Neurons build new synapses as you learn.

✂️

Trimming

Unused connections can be pruned back.

🩹

Recovery

After injury, the brain first reroutes information, then forms new connections.

Quick stats

2big change periods: childhood and teens
Daysto start forming new links after injury
Weeks+for the process to finish
3helpers: music, food, exercise

More about the human body

  • After an injury, the brain first reroutes information using existing connections.
  • The hippocampus, which helps memory, begins to shrink in early adulthood.
  • Scientists once thought the adult brain stopped changing.

Words to know

Neuroplasticity
The brain's ability to change.
Hippocampus
A brain area that helps form memories.

🧪 Try it yourself

Learn something new this week, like a short song on a recorder or a few words in a new language. You are building new connections!
