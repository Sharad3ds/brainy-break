😴 Does your brain clean itself while you sleep?

A brain network called the glymphatic system flushes out waste, and it is most active in deep sleep.

The big idea

The glymphatic system flushes waste from the brain and is most active in deep sleep, when brain cells shrink slightly to open space.

How it works

🚿

Fluid flush

Cerebrospinal fluid washes waste proteins out.

🌙

Deep sleep

It is most active in deep, non-REM sleep.

📉

Can slow down

Disrupted sleep, aging and high blood pressure reduce it.

More about the human body

  • Waste proteins like beta-amyloid and tau are linked to diseases such as Alzheimer's.
  • The study of zolpidem was done in mice.
  • Brain waves, blood flow and fluid movement work together.

Words to know

Glymphatic system
The brain's waste-clearing network.
Cerebrospinal fluid
The clear fluid that surrounds the brain.

🧪 Try it yourself

Tonight, go to bed at a regular time and let your brain do its cleaning. Notice how you feel in the morning!
