🦾 Why do muscles work in pairs?

Muscles can only pull, never push, so they work in pairs like the biceps and triceps.

The big idea

Muscles pull but cannot push, so they work in pairs: the biceps bends your elbow and the triceps straightens it.

How it works

🪢

Pull only

A muscle cannot push a bone back.

💪

Flexor

It contracts to bend a joint.

🔁

Extensor

It contracts to straighten a joint.

Quick stats

2muscles in a pair
1job each: pull
2steps: contract then relax
2arm example: biceps and triceps

More about the human body

  • The right side of the brain controls the left side of the body, and vice versa.
  • The cerebellum helps coordinate smooth movement.
  • Sensors in muscles and joints send feedback to the brain.

Words to know

Flexor
A muscle that bends a joint.
Extensor
A muscle that straightens a joint.

🧪 Try it yourself

Put a hand on your upper arm. Bend your elbow and feel the front muscle, the biceps, tighten. Straighten it and feel the back one, the triceps!
