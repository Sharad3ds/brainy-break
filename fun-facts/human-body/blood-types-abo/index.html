🩸 What are the blood types?

There are four main blood types, A, B, AB and O, and O negative is the universal red cell donor.

The big idea

There are 8 common blood types. O negative is the universal red cell donor, and O positive is the most common at about 37%.

How it works

🔤

Four groups

A, B, AB and O depend on antigens on red blood cells.

➕

Rh factor

Present (+) or absent (−) gives 8 common types.

🧬

Inherited

Blood type is passed down from your parents.

Quick stats

4main groups
8common types
37%are O positive
7%are O negative

More about the human body

  • Rare blood types lack an antigen that 99% of people have.
  • More than 300 other antigens are known.
  • Type O is more common in some groups than others, from about 45% to 57%.

Words to know

Antigen
A marker on a cell that the body can recognize.
Plasma
The liquid part of blood.

🧪 Try it yourself

Ask a grown-up if they know their blood type. Does it match yours? Blood types are passed down in families!
