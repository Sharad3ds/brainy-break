🛣 How long are all the blood vessels in your body?

Laid end to end, your blood vessels would stretch over 60,000 miles, enough to circle the globe more than twice.

The big idea

Your blood vessels add up to over 60,000 miles, enough to circle the globe more than twice.

How it works

🔴

Arteries

They carry oxygen-rich blood away from the heart.

🔬

Capillaries

The smallest vessels, with thin walls where things pass to cells.

🔵

Veins

They carry oxygen-poor blood back to the heart.

Quick stats

60,000+ mitotal length
2+times around Earth
1cell thick capillary walls
2big veins: superior and inferior vena cava

More about the human body

  • The superior vena cava returns blood from the head and arms.
  • The inferior vena cava returns blood from the abdomen and legs.
  • Capillary walls are one cell thick.

Words to know

Artery
A vessel that carries blood away from the heart.
Capillary
A tiny vessel that connects arteries and veins.

🧪 Try it yourself

Look at the inside of your wrist. Those blue lines are veins close to the skin. Imagine them joined up into a road 60,000 miles long!
