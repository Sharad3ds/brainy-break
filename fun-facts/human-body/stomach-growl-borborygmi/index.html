🔔 Why does your tummy growl?

A growling tummy is a normal part of digestion, and it is called borborygmi.

The big idea

A growling tummy is a normal part of digestion: muscles squeeze gas, food and liquid along, and the sound is called borborygmi.

How it works

🌊

Squeeze waves

Peristalsis pushes gas, food and liquid through your digestive tract.

🔇

Quiet when full

Food and liquid muffle the sounds, so an empty tract is noisier.

🍽️

Hunger hormone

Ghrelin signals hunger and also stimulates the squeezing.

Quick stats

3waves a minute in the stomach after a meal
12waves a minute in the small intestine after a meal
20 minbetween squeezes, empty tummy
1fancy name: borborygmi

More about the human body

  • Beans, plant foods and dairy can make extra gas, which can make the sounds louder.
  • Peristalsis happens whether you are hungry or full.
  • Empty stomach squeezes are slower, about once every 20 minutes.

Words to know

Peristalsis
Muscle waves that push food along.
Hormone
A chemical messenger inside your body.

🧪 Try it yourself

Next time you hear your tummy rumble, say hello! It is just your muscles squeezing things along.
