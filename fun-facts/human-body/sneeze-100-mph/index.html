🤧 How fast is a sneeze?

A sneeze can travel at up to 100 miles per hour, about twice as fast as a cough.

The big idea

A sneeze can travel at up to 100 miles per hour, roughly twice as fast as a cough.

How it works

🤧

A reflex

A sneeze is triggered when the nasal lining is irritated.

💨

Fast

It can go up to 100 miles per hour.

💪

Cover up

Cover with your sleeve or elbow rather than your hands.

Quick stats

More about the human body

  • A cough clears the big airways of irritants and germs.
  • A cough sends out almost 3,000 droplets in one go.
  • Use the crook of your elbow to cover a cough or sneeze.

Words to know

Reflex
A quick body reaction you do not choose.
Droplet
A tiny drop of liquid.

🧪 Try it yourself

Practice the elbow sneeze: bend your arm and sneeze or cough into the crook of your elbow. Germs stay off your hands!
