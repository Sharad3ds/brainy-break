💦 How much spit do you make?

Your salivary glands make about half a liter to one and a half liters of saliva every day.

The big idea

Your salivary glands make about half a liter to 1.5 liters of saliva a day, and saliva starts digestion.

How it works

💦

Easy to swallow

Saliva moistens food so it is easy to swallow.

🍞

Starts digestion

Amylase begins breaking starchy foods into sugars.

🛡️

Germ fighter

It has antibodies that help defend against viruses.

Quick stats

More about the human body

  • The big glands are the parotid, the submandibular and the sublingual.
  • Saliva is made of water, mucus, mineral salts and enzymes.
  • Another enzyme in saliva, lingual lipase, is linked to starting fat digestion.

Words to know

Saliva
Another word for spit.
Enzyme
A helper chemical that speeds up a job.

🧪 Try it yourself

Chew a cracker for a minute without swallowing. It may start to taste a little sweet as amylase turns starch into sugar!
