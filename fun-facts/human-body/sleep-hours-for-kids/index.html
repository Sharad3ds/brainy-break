The big idea

School-aged kids are recommended 9 to 12 hours of sleep every 24 hours, and enough sleep helps with attention, learning and memory.

How it works

🧠

Learning

Enough sleep supports attention, learning and memory.

😊

Mood

It supports emotional health and behavior.

📚

School

Too little sleep makes concentrating in class harder.

Quick stats

More about the human body

  • These numbers come from the American Academy of Sleep Medicine.
  • Many teens do not get enough sleep.
  • Preschoolers can count naps toward their sleep total.
