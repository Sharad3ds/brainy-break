🫁 How many tiny air sacs are in your lungs?

Your lungs have about 480 million tiny air sacs called alveoli.

The big idea

Your lungs have about 480 million alveoli with a surface area over 130 m². Their walls are just one cell thick.

How it works

🎈

Tiny sacs

About 480 million alveoli, each about 200 micrometers wide.

📐

Huge surface

More than 130 square meters in total.

🔄

Gas swap

Oxygen goes into the blood and carbon dioxide comes out.

More about the human body

  • Thin walls let gases pass quickly.
  • The lungs take in about 6 liters of air per minute.
  • Type 1 cells cover about 95% of the alveolar surface.

Words to know

Alveolus
A tiny air sac in the lung.
Carbon dioxide
A gas your body makes and breathes out.
