😉 How many times do you blink in a day?

Adults blink about 14 to 17 times a minute, but only about 5 when staring at a screen.

The big idea

You blink about 14–17 times a minute, but only about 5 at a screen. Blinking keeps eyes moist and clean.

How it works

💧

Tears

Blinks spread tears over the eyeball.

🧹

Cleaning

They remove dirt, dust and dead cells.

📱

Screens

Blinking drops sharply when you stare at a screen.

Quick stats

14–17blinks a minute
13,440–16,320blinks a waking day
~5blinks a minute at a screen
3kinds: automatic, on purpose, reflex

More about the human body

  • Reflex blinks happen when something approaches your eye.
  • Blinking shields the eyes from bright light.
  • Taking screen breaks and blinking on purpose can help with dry eyes.

Words to know

Reflex
An action your body does automatically.
Tears
The salty liquid that keeps your eyes moist.

🧪 Try it yourself

Count your blinks for 30 seconds without trying to change them. Then double the number. Does it match 14 to 17 a minute?
