🔬 How many cells are in your body?

Scientists estimate that an average human body has about 37.2 trillion cells.

The big idea

An average human body has an estimated 37.2 trillion cells, though the exact number is hard to measure.

How it works

🧮

How to count

Researchers combined volumes and densities for each organ and cell type.

🫀

Different types

Heart muscle and fat cells are among the many kinds of cells.

🤔

An estimate

Different methods give different results, so it is a best guess.

Quick stats

More about the human body

  • Counting cells is hard because cells are not packed evenly through the body.
  • Other methods gave estimates from about 15 trillion to about 70 trillion.
  • Microbes living on the body are not counted in this number.

Words to know

Cell
The tiny building block of every living thing.
Estimate
A careful guess that is close but not exact.

🧪 Try it yourself

Counting to a million out loud, one number a second, would take about 12 days. A trillion is a million millions, so counting your cells would take far longer than a lifetime!
