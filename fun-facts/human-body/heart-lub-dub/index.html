🔊 What makes the lub-dub sound of your heartbeat?

The lub-dub you hear is heart valves closing, and your heart has four chambers and four valves.

The big idea

The lub-dub is heart valves closing. Your heart has 4 chambers and 4 valves, and usually beats 60 to 100 times a minute.

How it works

🚪

One-way doors

Four valves keep blood moving the right way.

🔊

Lub

The tricuspid and mitral valves close.

🔊

Dub

The aortic and pulmonic valves close.

Quick stats

4chambers
4valves
60–100beats a minute at rest
2sounds: lub and dub

More about the human body

  • The top chambers are the right atrium and left atrium.
  • The bottom chambers are the right ventricle and left ventricle.
  • Blood goes from the right side to the lungs, then back to the left side to go out to the body.

Words to know

Atrium
A top chamber of the heart that receives blood.
Ventricle
A bottom chamber of the heart that pumps blood out.

🧪 Try it yourself

Put two fingers on your neck or wrist and feel your pulse. Count the beats for 15 seconds, then multiply by 4!
