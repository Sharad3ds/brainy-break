🧼 Why wash your hands for 20 seconds?

Many health groups recommend washing for about 20 seconds, and soap-and-water handwashing can prevent about 1 in 5 respiratory infections.

The big idea

Washing with soap for about 20 seconds can prevent about 30 percent of diarrhea illnesses and about 20 percent of respiratory infections.

How it works

🧼

Soap and water

Washing with soap removes germs from your hands.

⏱️

Take your time

Washing 15 to 30 seconds removes more germs than a quick rinse.

🏫

School days

It can reduce stomach-illness absences among schoolchildren.

More about the human body

  • Many experts also suggest 20 to 30 seconds for drying.
  • One gram of feces, about the weight of a paper clip, can contain one trillion germs.
  • Handwashing could protect about 1 in 3 young children who get diarrhea.

Words to know

Germ
A tiny living thing that can make you sick.
Hygiene
Habits that keep you clean and healthy.

🧪 Try it yourself

Wash your hands with soap and count slowly to 20, or sing Happy Birthday twice. Remember the backs of your hands and your thumbs!
