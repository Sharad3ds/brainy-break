😮 What causes hiccups?

A hiccup is a spasm of your diaphragm, and the hic sound comes from your vocal cords closing.

The big idea

Hiccups are repeated spasms of the diaphragm, and most short ones stop on their own after seconds or minutes.

How it works

💨

Diaphragm drop

The diaphragm suddenly drops, pulling in a quick gulp of air.

🔊

Vocal cords

The glottis shuts and makes the hic sound.

🥤

Triggers

Eating too fast, fizzy drinks and swallowed air can trigger them.

More about the human body

  • The vagus and phrenic nerves form a reflex loop that controls the diaphragm.
  • Hiccups lasting more than 48 hours are called persistent.
  • Smoking and alcohol can also trigger brief hiccups.

Words to know

Diaphragm
A big muscle under your lungs that helps you breathe.
Spasm
A sudden squeeze of a muscle you did not choose.

🧪 Try it yourself

Breathe in slowly and count to five, then breathe out slowly. Slow breathing is a calm way to wait for hiccups to pass.
