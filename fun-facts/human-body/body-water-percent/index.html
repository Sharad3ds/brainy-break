💧 How much of you is water?

Water makes up up to about 60 percent of an adult body, and babies start out even wetter.

The big idea

An adult body is up to about 60 percent water, and the lungs are about 83 percent water.

How it works

👶

Babies

Newborns are about 78 percent water.

🧠

Brain and heart

They are about 73 percent water.

🦴

Even bones

Bones are about 31 percent water.

More about the human body

  • Muscles and kidneys are about 79 percent water.
  • Skin is about 64 percent water.
  • Adult men are about 60 percent water and adult women about 55 percent.

Words to know

Hydrate
To give your body enough water.
Percent
A number out of 100.

🧪 Try it yourself

Fill a glass of water and have a drink. Remember, you are more than half water!
