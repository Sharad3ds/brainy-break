The big idea

Your brain is about 60% fat, weighs about 3 pounds, has about 86 billion neurons and runs on about 20 watts.

How it works

🥛

Half-gallon weight

An adult brain weighs about 3 pounds, like a half-gallon of milk.

💡

Lightbulb power

It runs on about 20 watts.

🧩

All in use

Using only 10 percent is a myth; we use all of the brain.

More about the human body

  • A grain-sized piece of brain tissue holds about 100,000 neurons and 1 billion synapses.
  • Brain freeze is medically called sphenopalatine ganglioneuralgia.
  • Memory storage capacity is considered virtually unlimited.

Words to know

Neuron
A nerve cell that sends signals.
Synapse
A connection point between two neurons.

🧪 Try it yourself

Feel a half-gallon carton of milk or a 3-pound bag of flour. That is about how heavy your brain is!
