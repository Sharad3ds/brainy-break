🧬 How long is the DNA inside one cell?

The DNA in a single cell is about 2 meters long if you uncoil it, yet it fits inside a tiny nucleus.

The big idea

Uncoiled, the DNA from one cell is about 2 meters long, with about 3 billion base pairs packed into 46 chromosomes.

How it works

📏

Long thread

One cell holds about 2 meters of DNA when uncoiled.

🔤

Many letters

It has about 3 billion base pairs.

📦

Tight packing

Enzymes coil the DNA until it is about 6 microns across.

More about the human body

  • A micron is one thousandth of a millimeter.
  • DNA is wound up, or supercoiled, by enzymes so it can fit.
  • The figures here are approximate.

Words to know

DNA
The instruction code inside your cells.
Chromosome
A tightly packed bundle of DNA.

🧪 Try it yourself

Take a long piece of string about 2 meters long and try to stuff it into a tiny box. Cells have to do the same with DNA!
