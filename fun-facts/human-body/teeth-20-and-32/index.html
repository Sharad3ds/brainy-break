🦷 How many teeth do you get in your life?

You get 20 baby teeth first, then 32 permanent teeth by about age 21.

The big idea

You get 20 baby teeth first and 32 permanent teeth, which have usually all come in by about age 21.

How it works

👶

Primary teeth

20 in total, starting around 6 months.

🧑

Permanent teeth

32 in total.

🪥

Care

Permanent teeth have to last, so brush and floss.

More about the human body

  • Baby teeth are shed at different times through childhood.
  • Permanent teeth usually finish coming in by age 21.
  • The ADA's eruption charts show when each tooth appears.

Words to know

Primary teeth
Baby teeth.
Permanent teeth
The adult teeth that come after baby teeth.

🧪 Try it yourself

Count your teeth with your tongue. Do you still have baby teeth? Adults with all their teeth have 32!
