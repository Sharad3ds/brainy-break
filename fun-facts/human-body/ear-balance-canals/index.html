🌀 How do your ears help you balance?

Three fluid-filled canals in your inner ear tell your brain how your head is moving.

The big idea

Three semicircular canals hold fluid and hair cells that tell your brain how your head moves. Eyes, muscles and skin help too.

How it works

🌀

Three canals

Set at right angles, they sense different motions.

💧

Fluid

Moving fluid stimulates the hair cells.

🧠

Brain mix

The brain combines ear, eye, muscle, joint and skin signals.

Quick stats

3canals
90°angle between them
2extra organs: utricle and saccule
4+clues: ear, eyes, muscles, skin

More about the human body

  • The canals detect up and down, side to side, and tilting motions.
  • The utricle and saccule also sense motion.
  • The vestibular nerve carries the signals to the brain.

Words to know

Vestibular system
The inner-ear system for balance.
Hair cell
A tiny sensor that bends when fluid moves.

🧪 Try it yourself

Stand on one foot with your eyes open, then closed. Closing your eyes takes away a clue, so balance gets harder!
