The big idea

The Kuiper Belt is a zone of millions of icy objects beyond Neptune, and Pluto was the first one found in 1930.

How it works

📍

Past Neptune

The inner edge is at about 30 AU.

🧊

Icy bodies

Objects contain rock, water ice, ammonia and methane.

⛄

Arrokoth

New Horizons flew by this snowman-shaped object on January 1, 2019.

More about the solar system

  • The whole belt weighs no more than about 10 percent of Earth.
  • Eris has slightly more mass than Pluto.
  • An AU is the distance from Earth to the Sun.

Words to know

AU
Astronomical unit: the Earth-Sun distance.
Kuiper Belt
A donut of icy objects beyond Neptune.

🧪 Try it yourself

Draw a big donut around a small Sun. The Kuiper Belt is a donut like that, far beyond Neptune!
