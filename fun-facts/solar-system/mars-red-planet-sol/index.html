🔴 Why is Mars red?

Mars looks red because iron in its rocks and dust has oxidized, like rust.

The big idea

Mars looks red because iron in its rocks and dust has oxidized, like rust.

How it works

🔴

Rusty color

Iron in the rocks and dust oxidizes and makes Mars look red.

⏱️

Short sol

A Martian day, a sol, is about 24.6 hours.

📅

Long year

A Mars year is 687 Earth days.

More about the solar system

  • Martian days are called sols.
  • A Mars year is 669.6 sols long.
  • Dust in the thin air would make the Martian sky look hazy and red.

Words to know

Oxidize
To react with oxygen, like iron rusting.
Sol
A day on Mars.

🧪 Try it yourself

Look at an old rusty nail or bike chain. The red color is iron rust. Mars is red for a similar reason!
