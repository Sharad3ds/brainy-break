The big idea

Hubble has orbited Earth since 1990 and has made more than 1.2 million observations.

How it works

🚌

School-bus size

It is about 43.5 feet long.

🛰️

Low orbit

It orbits about 340 miles up at about 17,000 mph.

🛠️

Repaired

Five servicing missions fixed and upgraded Hubble.

More about the solar system

  • Servicing missions were in 1993, 1997, 1999, 2002 and 2009.
  • Hubble was deployed on April 25, 1990.
  • Hubble has traveled more than 3 billion miles.

Words to know

Observation
A careful look or measurement.
Servicing mission
A trip by astronauts to repair a spacecraft.

🧪 Try it yourself

Look at a distant object through a rolled-up paper tube. Hubble sees far-away things much more clearly because it is above Earth's air!
