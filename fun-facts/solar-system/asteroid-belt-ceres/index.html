🪨 What is the asteroid belt?

The asteroid belt lies between Mars and Jupiter, and Ceres, a dwarf planet, makes up about 25 percent of its mass.

The big idea

Most asteroids live in the belt between Mars and Jupiter, and the dwarf planet Ceres holds about 25 percent of its mass.

How it works

📍

Location

The asteroid belt is between Mars and Jupiter.

🥇

Biggest

Ceres is a dwarf planet with a radius of about 296 miles.

🛰️

Visited

NASA's Dawn arrived at Ceres in 2015.

More about the solar system

  • Pluto is about 14 times more massive than Ceres.
  • Dawn was the first spacecraft to visit a dwarf planet.
  • Ceres is about 1/13 of Earth's radius.

Words to know

Asteroid
A small rocky world that goes around the Sun.
Belt
A wide ring of objects in space.

🧪 Try it yourself

Sprinkle some pebbles in a ring around a ball on a table. That is a bit like the asteroid belt around the Sun!
