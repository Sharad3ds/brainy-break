The big idea

Artemis II flew four astronauts around the Moon in April 2026 and set a record: 252,756 miles from Earth.

How it works

🧪

Test flight

Artemis I in 2022 tested SLS and Orion without a crew.

👩‍🚀

Crew flight

Artemis II was the first crewed flight of Orion.

🌙

Next step

A crewed landing is planned for 2028 on Artemis IV.

More about the solar system

  • The crew was Reid Wiseman, Victor Glover, Christina Koch and Jeremy Hansen.
  • Jeremy Hansen is from the Canadian Space Agency.
  • SLS is the only rocket that can send Orion, astronauts and cargo directly to the Moon in one launch.

Words to know

Orion
The spacecraft that carries astronauts.
SLS
The big rocket that launches Orion.
