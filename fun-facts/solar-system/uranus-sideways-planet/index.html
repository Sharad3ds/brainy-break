↔️ Why does Uranus spin on its side?

Uranus is tilted about 98 degrees, so it rolls around the Sun almost on its side.

The big idea

Uranus is tilted about 98 degrees, so it spins almost on its side.

How it works

↔️

Sideways

Its axis is tilted 97.77 degrees.

📅

Long year

It takes about 84 Earth years to orbit the Sun.

🩵

Blue-green

Methane absorbs red light, so Uranus looks blue-green.

More about the solar system

  • Uranus has 13 faint rings.
  • Its coldest atmosphere is about -224°C.
  • The planet is about 19 times farther from the Sun than Earth.

Words to know

Methane
A gas that gives Uranus its color.
Tilt
How much a planet's spin axis leans over.

🧪 Try it yourself

Roll a ball on a table and call it Uranus. Real Uranus rolls around the Sun almost on its side like that!
