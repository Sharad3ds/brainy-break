🎈 Why do astronauts float in space?

Astronauts on the space station float because they are in free fall, even though gravity there is still about 90 percent as strong.

The big idea

Astronauts float because they are in free fall with the station, even though gravity there is still about 90 percent as strong.

How it works

🌍

Gravity is there

At 200 to 250 miles up, gravity is about 90 percent of surface strength.

🪂

Free fall

Station, crew and objects all fall toward Earth together.

🌀

Curved fall

At about 17,500 mph, the fall curves around Earth.

More about the solar system

  • "Zero gravity" is a misleading nickname. Scientists say microgravity.
  • NASA uses airplanes that fly up-and-down arcs to give about 20 to 30 seconds of free fall.
  • Drop towers can also create free fall.

Words to know

Microgravity
A state where things seem to have almost no weight.
Free fall
Falling when gravity is the only force pulling.

🧪 Try it yourself

Jump off a low step (safely!). For a moment you are in free fall and feel light. Astronauts feel that all the time.
