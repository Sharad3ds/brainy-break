🌌 What are sunspots and solar flares?

Sunspots are cooler dark patches on the Sun, and flares from near them can help make auroras on Earth.

The big idea

Sunspots are cooler dark patches on the Sun, and particles from big eruptions can create auroras on Earth.

How it works

🧲

Magnetic fields

Sunspots form where magnetic fields are especially strong.

💥

Flares

Flares happen when magnetic field lines tangle, cross or reorganize.

🌌

Auroras

Charged particles from big eruptions near Earth can trigger auroras.

More about the solar system

  • Flares and coronal mass ejections are the most powerful explosions in the solar system.
  • A very strong eruption can disrupt electricity grids on Earth.
  • Intense flares can interfere with radio communications.

Words to know

Aurora
Colorful lights in the sky caused by charged particles.
Magnetic field
An invisible force around magnets.
