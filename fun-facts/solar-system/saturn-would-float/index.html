The big idea

Saturn is the only planet less dense than water, and it has 274 confirmed moons.

How it works

🛁

Light planet

Its density is lower than that of water.

🎈

Light gases

It is mostly hydrogen and helium.

🌙

Many moons

274 moons were confirmed as of March 2025.

More about the solar system

  • A Saturn year is about 29.4 Earth years.
  • Saturn's magnetic field is 578 times as powerful as Earth's.
  • Saturn is tilted about 26.7 degrees.

Words to know

Density
How much stuff is packed into a space.
Float
To stay on top of water instead of sinking.

🧪 Try it yourself

Drop a cork and a stone into water. The cork floats because it is less dense. Saturn is less dense than water, too! (Only as a planet, of course.)
