👨‍🚀 Who was the first person in space?

Yuri Gagarin became the first human in space on April 12, 1961, orbiting Earth once in 108 minutes.

The big idea

Yuri Gagarin was the first human in space on April 12, 1961, and his flight lasted 108 minutes.

How it works

1️⃣

First

Gagarin's Vostok 1 flight made one orbit of Earth.

🇺🇸

First American

Alan Shepard flew on May 5, 1961, for about 15 minutes.

👩‍🚀

First woman

Valentina Tereshkova flew in 1963 and completed 48 orbits.

More about the solar system

  • Alexey Leonov made the first spacewalk on March 18, 1965, for 12 minutes.
  • Sally Ride became the first American woman in space in 1983.
  • Shepard's Freedom 7 flight reached about 115 miles high.

Words to know

Orbit
One full trip around a planet.
Spacewalk
Working outside a spacecraft in space.
