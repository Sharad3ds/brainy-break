🌋 What are the biggest volcano and canyon in the solar system?

Mars has the largest volcano, Olympus Mons, over 25 miles tall, and the largest canyon, Valles Marineris, about 2,400 miles long.

The big idea

Mars has the largest volcano, Olympus Mons, over 25 miles tall, and the largest canyon, Valles Marineris, about 2,400 miles long.

More about the solar system

  • Mars has two moons, Phobos and Deimos.
  • Phobos is slowly moving toward Mars.
  • Mars has a thin atmosphere, mostly carbon dioxide, nitrogen and argon.

Words to know

Volcano
A mountain that lava can come out of.
Canyon
A deep, long valley.

🧪 Try it yourself

Valles Marineris is about 2,400 miles long, nearly as far as the distance across the United States. Look at a map and picture a canyon that long!
