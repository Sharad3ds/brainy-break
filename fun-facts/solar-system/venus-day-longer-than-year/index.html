🔄 Why is a day on Venus longer than its year?

Venus spins so slowly that one spin takes 243 Earth days, but a trip around the Sun takes 225.

The big idea

On Venus a spin takes 243 Earth days but a year takes only 225, so a day is longer than a year.

How it works

🐌

Slow spin

One rotation takes about 243 Earth days.

🌅

Backwards

Venus spins the opposite way, so the Sun rises in the west.

📅

Short year

A Venus year is about 225 Earth days.

More about the solar system

  • On Venus the Sun rises about every 117 Earth days.
  • Most planets spin the same way, but Venus spins the opposite way.
  • Venus is similar in size to Earth.

Words to know

Rotation
One full spin of a planet.
Orbit
One trip around the Sun.

🧪 Try it yourself

Spin around slowly on the spot once, then walk a big circle. A planet does both at the same time, but Venus spins very slowly!
