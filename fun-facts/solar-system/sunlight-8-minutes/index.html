💡 How long does sunlight take to reach Earth?

Sunlight takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to travel from the Sun to Earth.

The big idea

Sunlight takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to travel the 93 million miles to Earth.

How it works

⚡

Fast light

Light moves about 186,000 miles every second.

📏

Far away

The Sun is about 93 million miles from Earth on average.

⏳

Old light

The sunlight you see left the Sun about 8 minutes ago.

More about the solar system
