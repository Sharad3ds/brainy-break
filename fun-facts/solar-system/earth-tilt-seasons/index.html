🍂 Why does Earth have seasons?

Earth's tilted axis, about 23.4 degrees, causes the seasons, not its distance from the Sun.

The big idea

Earth's axis is tilted about 23.4 degrees, and that tilt causes the seasons.

How it works

📐

The tilt

Earth's axis is tilted about 23.4 degrees.

☀️

Direct sunlight

A half tilted toward the Sun gets more direct sunlight and has summer.

🧭

Same direction

The tilted axis points the same way all through the orbit.

More about the solar system

  • Earth is closest to the Sun in January and farthest in July, but this barely changes weather.
  • The North Pole tilts toward the Sun around June.
  • The South Pole tilts toward the Sun around December.

Words to know

Axis
An imaginary line a planet spins around.
Season
One of four parts of the year with different weather.

🧪 Try it yourself

Shine a flashlight straight onto a table, then at an angle. Straight-on light is stronger. That is why summer feels hotter!
