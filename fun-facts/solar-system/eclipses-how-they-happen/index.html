🌑 What is a solar eclipse?

A solar eclipse happens when the Moon passes between Earth and the Sun.

The big idea

A solar eclipse happens when the Moon passes between Earth and the Sun.

How it works

🌑

Alignment

The Sun, Moon and Earth must nearly line up.

⚫

Two shadows

The umbra gives a total eclipse and the penumbra gives a partial one.

📐

Tilted orbit

The Moon's orbit is tilted about 5 degrees, so eclipses are not monthly.

More about the solar system

  • The Moon looks only about half a degree wide in our sky.
  • Lunar eclipses need the same kind of alignment.
  • Never look straight at the Sun, even during an eclipse, without proper eclipse glasses.

Words to know

Umbra
The darkest part of a shadow.
Penumbra
The lighter outer part of a shadow.

🧪 Try it yourself

In a dim room, hold a ball between a lamp and a wall. Watch the shadow it makes. That is how the Moon's shadow works!
