The big idea

Neptune's winds blow at over 1,200 miles per hour, and one year there lasts about 165 Earth years.

How it works

💨

Super winds

Winds are over 1,200 mph, about nine times Earth's peak winds.

📅

Long year

One orbit takes about 165 Earth years.

🔵

Blue color

Methane reflects blue light and absorbs other colors.

More about the solar system

  • Neptune is about four times as wide as Earth.
  • Triton was found on October 10, 1846 by William Lassell.
  • Photos of Neptune from Voyager 2 were enhanced, so the blue looks deeper than it is.
