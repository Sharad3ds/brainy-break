🌀 What is the Great Red Spot?

Jupiter's Great Red Spot is a storm twice as wide as Earth that has been watched for more than 300 years.

The big idea

The Great Red Spot is a storm twice as wide as Earth, seen for more than 300 years.

How it works

🌀

Giant storm

The Great Red Spot is about twice as wide as Earth.

🔭

Long watched

It has been observed for more than 300 years.

⏱️

Fast spin

Jupiter turns once in about 9.9 hours.

More about the solar system

  • A Jupiter year is about 4,333 Earth days.
  • Jupiter's clouds form colored bands.
  • Jupiter is about 5.2 times farther from the Sun than Earth.

Words to know

Storm
A swirl of wind and clouds.
Rotation

🧪 Try it yourself

Stir a cup of water with a spoon and watch the swirl. Jupiter's storm is like that, but twice as wide as our whole planet!
