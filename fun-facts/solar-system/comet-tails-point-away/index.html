💫 Why do comets have tails?

A comet's tail always points away from the Sun, pushed by sunlight and the solar wind.

The big idea

A comet's tail points away from the Sun, pushed by sunlight and the solar wind.

How it works

🔥

Warming up

Near the Sun, a comet's ices turn to gas and make a coma.

🌬️

Pushed back

Sunlight pressure and solar wind push the gas and dust away.

2️⃣

Two tails

Comets have a dust tail and an ion tail.

More about the solar system

  • Not every comet forms a long bright tail.
  • The coma is an atmosphere made from the comet's melting ices.
  • The solar wind is a stream of particles from the Sun.

Words to know

Coma
The glowing cloud around a comet's nucleus.
Solar wind
A stream of particles flowing out from the Sun.

🧪 Try it yourself

Hold a ribbon in front of a fan. It streams away from the fan. A comet's tail streams away from the Sun just like that!
