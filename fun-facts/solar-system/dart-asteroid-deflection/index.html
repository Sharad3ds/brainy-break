The big idea

NASA's DART spacecraft hit the moonlet Dimorphos in 2022 and shortened its orbit by 32 minutes.

How it works

🎯

On purpose

DART crashed into Dimorphos on September 27, 2022 as a test.

⚡

Fast

It hit at about 14,000 mph.

⏱️

It worked

Dimorphos's orbit changed by 32 minutes.

More about the solar system

  • Neither Didymos nor Dimorphos was a danger to Earth, before or after.
  • The spacecraft weighed about 1,260 pounds.
  • The test was to learn how to protect Earth in the future.
