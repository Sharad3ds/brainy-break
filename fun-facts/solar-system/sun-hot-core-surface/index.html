🔥 How hot is the Sun?

The Sun's surface is about 10,000 degrees Fahrenheit, and its core is about 27 million degrees.

The big idea

The Sun's surface is about 10,000°F and its core is about 27 million°F.

How it works

🔥

Hot surface

The photosphere is about 10,000°F (5,500°C).

🌡️

Hotter core

The core is about 27 million°F (15 million°C).

💡

Own light

The Sun is a star, so it makes its own light and heat.

More about the solar system

  • The visible surface of the Sun is called the photosphere.
  • The core is much hotter than the surface.
  • The Sun is mostly hydrogen and helium.

Words to know

Core
The center of a star or planet.
Photosphere
The Sun's visible surface.

🧪 Try it yourself

Feel the warmth of sunlight on your hand (not by staring at the Sun!). Remember, that warmth came from a place that is 93 million miles away.
