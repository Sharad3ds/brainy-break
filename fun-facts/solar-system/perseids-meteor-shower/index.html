🌠 What is a meteor shower?

The Perseid meteor shower happens when Earth passes through dust left by Comet Swift-Tuttle.

The big idea

A meteor shower happens when Earth passes through a comet's dusty trail, like the August Perseids.

How it works

🛤️

Dust trails

Comets leave debris trails as they orbit the Sun.

🌍

Earth crosses

Each year Earth passes through these trails.

🌠

Streaks

The particles burn up and make streaks of light.

More about the solar system

  • The Perseid peak night is around August 12 to 13.
  • Most comet debris is between a sand grain and a pea in size.
  • Meteors also come from pieces of broken asteroids.

Words to know

Meteor shower
Many shooting stars in one night.
Debris
Small leftover pieces.
