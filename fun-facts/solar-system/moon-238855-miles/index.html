🌙 How far away is the Moon?

The Moon is about 238,855 miles from Earth, and it is slowly drifting away by about an inch a year.

The big idea

The Moon is about 238,855 miles from Earth and drifts about one inch farther each year.

How it works

📏

Far but close

It is about 238,855 miles away on average.

🏋️

Light gravity

Moon gravity is one sixth of Earth's.

➡️

Drifting

It moves away from Earth about an inch a year.

More about the solar system

  • The Moon likely formed after a Mars-sized body hit Earth several billion years ago.
  • The big Tycho crater is more than 52 miles wide.
  • The Moon's radius is about 1,080 miles.

Words to know
