🪐 Which planet is the biggest?

Jupiter is the largest planet, so big that about 1,000 Earths could fit inside it if it were hollow.

The big idea

Jupiter is the largest planet, about 11 times Earth's radius, and it is mostly hydrogen and helium.

How it works

📏

Huge size

Its radius is about 43,441 miles, about 11 times Earth's.

🎈

Gas giant

It is mostly hydrogen and helium, similar to the Sun's mix.

⚖️

Heavy

It holds more than twice the material of all other solar system bodies combined.

More about the solar system

  • Sunlight takes about 43 minutes to reach Jupiter.
  • Jupiter's rings were found in 1979 by Voyager 1.
  • Jupiter has 115 officially recognized moons.

Words to know

Gas giant
A big planet made mostly of gas.
Hydrogen
The lightest and most common gas in the universe.
