More about the solar system

  • Long-period comets can take up to 30 million years to orbit the Sun once.
  • Long-period comets often come from the Oort Cloud, about 100,000 astronomical units away.
  • Halley's Comet gets as close to the Sun as about 55 million miles.

Words to know

Nucleus
The solid icy center of a comet.
Comet
An icy visitor that grows a tail near the Sun.

🧪 Try it yourself

Mix some soil into a snowball or a ball of ice. That is a bit like a comet: dirty and icy at the same time!
