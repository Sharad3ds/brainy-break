🕳️ What is a black hole?

A black hole has gravity so strong that nothing, not even light, can escape from it.

The big idea

A black hole has gravity so strong that nothing, not even light, can escape from it.

How it works

⭐

Star end

Black holes form at the end of some stars' lives.

⭕

Event horizon

It is the boundary past which nothing can escape.

🌑

Looks black

Because light cannot escape, black holes look black.

More about the solar system

  • A spacecraft near the event horizon would be stretched, because gravity is much stronger on the side closer to the black hole.
  • Some black holes grow as they trap more material.
  • The central point is called a singularity.

Words to know

Gravity
The force that pulls things together.
Event horizon
The edge of a black hole, the point of no return.

🧪 Try it yourself

Toss a ball up. Gravity brings it back down. A black hole has gravity so strong that even light cannot get away!
