The big idea

A light-year is distance, not time: about 5.88 trillion miles.

How it works

📏

A distance

It measures how far light travels in one year.

⚡

Fast light

Light goes about 186,000 miles per second.

🌌

Big space

Space is so big that miles are too small to measure it easily.

Quick stats

5.88 trillionmiles
9.46 trillionkilometers
186,000miles a second

More about the solar system

  • Our nearest star neighbor, Proxima Centauri, is about 4.25 light-years away.
  • The Milky Way is about 100,000 light-years across.
  • Light-years are used for distances between stars.

Words to know

Light-year
The distance light travels in one year.
Trillion
A thousand billions.

🧪 Try it yourself

Think of a light-year as a giant ruler. Say: one year of light = one very long step!
