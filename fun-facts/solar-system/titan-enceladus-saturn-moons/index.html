💨 What is special about Titan and Enceladus?

Saturn's moon Titan has lakes of liquid methane and ethane, and Enceladus sprays water from cracks near its south pole.

The big idea

Titan has lakes of liquid methane and ethane, and Enceladus sprays water geysers from cracks near its south pole.

How it works

🏞️

Titan's lakes

Liquid methane and ethane rain down and fill lakes.

⛲

Geysers

Enceladus sprays water vapor and icy particles into space.

🌊

Hidden oceans

Cassini found evidence of oceans under the ice.

More about the solar system

  • Titan's haze makes it look like a fuzzy orange ball from space.
  • Enceladus feeds material into Saturn's E-Ring.
  • Cassini plunged into Saturn's atmosphere on September 15, 2017.

Words to know

Geyser
A jet of water shooting up.
Methane
A gas that can be liquid when very cold.
