🌌 What is the Milky Way?

The Milky Way is a barred spiral galaxy about 100,000 light-years across with around 100 billion stars.

The big idea

The Milky Way is a barred spiral galaxy about 100,000 light-years across with near 100 billion stars.

How it works

🌀

Spiral shape

It is a large barred spiral galaxy with four spiral arms by the latest data.

⭐

Many stars

Best estimates put the count near 100 billion.

📍

Our spot

The Sun sits about 25,000 light-years from the center.

More about the solar system

  • The solar system takes about 230 to 250 million years to orbit the galactic center.
  • A galaxy is a huge group of stars, gas and dust held together by gravity.
  • Our Sun is in the outer part of the Milky Way.

Words to know

Galaxy
A giant family of stars, gas and dust.
Spiral
A shape that curls around like a swirl.

🧪 Try it yourself

Stir a spoon in a cup of tea with a little milk and watch the swirl. The Milky Way swirls like that, only much bigger!
