🌊 How does the Moon cause tides?

The Moon's gravity makes the oceans bulge, so most shores get two high tides every day.

The big idea

The Moon's gravity makes the oceans bulge, so most shores see two high tides and two low tides each day.

How it works

🌊

Two bulges

Oceans bulge toward the Moon and on the far side of Earth.

⏱️

Just over 12 hours

High tide to high tide takes a little over 12 hours.

☀️

Sun helps

The Sun adds less than half the Moon's tide force.

More about the solar system

  • The Sun is about 390 times farther away than the Moon, so its pull on tides is less than half.
  • Neap tides happen about a week after spring tides, with moderate tides.
  • Local winds, storms and the shape of the coast also affect real tides.

Words to know

Tide
The rise and fall of the sea each day.
Bulge
A place that sticks out.
