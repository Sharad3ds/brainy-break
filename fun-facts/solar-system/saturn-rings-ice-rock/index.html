The big idea

Saturn's rings are billions of chunks of ice and rock, stretching up to 175,000 miles but only about 30 feet thick.

How it works

🧊

Icy chunks

The rings are billions of small chunks of ice and rock.

📏

Very wide

They extend up to about 175,000 miles from Saturn.

📉

Very thin

The main rings are typically about 30 feet thick.

More about the solar system

  • The Cassini Division separates rings A and B.
  • The ring pieces are coated with materials such as dust.
  • Saturn is about 9 times as wide as Earth.

Words to know

Ring
A flat circle of pieces going around a planet.
Division
A gap between two rings.
