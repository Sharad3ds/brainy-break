🌓 Why does the Moon change shape?

The Moon makes no light of its own, and its phases repeat about every 29.5 days.

The big idea

The Moon has eight phases that repeat about every 29.5 days, because we see different amounts of its sunlit half.

How it works

💡

Reflected light

The Moon does not make light, it reflects sunlight.

🌗

Half lit

The Sun lights half of the Moon, and our view of it changes.

🔄

A cycle

One new moon to the next takes about 29.5 days.

More about the solar system

  • The phases are new, waxing crescent, first quarter, waxing gibbous, full, waning gibbous, third quarter and waning crescent.
  • The Moon's orbit takes about 27.3 days.
  • Moonlight is really sunlight reflected from the Moon.

Words to know

Phase
The shape of the lit part of the Moon we see.
Waxing
Getting bigger, with more of the Moon lit up.

🧪 Try it yourself

In a dark room, hold a ball and shine a flashlight on it. Turn slowly around it and watch the lit part change like the Moon phases!
