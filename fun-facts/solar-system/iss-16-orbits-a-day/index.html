🛰️ How fast does the space station travel?

The International Space Station travels about 5 miles per second and circles Earth about every 90 minutes.

The big idea

The International Space Station circles Earth about every 90 minutes, about 16 times a day.

How it works

⚡

Fast

It travels about 5 miles per second, around 18,000 mph.

🏟️

Big

It is 356 feet long, about a football field.

🌍

Shared

Five space agencies from 15 countries operate it.

More about the solar system

  • It is larger than a six-bedroom house inside, with two bathrooms and a gym.
  • It has a 360-degree bay window.
  • Eight solar arrays give it 75 to 90 kilowatts of power.

Words to know

Orbit
A path around a planet.
Space agency
A group that runs space missions.

🧪 Try it yourself

Sixteen orbits a day means about 16 sunrises and 16 sunsets for the astronauts every day. Imagine that!
