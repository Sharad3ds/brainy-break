The big idea

The Sun is about 100 times wider than Earth, and about 1.3 million Earths could fit inside it.

How it works

📏

Super wide

The Sun is about 865,000 miles across, roughly 100 times wider than Earth.

⚖️

Heavy

It holds 99.8 percent of the mass of the solar system.

🎈

Mostly gas

It is made mainly of hydrogen and helium.

More about the solar system

  • The Sun formed about 4.6 billion years ago.
  • More than 330,000 Earths would be needed to match the Sun's mass.
  • A star, the Sun is the center of our solar system.

Words to know

Star
A huge ball of hot gas that makes its own light.
Mass
How much stuff something is made of.

🧪 Try it yourself

Pick a tiny pea to be Earth. A pea is about 1 centimeter wide, so a Sun that is 100 times wider would be about as wide as a big beach ball, around 1 meter!
