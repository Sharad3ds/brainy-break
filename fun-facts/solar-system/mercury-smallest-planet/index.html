🪨 Which planet is the smallest?

Mercury is the smallest planet, a little more than a third as wide as Earth, and its year is only 88 days.

The big idea

Mercury is the smallest planet, and one trip around the Sun takes just 88 Earth days.

How it works

📏

Tiny planet

Its radius is 1,516 miles, a little more than one third of Earth's width.

☀️

Close to the Sun

It is about 36 million miles from the Sun on average.

🕳️

Craters

Its surface is heavily scarred by impact craters, like the Moon.

More about the solar system

  • The big Caloris crater on Mercury is about 960 miles across.
  • Mercury has a thin exosphere instead of a real atmosphere.
  • Mercury has a large metallic core, about 85 percent of the planet's radius.

Words to know

Planet
A big world that goes around a star.
Crater
A bowl-shaped hole made by an impact.
