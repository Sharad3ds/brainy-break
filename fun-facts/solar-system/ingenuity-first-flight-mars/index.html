🚁 Can a helicopter fly on Mars?

NASA's Ingenuity helicopter made the first powered, controlled flight on another planet on April 19, 2021.

The big idea

On April 19, 2021, Ingenuity made the first powered, controlled flight on another planet.

How it works

🚗

Rover ride

Perseverance carried Ingenuity to Mars and landed in Jezero Crater.

🚁

First flight

Ingenuity flew on April 19, 2021.

📏

Small

It was 19 inches tall and weighed about 4 pounds.

More about the solar system

  • Perseverance landed at Octavia E. Butler Landing in Jezero Crater.
  • Ingenuity was deployed from the rover on April 3, 2021.
  • Ingenuity weighed about 1.8 kilograms.

Words to know

Rover
A robot car that explores another world.
Helicopter
An aircraft with spinning blades.

🧪 Try it yourself

Fold a paper helicopter and drop it from a chair. Watch it spin down. Ingenuity's blades spun much faster to fly in Mars's thin air!
