☄️ What is the difference between a meteor and a meteorite?

The big idea

A meteoroid is in space, a meteor is the streak of light, and a meteorite is a piece that reaches the ground.

How it works

🪨

Meteoroid

A space rock from dust grain size up to a small asteroid.

🌠

Meteor

A meteoroid burning up in the atmosphere, a shooting star.

🌍

Meteorite

A piece that survives and lands, typically pebble to fist size.

More about the solar system

  • Space rocks move at tens of thousands of miles per hour before entering the atmosphere.
  • Most rocks smaller than a football field break apart in the atmosphere.
  • Less than 5 percent of the original object usually reaches the ground.

Words to know

Meteor
The streak of light from a space rock burning up.
Meteorite
A space rock that lands on the ground.

🧪 Try it yourself

Remember: meteOR is in the air, meteORITE is on the ground. The one that is in space is the meteoroid.
