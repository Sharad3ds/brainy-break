The big idea

The nearest star after the Sun is Proxima Centauri, about 4.25 light-years away.

How it works

📏

So far

It is about 4.25 light-years away.

⭐

Three stars

It belongs to the Alpha Centauri system of three stars.

🪐

A planet

At least one planet, Proxima b, has been found.

More about the solar system

  • Astronomers think there may possibly be two more planets around Proxima.
  • A light-year is the distance light travels in one year.
  • Proxima Centauri is a small, cool star.

Words to know

Light-year
The distance light travels in one year.
Exoplanet
A planet that goes around a star other than the Sun.
