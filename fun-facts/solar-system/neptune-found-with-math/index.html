🧮 How was Neptune found with math?

Astronomers predicted where Neptune should be, and it was found on September 23-24, 1846.

The big idea

Neptune was found in 1846 after mathematicians used Uranus's orbit irregularities to predict where it was.

How it works

🤔

A clue

Irregularities in Uranus's orbit hinted at another planet.

🧮

Math

Le Verrier and Adams worked out where it should be.

🔭

Found!

Galle found it at the Berlin Observatory, about 1 degree from the prediction.

More about the solar system

  • Neptune was found on the first night Galle searched.
  • Galle used the Fraunhofer telescope at the Berlin Observatory.
  • By 1845, Uranus had completed nearly one full orbit since its discovery.

Words to know

Prediction
A guess about the future based on evidence.
Observatory
A place with telescopes for studying the sky.
