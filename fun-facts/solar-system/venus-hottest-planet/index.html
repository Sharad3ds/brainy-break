The big idea

Venus is the hottest planet, about 900°F at the surface, because its thick air traps heat.

How it works

🔥

Very hot

The surface is about 900°F (475°C), hot enough to melt lead.

🧥

Heat trap

Carbon dioxide in its thick atmosphere traps heat.

☁️

Acid clouds

Its thick clouds are made of sulfuric acid.

More about the solar system

  • Venus is similar in size to Earth, a little smaller.
  • Venus is the third-brightest object in our sky after the Sun and the Moon.
  • Venus has no moon, but it has a tiny quasi-satellite called Zoozve.

Words to know

Greenhouse effect
When gases trap heat in an atmosphere.
Atmosphere
The layer of gas around a planet.

🧪 Try it yourself

Sit in a parked car on a sunny day (with a grown-up!). It gets warm inside because glass traps heat, a bit like Venus!
