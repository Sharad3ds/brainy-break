The big idea

Mars has two lumpy moons, Phobos and Deimos, and Phobos circles Mars three times a day.

How it works

🥔

Lumpy shape

Both moons are lumpy and heavily cratered.

🔄

Fast orbit

Phobos circles Mars three times a day.

📉

Spiraling in

Phobos moves about 6 feet closer to Mars each century.

More about the solar system

  • Phobos has a crater called Stickney, about 6 miles wide.
  • Phobos means fear and Deimos means dread, after sons of Ares.
  • Asaph Hall found Deimos first, and Phobos six nights later.

Words to know

Crater
A bowl-shaped hole from an impact.
Orbit
The path a moon takes around a planet.

🧪 Try it yourself

Wiggle your hand around in a circle three times quickly. Phobos goes around Mars that many times in just one day!
