💜 Why is Pluto a dwarf planet?

Pluto is about one fifth as wide as Earth and was reclassified as a dwarf planet in 2006.

The big idea

Pluto is a dwarf planet about one fifth as wide as Earth, with five known moons.

How it works

📏

Small world

It is about 1,477 miles across, roughly one fifth of Earth's width.

🌙

Five moons

Charon, Nix, Hydra, Kerberos and Styx.

🚀

New Horizons

A spacecraft flew past on July 14, 2015.

More about the solar system

  • Pluto is about 3.7 billion miles from the Sun on average.
  • Its orbit takes 248 Earth years.
  • The IAU made Pluto a dwarf planet in 2006.

Words to know

Dwarf planet
A small world that goes around the Sun but has not cleared its orbit.
Flyby
When a spacecraft zooms past a world.
