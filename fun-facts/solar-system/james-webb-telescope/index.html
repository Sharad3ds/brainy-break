The big idea

Webb has a 6.5-meter mirror made of 18 segments and orbits about 1.5 million kilometers from Earth.

How it works

🪞

Big mirror

The primary mirror is about 6.5 meters across and has 18 segments.

🌡️

Cold

It works below 50 K, which is about -370°F.

📍

Far away

It orbits around the L2 point, about 1.5 million km from Earth.

More about the solar system

  • The sunshield is about 69.5 feet by 46.5 feet.
  • It sees infrared light from about 0.6 to 28.5 microns.
  • Webb was launched on an Ariane 5 rocket.

Words to know

Infrared
Light we cannot see, felt as heat.
Segment
One piece of a bigger whole.

🧪 Try it yourself

Fit several same-shaped tiles or coins together to cover a flat area. Webb's mirror is made of 18 pieces fitted together like that!
