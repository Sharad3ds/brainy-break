🌡️ How hot and cold does Mercury get?

Mercury's daytime highs reach about 800°F, and its nights drop to about -290°F.

The big idea

Mercury swings from about 800°F by day to about -290°F at night.

How it works

🔥

Hot days

Daytime highs reach about 800°F (430°C).

🧊

Cold nights

Nighttime lows drop to about -290°F (-180°C).

🫧

Thin air

Mercury has only a thin exosphere, not a true atmosphere.

More about the solar system

  • A day from sunrise to sunrise on Mercury is just over two Mercury years long.
  • The exosphere is mostly oxygen, sodium, hydrogen, helium and potassium.
  • Mercury is the planet closest to the Sun, but not the hottest.

Words to know

Exosphere
A very thin layer of gas around a world.
Extreme
Very hot or very cold, far from normal.

🧪 Try it yourself

Put one hand near a warm lamp and the other near an ice pack. Mercury has both in the same day, but much more extreme!
