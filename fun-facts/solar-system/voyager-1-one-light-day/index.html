🛸 How far has Voyager 1 traveled?

Voyager 1, launched in 1977, has reached about one light-day from Earth, more than 16 billion miles away.

The big idea

Voyager 1 has reached about one light-day from Earth, more than 16 billion miles.

How it works

🛸

Interstellar

It entered interstellar space in August 2012.

📡

One light-day

A radio signal takes about one day to reach it.

💿

Golden Record

It carries greetings in 55 languages, 35 sounds, 90 minutes of music and 115 images.

More about the solar system

  • Voyager 1 visited Jupiter in 1979 and Saturn in 1980.
  • The Pale Blue Dot photo of Earth was taken from about 40 AU away.
  • The Golden Record is a 30-centimeter gold-plated copper disc.

Words to know

Interstellar
Between the stars.
Light-day
The distance light travels in one day.

🧪 Try it yourself

Think of sending a text message that takes a whole day to arrive. That is how far Voyager 1 is from home!
