🔴 What will happen to the Sun one day?

The big idea

In about 6 billion years the Sun will become a red giant, and it will throw off about half its mass.

How it works

⛽

Fuel runs out

The core runs out of hydrogen fuel and contracts and heats up.

🔴

Red giant

The outer gas puffs up and the Sun grows huge.

🪐

Planets move

As the Sun loses mass, outer planets' orbits expand.

Quick stats

6 billionyears until red giant, about
50%of its mass thrown off, about
60xNeptune's future distance

More about the solar system

  • Mercury and Venus will likely be swallowed.
  • Earth's fate is less certain, but it will not be habitable.
  • Some very cold planets may thaw because they will get much more heat.

Words to know

Red giant
A big, puffed-up star near the end of its life.
Orbit
The path a planet takes around a star.

🧪 Try it yourself

Inflate a balloon a little, then more. Our Sun will puff up like that billions of years from now, but we have a very long time!
