🔋 How does a battery work?

Chemicals inside a battery react and push electrons around a circuit.

The big idea

A battery uses a chemical reaction to push electrons around a circuit.

How it works

🧪

Chemistry

Chemicals inside react when the battery is connected.

🔄

Circuit

Electrons leave the negative end and return to the positive end.

🔋

Flat

When most of the zinc has reacted, the cell is flat.

Quick stats

1.5 Vper alkaline cell, about
4cells make about 6 volts
Zincgives up electrons
Lithium-ionrechargeable type

More about nature

  • Alkaline cells usually cannot be recharged.
  • Rechargeable batteries like lithium-ion use reversible reactions.
  • Used batteries should be recycled.

Words to know

Volt
A unit for how hard a battery pushes electricity.
Terminal
The end of a battery where wires connect.

🧪 Try it yourself

Ask a grown-up to show you a flashlight. Which way are the batteries facing? What happens if one is turned around?
