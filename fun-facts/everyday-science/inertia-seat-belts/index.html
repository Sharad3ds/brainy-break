🚗 Why do you need a seat belt?

Things keep doing what they are doing unless a force changes it, and that idea is called inertia.

The big idea

Things keep their motion unless a force changes it. That is inertia.

How it works

📜

First law

An object at rest stays at rest, and a moving object keeps its speed and direction, unless a force acts.

🚗

Car stops

When a car stops, your body tends to keep moving.

🪢

Seat belt

The belt gives the force that stops your body safely.

More about nature

  • Inertia is also called the law of inertia.
  • Without a seat belt, nothing stops your forward motion in a sudden stop.
  • Every object resists changes in its motion.

Words to know

Inertia
Resisting a change in how you are moving.
Force
A push or a pull.

🧪 Try it yourself

Put a toy on a skateboard or toy car, roll it into a wall and see what the toy does. Always wear a real seat belt!
