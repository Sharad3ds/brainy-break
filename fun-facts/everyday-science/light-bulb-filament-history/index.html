💡 How does a light bulb make light?

In an incandescent bulb, electricity heats a thin wire called a filament until it glows.

The big idea

An incandescent bulb glows because electricity heats a thin filament.

How it works

🧵

Filament

Electric current heats the filament so it makes light.

🪫

Tungsten

Tungsten filaments, introduced in 1904, lasted longer than carbon.

👩‍🔬

Team effort

Many inventors contributed, not just one.

More about nature

  • By the 1950s, incandescent bulbs turned only about 10 percent of their energy into light.
  • In 1913 Irving Langmuir filled bulbs with an inert gas like nitrogen.
  • Earlier work on electric light goes back to 1835.

Words to know

Filament
The thin wire inside a bulb that glows.
Incandescent
Glowing because it is hot.
