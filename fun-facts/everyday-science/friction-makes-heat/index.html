🔥 Why do your hands get warm when you rub them?

Friction is a force that resists sliding, and it makes heat.

The big idea

Friction resists sliding and makes heat.

How it works

✋

Resists motion

Friction opposes one surface sliding or rolling over another.

🔥

Heat

Rubbing surfaces together makes them warmer.

🛢️

Oil helps

Lubricants like oil reduce friction between moving parts.

Quick stats

2kinds: static and kinetic
Staticholds resting things still
Kineticresists moving things
Oilreduces friction

More about nature

  • Brakes use friction against wheels to slow bikes and cars.
  • A bicycle chain and axle resist motion because of friction.
  • Heat from friction can damage machine parts.

Words to know

Friction
A force that resists sliding or rolling.
Lubricant
Something slippery, like oil, that reduces friction.

🧪 Try it yourself

Rub your hands together quickly for ten seconds. Feel the warmth? Now try with a little hand lotion. What changes?
