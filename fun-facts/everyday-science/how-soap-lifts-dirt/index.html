🧼 How does soap clean?

A soap molecule has one end that loves water and one end that loves grease, so it lifts dirt away.

The big idea

Soap has one end that likes water and one that likes grease, so it lifts dirt away.

How it works

💧

Two ends

One end likes water, the other likes grease and oil.

🦠

Germs too

Soap can bind to the fatty outer layers of some bacteria and viruses.

🫧

Micelles

Tiny clusters hold the loosened grime so it cannot reattach.

Quick stats

2ends on a soap molecule
Micelletiny cluster of soap
Waterrinses it away
Soapdoes a better job than water alone

More about nature

  • Water and rubbing remove some dirt, but soap does better.
  • Soap can break apart some germs by attacking their fatty layers.
  • Scrub for 20 seconds when washing hands.

Words to know

Micelle
A tiny ball of soap molecules around dirt.
Grease
Oily stuff that water alone cannot wash away.

🧪 Try it yourself

Put a drop of cooking oil on a plate. Try washing it with water only, then with soap. What is different?
