🌇 Why are sunsets red?

A low Sun sends light through more air, which scatters away most of the blue.

The big idea

At sunset light crosses more air, so reds and yellows reach your eyes.

How it works

🌇

Low Sun

Light passes through much more atmosphere.

🔵

Blue lost

Most blue and violet are scattered away.

🟠

Warm colors

Reds and yellows remain.

More about nature

  • Larger particles can scatter more reds and yellows.
  • Sometimes the whole western sky glows.
  • Never look straight at the Sun.
