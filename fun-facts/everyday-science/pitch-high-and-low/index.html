🎵 Why are some sounds high and some low?

Pitch relates to frequency: faster vibrations sound higher.

The big idea

High pitch means high frequency.

How it works

🎵

Pitch

How high or low a sound is.

⏱️

Frequency

Short wavelength means high frequency and high pitch.

🔉

Volume

How loud or soft, measured by amplitude.

Quick stats

Highpitch, high frequency
Lowpitch, low frequency
Shortwavelength, high pitch
Amplitudesets loudness

More about nature

  • Pitch and volume are two separate things.
  • Rubber bands and tuning forks can show different pitches.
  • Shorter vibrating things often sound higher.

Words to know

Pitch
How high or low a sound is.
Amplitude
The size of a vibration, linked to loudness.

🧪 Try it yourself
