🌑 How do shadows form?

A shadow is a dark shape left where an object blocks light, which travels in straight lines.

The big idea

A shadow is the dark shape where an object blocks light.

How it works

➡️

Straight

Light moves in straight paths.

🧱

Opaque

Opaque things block light and make shadows.

📏

Distance

Shadow size depends on distance from the light.

Quick stats

3kinds: opaque, translucent, transparent
Straightlight paths
Darkshadow shape
Distancechanges the size

More about nature

  • Transparent materials let most light through.
  • Translucent materials let some light through.
  • The outline is clear because light goes straight.

Words to know

Opaque
Blocks all light.
Translucent
Lets some light through.

🧪 Try it yourself

Use a flashlight and a toy. Move the toy closer to and farther from the light. How does the shadow change?
