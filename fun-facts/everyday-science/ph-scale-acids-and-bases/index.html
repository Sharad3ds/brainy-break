🍋 What does pH tell us?

The pH scale shows how acidic or basic a liquid is: 7 is neutral, lemon juice is around 2 and baking soda is around 9.

The big idea

pH 7 is neutral. Lower is acidic, higher is basic.

How it works

🍋

Acids

Lemon juice and vinegar are around pH 2.

⚖️

Neutral

Pure water is pH 7.

🧪

Bases

Baking soda is around 9, and bleach is around 13.

Quick stats

0-14most pH values
7neutral
2lemon juice, about
10xchange for each whole step

More about nature

  • Each whole-number step is a tenfold change in hydrogen ion concentration.
  • Test paper, indicator sticks or a pH meter measure pH.
  • Electronic meters are the most precise.

Words to know

Acid
A substance with a low pH, like lemon juice.
Base
A substance with a high pH, like baking soda.

🧪 Try it yourself

With a grown-up, use pH test strips on safe things like lemon juice, water and a baking soda solution. Do not taste them!
