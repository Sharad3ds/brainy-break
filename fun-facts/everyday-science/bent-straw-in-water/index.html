🥤 Why does a straw look bent in water?

Light changes direction when it passes from water into air; this is called refraction.

The big idea

Refraction bends light, so a straw in water looks broken.

How it works

🔀

Bending

Light changes direction between air and water.

🧠

Brain

Your brain cannot correct for the shift.

🔍

Magnified

Water makes the underwater part look larger.

More about nature

  • The straw is not really broken.
  • Water acts like a magnifying lens.
  • Light bends passing into denser water.

Words to know

Refraction
Light bending as it passes between materials.
Lens
Curved clear material that bends light.

🧪 Try it yourself

Put a straw in a glass of water. Look from the side. Then lift it out. Is the straw really broken?
