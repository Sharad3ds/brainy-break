⚡ Why do we see lightning before we hear thunder?

Light travels about 300,000,000 meters per second; sound only about 340.

The big idea

Light is far faster than sound, so you see lightning first.

How it works

💡

Light

About 300,000,000 meters per second.

🔊

Sound

About 340 meters per second.

🔢

Counting

Seconds divided by 3 gives roughly kilometers.

Quick stats

More about nature

  • A 9 second delay means about 3 kilometers.
  • Flash and thunder together means very close.
  • Go indoors during thunderstorms.

Words to know

Thunder
The sound made by lightning.
Delay
The gap in time.

🧪 Try it yourself

In a storm, safely indoors, count seconds from flash to thunder. Divide by 3. How far was it?
