🧪 Why do baking soda and vinegar fizz?

They react to make carbon dioxide gas, which makes the bubbles.

The big idea

Baking soda and vinegar react to make carbon dioxide gas, the fizz.

How it works

🧂

Two chemicals

Sodium bicarbonate meets acetic acid.

🧪

Reaction

They make sodium acetate, water and carbon dioxide.

🎈

Gas fills

The gas can inflate a balloon.

Quick stats

3things made: salt-like sodium acetate, water, gas
CO2the gas

More about nature

  • Gases are invisible but take up space.
  • Acetic acid is the acid in vinegar.
  • Always do experiments with a grown-up and wear eye protection.

Words to know

Reaction
When substances change into new ones.
Carbon dioxide
A gas with no color.

🧪 Try it yourself

With a grown-up, pour a little vinegar in a bottle and add baking soda to a balloon on top. Watch it inflate!
