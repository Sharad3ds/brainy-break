🚢 Why do giant ships float?

A floating ship pushes aside water that weighs as much as the ship itself.

The big idea

A ship floats when the water it moves aside weighs as much as the ship.

How it works

⬆️

Buoyant force

A fluid pushes up on an object with a force equal to the weight of fluid it displaces.

📦

Loading

More cargo makes the ship sink deeper and displace more water.

🧱

Density

Things less dense than water float. Big, hollow shapes can be low in density.

Quick stats

More about nature

  • A fully submerged object moves aside water equal to its own volume.
  • A ship needs a big underwater hull to move enough water.
  • Specific gravity compares a thing's density with water.

Words to know

Buoyancy
The upward push of water on an object.
Density
How much mass is packed into a space.
