🔥 What does fire need to burn?

Fire needs fuel, heat and oxygen. Take away any one and it goes out.

The big idea

Fire needs fuel, heat and oxygen. Remove one and it goes out.

How it works

🪵

Fuel

Something that can burn, like wood.

🌡️

Heat

Enough heat to start and keep it burning.

💨

Oxygen

Air supplies the oxygen.

Quick stats

More about nature

  • Firefighters clear fuel with firelines.
  • Rolling on the ground smothers flames on a person.
  • Never play with matches or lighters.

Words to know

Fuel
Anything that can burn.
Smother
To cover so air cannot reach.
