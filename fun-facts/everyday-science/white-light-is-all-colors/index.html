🔺 Is white light really many colors?

Sunlight looks white but contains all the colors of the rainbow.

The big idea

Sunlight looks white but is a mix of all the colors.

How it works

⚪

White

Sunlight looks white.

🌈

All colors

It contains all the visible colors.

💧

Bending

Bending light can separate the colors.

More about nature

  • Droplets bend and separate sunlight into colors.
  • ROY G BIV helps you remember the order.
  • A double rainbow shows colors in reverse order.

Words to know

Prism
A clear shape that splits light into colors.
Visible
Possible to see.

🧪 Try it yourself

Hold a clear glass of water in a sunny window. Look for colors on the wall.
