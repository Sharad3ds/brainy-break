⚛️ How small is an atom?

About 50 million atoms in a row would measure only 1 centimeter, and most of an atom is empty space.

The big idea

About 50 million atoms in a row would measure only 1 centimeter.

How it works

⚛️

Three particles

Protons, neutrons and electrons make an atom.

🎯

Nucleus

Protons and neutrons sit at the center.

🕳️

Empty space

Most of an atom is empty space.

Quick stats

50 millionatoms per centimeter, about
3kinds of particles
99.9%of mass in the nucleus
1/100,000of the volume, about

More about nature

  • Electrons move in a cloud around the nucleus.
  • Protons are positive, neutrons have no charge and electrons are negative.
  • Hydrogen has one proton and helium has two.

Words to know

Nucleus
The tiny center of an atom.
Atom
The smallest piece of an element.

🧪 Try it yourself

Fold a sheet of paper in half again and again. You will soon find it impossible, but atoms are far smaller than any fold!
