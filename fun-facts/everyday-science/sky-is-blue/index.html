🔵 Why is the sky blue?

Short blue waves scatter off gas molecules in every direction.

The big idea

Blue light scatters in every direction, so the sky looks blue.

How it works

🔵

Scattering

Short waves bounce off air molecules in every direction.

👁️

Our eyes

We are less sensitive to violet than blue.

🌫️

Paler horizon

Light scatters many times near the horizon.

Quick stats

More about nature

  • Gas molecules in the atmosphere cause the scattering.
  • Some violet is absorbed high in the atmosphere.
  • Never look straight at the Sun.

Words to know
