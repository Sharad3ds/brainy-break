⚖️ How does a lever make lifting easier?

A lever with a longer arm on one side can lift a 100 pound weight with only 50 pounds of force.

The big idea

A lever lets 50 pounds of force lift a 100 pound weight, if you move the arm twice as far.

How it works

⚖️

Fulcrum

A lever is a beam that pivots on a fulcrum.

📏

Longer arm

A longer arm on your side means less force is needed.

🔁

Trade

You pay with more distance.

More about nature

  • The six simple machines are the lever, wheel and axle, inclined plane, pulley, screw and wedge.
  • A wheel and axle reduces friction and multiplies turning force.
  • Simple machines trade force for distance, change a force's direction, or reduce friction.

Words to know

Fulcrum
The pivot point of a lever.
Simple machine
A basic tool that makes work easier.
