🪶 Do heavy things fall faster?

On the Moon, an astronaut dropped a hammer and a feather and they landed at the same time.

🎉 Great job!

The big idea

Without air, a hammer and a feather fall at the same rate.

How it works

🌬️

Air matters

On Earth, air resistance slows a feather much more than a hammer.

🌙

No air

The Moon has essentially no air to slow things down.

🧪

Same rate

Objects released together fall together, whatever their mass.

Quick stats

1.32 kghammer
0.03 kgfeather
1.6 mdrop height, about
0air to slow them, essentially

More about nature

  • Galileo had reached this idea centuries earlier.
  • Commander David Scott did the drop for the TV cameras.
  • It happened at the end of the last Apollo 15 moonwalk.

Words to know

Air resistance
Air pushing against something that moves through it.
Mass
How much stuff an object is made of.
