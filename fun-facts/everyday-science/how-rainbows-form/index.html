🌈 How do rainbows form?

Sunlight bends and splits into colors as it passes through water droplets.

The big idea

A rainbow is sunlight splitting into colors inside water droplets.

How it works

☀️

Sunlight

Sunlight looks white but contains all visible colors.

💧

Droplets

Light bends and separates as it passes through them.

🌈

Seven colors

Red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet.

More about nature

  • A garden hose on a sunny day can make a small rainbow.
  • A faint second rainbow can appear above, with colors reversed.
  • You need sunshine and water droplets together.

Words to know

Spectrum
The range of colors in light.
Droplet
A tiny drop of water.

🧪 Try it yourself

On a sunny day, with the Sun behind you, spray a garden hose mist in front of you. Can you see colors?
