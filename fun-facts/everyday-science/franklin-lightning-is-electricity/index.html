🪁 Is lightning electricity?

In 1752 Benjamin Franklin showed in a famous kite experiment that lightning is electrical.

The big idea

Franklin's 1752 kite experiment showed that lightning is electrical.

How it works

🪁

Kite

He flew a kite with a pointed wire during a storm.

🔑

Key

A metal key on the string gave him a small spark.

⚡

The point

It showed lightning and electricity are the same kind of thing.

More about nature

  • Franklin did not discover electricity. It had been studied for centuries.
  • He also filled a Leyden jar, a device that stores electricity.
  • Experiments with lightning are very dangerous.

Words to know

Lightning rod
A metal rod that safely leads lightning to the ground.
Leyden jar
An early device for storing electricity.

🧪 Try it yourself

Do not try this one! Instead, rub a balloon on your hair and see small sparks of static in a dark room.
