The big idea

Energy is never created or destroyed. It only changes form.

How it works

♻️

Conserved

The total amount of energy stays the same.

🚗

Engines

A gasoline engine turns chemical energy into mechanical energy.

🔥

Waste heat

Some energy always ends up as heat that is hard to use.

More about nature

  • Efficiency means how much energy becomes useful output.
  • No energy conversion is perfect.
  • The human body gives off most leftover energy as heat.

Words to know

Conservation
Staying the same overall.
Efficiency
How much energy becomes useful work.

🧪 Try it yourself

Rub your hands and feel heat. Where did the motion energy go? It became heat!
