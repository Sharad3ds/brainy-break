🟤 What is rust?

Rust forms when iron reacts with oxygen and moisture, making reddish-brown iron oxide.

The big idea

Iron rusts when it meets oxygen and moisture.

How it works

🟤

Iron oxide

Rust is a reddish-brown iron oxide.

💧

Water

Moisture is essential for rusting.

🎨

Protection

Paint, coatings and galvanizing keep oxygen and moisture away.

Quick stats

3needed: iron, oxygen, moisture
Saltspeeds rusting
Paintprevents rust
Reddish-browncolor of rust

More about nature

  • Rust can sometimes shield metal underneath.
  • If the rust layer is damaged, corrosion starts again.
  • Galvanizing means coating iron with zinc.

Words to know

Rust
The reddish-brown coating on iron.
Oxidation
A reaction with oxygen.

🧪 Try it yourself

Put two iron nails outdoors, one dry and one in a wet cup. Check them after a few days. Which rusts?
