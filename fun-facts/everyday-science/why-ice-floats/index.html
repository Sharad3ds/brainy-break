🧊 Why does ice float?

Ice is about 9 percent less dense than liquid water, so it floats.

The big idea

Ice is about 9 percent less dense than water, so it floats.

How it works

⚖️

Density

Less dense things float on more dense liquids.

⭐

Odd water

Most solids are denser than their liquid, but ice is not.

🔗

Hydrogen bonds

Hydrogen bonds hold water molecules apart as it freezes.

More about nature

  • Lakes and rivers freeze from the top down.
  • Fish can survive under the ice.
  • Water is an unusual substance in this way.
