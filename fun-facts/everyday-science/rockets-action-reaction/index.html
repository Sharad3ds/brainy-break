🚀 How do rockets move?

For every action there is an equal and opposite reaction, which is how rockets and balloons fly.

The big idea

For every action there is an equal and opposite reaction. That is how rockets fly.

How it works

💨

Action

Gas rushes out of the nozzle in one direction.

🚀

Reaction

The rocket moves forward in the opposite direction.

⚖️

Unbalanced

Opening the nozzle leaves an unbalanced push on the far side.

More about nature

  • This is called the essence of jet and rocket propulsion.
  • A balloon let go flies around the room the same way.
  • Momentum going one way equals momentum the other way.

Words to know

Thrust
The push that moves a rocket forward.
Nozzle
The opening that gas flows out of.

🧪 Try it yourself

Blow up a balloon and let it go outside. Which way does the air go? Which way does the balloon go?
