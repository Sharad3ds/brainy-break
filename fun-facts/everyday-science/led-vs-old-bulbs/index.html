💡 Why do LED bulbs save energy?

LED bulbs make roughly five times more light per watt than old style bulbs.

The big idea

LED bulbs make about 5.7 times more light per watt than old style bulbs, on average.

How it works

📏

Efficacy

Luminous efficacy is how much light a bulb makes per watt.

💡

Old bulbs

A 60 to 100 watt incandescent gives about 15 lumens per watt.

🌿

LEDs

LED replacement bulbs range from 70 to 120 lumens per watt.

Quick stats

15lumens per watt, old bulb
73lumens per watt, CFL
85lumens per watt, LED average
5.7xmore light per watt, LED vs old

More about nature

  • CFLs use about 75 percent less energy than incandescents.
  • CFLs last about 10 times longer.
  • The figures come from the US Department of Energy.

Words to know

Watt
A unit of power.
Lumen
A unit of how much light there is.

🧪 Try it yourself
