🔊 What is sound?

Sound comes from vibrating objects, and you can feel and even see the vibrations.

The big idea

Sound comes from vibrating objects.

How it works

📳

Vibrate

Vibrating objects make sound.

🔁

Changes

Changes in vibration change the sound.

👀

See it

Sound energy can be seen as well as heard.

Quick stats

More about nature

  • Tuning forks, spatulas and rubber bands show vibrations.
  • A boom box lets you feel vibrations.
  • Quiet things can still vibrate, too.

Words to know

Vibration
A quick back and forth movement.
Tuning fork
A metal fork that vibrates to make a note.

🧪 Try it yourself

Stretch a rubber band between your fingers and pluck it gently. Watch and listen.
