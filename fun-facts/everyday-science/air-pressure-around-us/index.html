🎈 Does air push on us?

Air pressure at sea level is about 14.7 pounds on every square inch, and it drops as you go up.

The big idea

Air pressure at sea level is about 14.7 pounds per square inch, and it falls as you go up.

How it works

💥

Collisions

Air molecules collide with every surface.

🔢

Adds up

Tiny collisions add up to a pressure we can measure.

🏔️

Altitude

Pressure drops with height because there are fewer molecules.

More about nature

  • Standard sea-level pressure is 29.92 inches of mercury.
  • Meteorologists measure pressure in millibars.
  • Air pressure is measured in millibars or inches of mercury.

Words to know

Air pressure
The push of air molecules on a surface.
Altitude
How high you are above sea level.

🧪 Try it yourself

Blow up a balloon and tie it. Squeeze it gently. Feel the air pushing back?
