🧭 Why does a compass point north?

Earth acts like a giant magnet, and its magnetic field pulls compass needles toward the north.

The big idea

Earth acts like a giant magnet, so compass needles point north.

How it works

🌍

Giant magnet

Earth's magnetic field comes from its core.

🧭

Compass

The field pulls a compass needle toward the north.

🛡️

Shield

The magnetosphere pushes back against the solar wind.

More about nature

  • The field emerges near the South Pole and re-enters near the North Pole.
  • The magnetosphere stretches far into space.
  • Without it the solar wind could wear away the atmosphere.

Words to know

Compass
A tool with a magnetic needle that shows direction.
Solar wind
A stream of particles from the Sun.

🧪 Try it yourself

With a grown-up, hold a compass far from magnets. Slowly turn around. Which way does the needle point?
