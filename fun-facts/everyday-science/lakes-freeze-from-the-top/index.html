🐟 Why do fish survive under frozen lakes?

Ice floats, so lakes and rivers freeze from the top down.

The big idea

Ice floats, so lakes freeze from the top down and fish can survive below.

How it works

🧊

Floats

Ice is less dense than water, so it stays on top.

⬇️

Top down

Lakes and rivers freeze from the top.

🐟

Fish safe

Liquid water below lets fish survive.

More about nature

  • Ice acts like a floating lid.
  • Most solids sink in their liquids, but ice floats.
  • Hydrogen bonds make ice less dense.

Words to know

Frozen
Turned from liquid into solid ice.
Dense
Packed tightly.
