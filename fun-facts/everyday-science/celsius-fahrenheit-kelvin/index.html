🔢 What are Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin?

Water freezes at 0 C or 32 F and boils at 100 C or 212 F; Kelvin starts at absolute zero.

The big idea

Water freezes at 0°C or 32°F, and boils at 100°C or 212°F.

How it works

🌍

Celsius

Most scientists and most countries use it.

🇺🇸

Fahrenheit

Used in the United States, Burma and Liberia.

🧊

Kelvin

Zero is absolute zero.

Quick stats

0 Cwater freezes
100 Cwater boils
32 Fwater freezes
212 Fwater boils

More about nature

  • Kelvin is used when very precise readings are needed.
  • Absolute zero is the total absence of heat energy.
  • Most scientists use Celsius.

Words to know

Celsius
A scale where water freezes at 0.
Absolute zero
The lowest possible temperature.
