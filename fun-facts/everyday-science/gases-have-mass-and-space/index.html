The big idea

Gases have mass and take up space, even though you cannot see them.

How it works

⚛️

Particles

Solids, liquids and gases are all made of particles.

🎈

Space

A gas can inflate a balloon, so it takes up space.

⚖️

Mass

Particles give matter mass.

More about nature

  • Experiments can show that gases have mass.
  • Mass is how much material an object contains.
  • Air is a mixture of gases.

Words to know

Mass
How much material something contains.
Gas
A state of matter that fills its container.
