🧬 Who named cells?

In 1665 Robert Hooke looked at cork and called its boxlike compartments cells.

The big idea

Robert Hooke named cells in 1665.

How it works

Cork

🔬

More about nature

  • He described the pores as like a honeycomb.
  • What he saw were the walls of cork cells.
  • His book is called Micrographia.

Words to know

Cell
The tiny building block of living things.
Cork
Light bark from a tree.

🧪 Try it yourself

Peel a thin layer of onion skin. With a grown-up, view it with a magnifier. Can you see little boxes?
