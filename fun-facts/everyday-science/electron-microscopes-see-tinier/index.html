🧫 How do electron microscopes see tinier things?

Electron microscopes use a beam of electrons instead of light and can magnify over 50,000 times.

More about nature

  • The field-ion microscope can enlarge over one million times.
  • Ernst Ruska built on Louis de Broglie's 1924 idea.
  • The scanning tunneling microscope came in the late 20th century.

Words to know

Electron
A tiny particle in atoms.
Vacuum
A space with almost no air.
