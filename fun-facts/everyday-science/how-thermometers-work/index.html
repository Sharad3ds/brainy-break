🌡️ How does a thermometer work?

In a liquid thermometer, the liquid rises in a narrow tube as it warms and falls as it cools.

The big idea

In a liquid thermometer, the liquid rises as it heats.

How it works

📈

Liquid

It rises as it heats and falls as it cools.

💻

Thermistor

Its resistance changes with temperature.

🔭

Infrared

Reads radiation from a distance.

More about nature

  • Mercury, kerosene and ethanol are common liquids.
  • Maximum thermometers hold the highest reading, used for body temperature.
  • Cryometers measure very low temperatures and pyrometers very high ones.

Words to know

Thermometer
A tool that measures temperature.
Thermistor
A part whose resistance changes with temperature.
