🪞 How does a mirror work?

The angle light hits a mirror equals the angle it bounces off.

The big idea

Light bounces off a mirror at the same angle it arrived.

How it works

🪞

Smooth

A mirror is a smooth specular reflector.

📐

Equal angles

The angle of reflection equals the angle of incidence.

📄

Rough

Rough surfaces scatter light in many directions.

Quick stats

Equalthe two angles
1direction reflected by a mirror
Manydirections from paper
Samedistance behind the mirror

More about nature

  • Paper, clothes and walls reflect diffusely.
  • Angles are measured from the perpendicular to the surface.
  • Mirror image appears behind the mirror.

Words to know

Reflection
Light bouncing off a surface.
Incidence
The arriving light.

🧪 Try it yourself

Shine a flashlight at a mirror at different angles. Where does the light land?
