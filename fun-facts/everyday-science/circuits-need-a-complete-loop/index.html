🔌 Why do circuits need a loop?

Electricity only flows when a circuit is a complete loop, and metals conduct it best.

The big idea

Electricity flows only around a complete loop. Metals conduct it, plastics resist it.

How it works

⭕

Loop

A broken loop makes an open circuit with no current.

🔩

Conductors

Metals are generally very good conductors.

🧤

Insulators

Plastic, wood and rubber resist current.

Quick stats

More about nature

  • Graphite is a non-metal that can conduct, but weakly.
  • A switch opens and closes the loop.
  • Never experiment with wall outlets. Use batteries only.

Words to know

Circuit
A path that electricity can flow around.
Insulator
A material that resists electric current.

🧪 Try it yourself

With a grown-up, use a battery, bulb and wires. Test a paper clip, foil, a plastic spoon and a wooden stick in the loop.
