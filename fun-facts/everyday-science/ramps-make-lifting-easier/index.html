📐 Why do ramps make work easier?

A gentle ramp needs less force than lifting straight up, but the load travels farther.

The big idea

A gentle ramp cuts the force needed, but the load travels farther.

How it works

📐

Inclined plane

A ramp is a simple machine.

⬆️

Less force

A gentler slope needs less force to raise a load.

📏

More distance

The load must travel a longer way.

More about nature

  • A screw is essentially a ramp wrapped around a shaft.
  • A wedge is a moving ramp that splits or lifts things.
  • Doorstops and axes are wedges.

Words to know

Inclined plane
A slope used to raise or lower things.
Wedge
A moving slope that splits or holds things.

🧪 Try it yourself

Lift a toy car straight up to a table, then slide it up a board. Which feels easier? Which path is longer?
