⚡ Why does a balloon stick to the wall?

Rubbing moves tiny electrons from one thing to another, so opposite charges attract.

The big idea

Rubbing moves electrons, so things get charged and can stick or push apart.

How it works

⚛️

Charges

Atoms have positive protons and negative electrons.

👕

Rubbing

Rubbing certain materials moves electrons from one to the other.

🔌

Shock

Extra electrons can jump when you touch something, giving a small shock.

Quick stats

2kinds of charge: positive and negative
Oppositesattract
Samerepel
Electronsare the part that moves

More about nature

  • Shuffling shoes on a carpet can leave extra electrons on your body.
  • Touching a pet or doorknob lets the extra electrons jump off.
  • Rubbed balloons stick because opposite charges attract.

Words to know

Electron
A tiny negatively charged particle.
Static electricity
A build-up of charge on an object.

🧪 Try it yourself

Rub a balloon on your hair, then hold it near small bits of paper. What happens? Do it away from water.
