🎢 How do roller coasters use energy?

At the top of a hill a coaster has stored height energy, and it turns into speed energy on the way down.

The big idea

A roller coaster swaps height energy and speed energy all along the track.

How it works

⛰️

Potential

Height gives stored potential energy.

🏃

Kinetic

Moving things have kinetic energy.

⚖️

Conserved

Ignoring air resistance, the total mechanical energy stays constant.

Quick stats

353,000 Jtotal energy, example 500 kg car
Topmost potential energy
Bottommost kinetic energy
Constanttotal, ignoring air

More about nature

  • The cars slow down on later hills as speed turns back into height.
  • Mechanical energy means kinetic plus potential energy.
  • The example figure is for a 500 kilogram car.

Words to know

Potential energy
Stored energy, like from being high up.
Kinetic energy
The energy of moving things.

🧪 Try it yourself

Roll a toy car down a ramp. Make the ramp higher. Does the car go faster at the bottom?
