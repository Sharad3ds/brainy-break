The big idea

Mass stays the same, but weight changes with gravity.

How it works

🧱

Mass

The amount of matter, measured in kilograms.

⬇️

Weight

The pull of gravity, measured in newtons.

🌙

Moon

Gravity is roughly one-sixth as strong.

Quick stats

40 kgsame everywhere
88 lbon Earth, about
15 lbon the Moon, about
1/6gravity on the Moon, roughly

More about nature

  • In orbit, weight is essentially zero.
  • One kilogram of mass corresponds to about 2.2 pounds of weight on Earth.
  • Newtons are the standard unit of force.

Words to know

Mass
How much matter is in something.
Gravity
The pull between objects.

🧪 Try it yourself

Weigh yourself, then work out about how much you would weigh on the Moon, a bit under one-sixth.
