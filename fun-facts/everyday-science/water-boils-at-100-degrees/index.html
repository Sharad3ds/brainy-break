♨️ At what temperature does water boil?

Water boils at about 100 degrees Celsius, 212 Fahrenheit, at sea level, and at a lower temperature up high.

The big idea

Water boils at about 100°C (212°F) at sea level, and lower up high.

How it works

🌊

Sea level

Air pressure is strongest, so water boils at about 100 degrees Celsius.

🏔️

High up

Lower air pressure means water boils at a lower temperature.

🌡️

Both units

100 degrees Celsius equals 212 degrees Fahrenheit.

More about nature

  • At altitude, food can take longer to cook in boiling water.
  • Boiling means water turns to vapor all through the liquid.
  • Always ask a grown-up for help near hot water.

Words to know

Boiling point
The temperature where a liquid boils.
Altitude
Height above sea level.
