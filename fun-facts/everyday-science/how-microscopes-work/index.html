🔬 How does a microscope work?

A compound microscope magnifies twice: first the objective lens, then the eyepiece.

The big idea

A compound microscope magnifies twice: objective, then eyepiece.

How it works

1️⃣

Objective

Makes the first magnified image.

2️⃣

Eyepiece

Magnifies that image again.

🔍

Three lenses

A typical one has objectives of 10, 40 and 100.

Quick stats

2magnifying steps
3typical objectives
10xlowest typical objective
100xhighest typical objective

More about nature

  • Zacharias Janssen is credited with the compound microscope around 1590.
  • Quality depends more on resolving power than magnification.
  • Resolving power shows close lines as separate.

Words to know

Objective lens
The lens near the specimen.
Eyepiece
The lens you look through.
