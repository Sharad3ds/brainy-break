☀️ How do solar panels make electricity?

Solar cells, usually made of silicon, absorb sunlight and pass its energy to electrons, which flow as current.

The big idea

Solar cells absorb sunlight and push electrons into a flowing current.

How it works

🔷

Silicon

Silicon is abundant, though it must be refined.

➡️

One way

The cell is built so current flows in only one direction.

🔌

Inverter

An inverter converts direct current into alternating current for homes.

Quick stats

Siliconthe usual material
DCwhat the cells make
ACwhat homes use
Lightneeded to keep flowing

More about nature

  • Metal grid lines on the cells collect the current.
  • Solar panels turn radiant energy into electrical energy.
  • The circuit has to stay connected.

Words to know

Semiconductor
A material that can partly conduct electricity.
Inverter
A device that changes DC into AC.

Find a small solar-powered calculator or garden light. Cover its solar cell with your hand. What happens?
