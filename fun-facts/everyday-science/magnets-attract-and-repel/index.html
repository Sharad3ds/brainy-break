🧲 Why do magnets stick and push?

Every magnet has a north and a south pole. Opposite poles attract and matching poles repel.

The big idea

Opposite magnet poles attract. Matching poles repel.

How it works

🧲

Two poles

Every magnet has a north pole and a south pole.

🔩

Special metals

Magnets strongly attract iron, steel, nickel and cobalt.

👻

Magnetic field

The area a magnet affects is called its magnetic field.

Quick stats

2poles on every magnet
4metals pulled strongly: iron, steel, nickel, cobalt
N + Sattract each other
N + Npush apart

More about nature

  • Magnets can attract or repel other hard magnets.
  • The forces between the poles create the magnetic field.
  • A magnet pulls and pushes on things without touching them, through its field.

Words to know

Pole
One end of a magnet, north or south.
Magnetic field
The area around a magnet where it can push or pull.

🧪 Try it yourself

Try two fridge magnets with a grown-up. Flip one over. When do they stick and when do they push away?
