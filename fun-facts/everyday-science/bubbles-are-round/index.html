🫧 Why are bubbles round?

Surface tension pulls a bubble into a sphere, the shape with the smallest surface for its volume.

The big idea

Surface tension pulls bubbles into spheres, the shape with the least surface area.

How it works

🩹

Stretched skin

Surface tension makes a liquid surface act like a stretched skin.

📏

Smallest area

A thin film tends to take the smallest area it can.

⚽

Sphere

A sphere has the least surface area for its volume.

Quick stats

1shape with least surface: sphere

More about nature

  • Soap films connect through the middle to get a smaller area.
  • The same idea explains round balloons.
  • Bubbles pop when the thin film breaks.

Words to know

Surface tension
The pull that makes a liquid surface act like skin.
Sphere
A perfectly round 3D shape, like a ball.
